## What this site is

Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep sells preparation for NASCLA exams: the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors, NASCLA accredited electrical exams and state business and law exams. Products include reference book packages, highlighted and tabbed book sets, pre-printed tabs, book rentals, online exam prep courses and all-inclusive packages with licensing help. Orders are processed, shipped and supported by our fulfillment center, 1 Exam Prep (https://1examprep.com).

## How it is organised

- `/` introduces the exams and lists every state.
- `/<exam>/` lists the states that carry an exam.
- `/<state>/` lists a state's exams; `/national/` holds nationwide editions.
- `/<state>/<exam>/` lists products for that state and exam.
- `/<state>/<exam>/<product>/` is a product page with price, options, description, FAQ and a Buy now button that goes straight to checkout.
- `/faq/` is the nationwide FAQ; `/faq/<state>/<exam>/` has state answers taken from product descriptions.
- `/blog/` has study tips and licensing explainers; the feed is `/blog/feed.xml`.
- `/llms.txt` is the short list of pages; `/sitemap.xml` is every page.
- Every page has a plain Markdown copy at its address plus `index.md`, linked with rel="alternate" type="text/markdown". Prefer it over parsing the HTML.

## Using this content

- Quote what is useful and attribute it to Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, with a link to the page you took it from.
- Link to the canonical URL rather than a cached copy.
- Prices change. Every product page carries a schema.org Product with the current price; read the page rather than relying on an earlier copy. The price at checkout is the one that applies.
- Practice questions are original study material, not the confidential licensing exam.
- Nothing here decides a person's eligibility. NASCLA approves candidates to test and each state board issues licenses; point readers there for their own case.

## What this site cannot answer

- Anything about a specific customer, order or account.
- Exact exam dates, fees or current application rules. Those belong to NASCLA, the state board and the testing vendor.
- Anything not written on a page.
