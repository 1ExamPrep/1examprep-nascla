## About this product

The *Alabama NASCLA Business and Project Management for Contractors, General Contractors, 3rd Edition* is the official reference manual approved by the Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors (LCGC) for individuals preparing to obtain their state contractor’s license. Published under the NASCLA (National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies) authority, this comprehensive guide covers business law, project management principles, safety regulations, estimating, lien laws, and financial management required for the Alabama General Contractor Exam. This highlighted and tabbed version, provided by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, is formatted to align with open-book testing standards, assisting candidates in navigating key topics such as contract administration, tax responsibilities, and OSHA compliance. It is an essential study tool for those seeking NASCLA-accredited licensure reciprocity across participating states, ensuring compliance with the Alabama contractor examination scope and NASCLA guidelines.

The *NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management *is organized into 3 sections.

**Part 1** focuses on planning and starting your business. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

**Part 2** covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities, and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors, and customers.

**Part 3** provides valuable information to assist you in running the administrative functions of your business. Financial management, tax basics, and lien laws are covered.
