## About this product

The *Alabama NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Alabama, Residential, 4th Edition* is an official reference published for candidates preparing for the Alabama Home Builders Licensure Board (HBLB) examination. Distributed through Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, this NASCLA-accredited guide covers essential topics for the residential contractor license exam, including business organization, estimating, bidding, contract management, labor laws, lien laws, tax requirements, and project management standards relevant to Alabama. The 4th Edition aligns with NASCLA’s uniform contractor licensing examination standards and serves as a key reference for business and law portions of the residential contractor test. This guide is widely recognized across contractor schools and licensing courses for compliance with Alabama’s contractor certification requirements under NASCLA and the HBLB.

***Please note, both the 3rd and 4th Edition will be honored in the Prov, Inc. testing center. With both publications being allowed, returns of the 3rd Edition will not be accepted in exchange for the 4th Edition.***

This book is organized into three sections.

Part 1 focuses on planning and starting your business. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

Part 2 covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities, and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors, and customers.

Part 3 provides valuable information to assist you in running the administrative funtions of your business. Financial management, tax basics, and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and can cause you serious problems if you do not give them proper attention.
