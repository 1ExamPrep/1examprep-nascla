## About this product

The Alabama NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Alabama, Residential, 4th Edition; Tabs Bundle [Book + Tabs], offered by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, provides the complete reference manual and pre-printed tab set approved by the Alabama Home Builders Licensure Board and NASCLA for the state’s residential contractor licensing exam. This guide covers Alabama-specific laws, business organization, financial management, lien laws, project management, safety, and labor regulations relevant to residential builders and remodelers. Each copy includes a coordinated tab set designed to match PSI’s open-book exam structure, allowing quick access to critical content areas during testing. Developed by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA), this resource is the official business and law text required for Alabama Residential Contractor examination candidates seeking licensure compliance and certification under state construction licensing standards.

***Please note, both the 3rd and 4th Edition will be honored in the Prov, Inc. testing center. With both publications being allowed, returns of the 3rd Edition will not be accepted in exchange for the 4th Edition.***

In addition to receiving the NASCLA Business, and Project Management for Contractors, Residential Contractors, 4th Edition you will also receive our quick reference tabs.

 Our permanent quick reference tabs will help you navigate quickly through this book during your exam. These tabs are self-adhesive and must be added at least one-day prior for sufficient adhesive power.

 Please note that the Testing Facility Proctor is the deciding factor on whether or not tabs will be accepted and allowed into the exam. If the tabs do not adhere properly, they will not be accepted.

 The *NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management *is organized into 3 sections.

**Part 1** focuses on planning and starting your business. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

**Part 2** covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities, and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors, and customers.

**Part 3** provides valuable information to assist you in running the administrative functions of your business. Financial management, tax basics, and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and can cause you serious problems if you do not give them proper attention.
