## About this package

The Alabama NASCLA Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors Complete Book Set provides all approved reference materials required for the NASCLA-accredited commercial general building contractor exam in Alabama. The set includes codes and manuals covering building construction practices, structural design fundamentals, concrete and masonry standards, carpentry and framing, roofing systems, sitework, project management, safety requirements, estimating, and contractor business responsibilities. Aligned with One Exam Prep’s exam-preparation format, this collection supplies the technical criteria and regulatory guidance needed to meet Alabama’s licensing standards for commercial general building contractors.
