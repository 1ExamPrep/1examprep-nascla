Alabama Commercial GC questions

How many questions are on the NASCLA examination?

The examination presents 125 questions, including 115 scored questions and 10 unscored pretest questions. Candidates receive 330 minutes and must answer at least 81 scored questions correctly to pass.

Does Alabama accept the NASCLA examination?

Yes. Alabama accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors for applicable trade examination requirements. Separate state licensing requirements still apply.

How long does it take to prepare my book order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for The 1 Package book orders.

How long is course access available?

The package includes 6 months of access to the Alabama Business and Law course and the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor trade course.

Is the Alabama Business and Law examination still required?

Yes. Prime contractor applicants must complete the Alabama Business and Law examination even when using the NASCLA examination for the applicable trade requirement.

Does passing NASCLA automatically provide an Alabama license?

No. Applicants must complete the Alabama licensing process and satisfy all applicable classification, experience, business, financial, examination, and application requirements.
