## Exam Details

The **NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors** is a standardized construction trade examination accepted by participating state licensing agencies, including Alabama.

The examination is administered through PSI and evaluates professional knowledge across major commercial construction disciplines.

- **Exam Name:** NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors
- **Testing Provider:** PSI
- **Exam Format:** Open book
- **Scored Questions:** 115
- **Unscored Pretest Questions:** 10
- **Total Questions Presented:** 125
- **Time Allowed:** 330 minutes, or 5 hours and 30 minutes
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 81 correct answers out of 115 scored questions

The examination covers commercial construction management, procurement, site construction, concrete, masonry, structural metals, wood construction, thermal and moisture protection, building finishes, mechanical and plumbing systems, and electrical systems.

General Requirements and Procurement and Contracting Requirements account for a significant portion of the scored examination questions. These subjects include project administration, contracts, estimating, construction management, and professional business practices.

The examination also assesses knowledge of technical construction requirements involving structural concrete, masonry, steel systems, roofing, wood framing, building accessibility, and construction safety.

Effective preparation combines broad construction knowledge with familiarity with specialized reference materials. Students should understand which publications address specific construction subjects and practice locating technical information efficiently.

## Open Book Test

The NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors is an **open book test**. Candidates may use authorized reference materials during testing under PSI examination rules.

Although reference books are permitted, the examination requires preparation, technical understanding, and effective time management. Candidates must work through construction questions while identifying the appropriate references for information involving codes, calculations, construction methods, and project administration.

The 1 Package includes highlighted and tabbed rental books to support organized preparation and efficient reference navigation.

PSI permits authorized books containing highlighting, underlining, and indexing. Permanent tabs are allowed, while temporary tabs, additional papers, and prohibited handwritten markings are not permitted.

Students should practice using their references before examination day. Learn the organization of construction standards, indexes, technical tables, and building code chapters.

For building code questions, the examination uses the editions specified in the authorized reference list. The included International Building Code, 2024 is an accepted edition.

Developing familiarity with the reference collection can help candidates use their examination time more effectively and approach technical questions with greater confidence.

## State Requirements

The **Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors** regulates covered general contracting activities and applicable construction classifications within the state.

Alabama's general contractor licensing framework applies to covered construction projects with a total undertaking cost of $100,000 or more, including labor and materials, subject to applicable statutory exemptions.

Alabama accepts the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination for qualifying trade examination requirements. Applicants must also complete applicable Alabama Business and Law testing and satisfy licensing board requirements.
