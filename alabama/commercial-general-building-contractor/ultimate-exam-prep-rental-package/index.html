Exam Details

This Ultimate package supports preparation for two examinations associated with the Alabama commercial general contractor licensing process: the Alabama Business and Law examination and the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors.

The Alabama Business and Law examination addresses the responsibilities involved in operating a contracting business in Alabama. Preparation includes working with the Alabama contractor business-and-law reference and reviewing subjects related to licensing, business organization, financial management, contracts, estimating, bidding, project administration, employment practices, insurance, bonding, safety, and other contractor responsibilities.

The NASCLA examination addresses the technical trade portion of commercial general building contractor preparation. It covers a broad range of commercial construction knowledge and requires candidates to use references dealing with project management, jobsite safety, building codes, accessibility, site work, concrete, masonry, steel, carpentry, roofing, mechanical and electrical systems, and other major construction areas.

The NASCLA Accredited Examination Program allows passing candidates to transmit their examination results to participating licensing agencies through the NASCLA National Examination Database. An examination result does not automatically issue an Alabama contractor license. Applicants must still complete the Alabama licensing process and satisfy the requirements established by the Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors.

Alabama prime contractor applicants must complete the applicable Business and Law examination administered through PSI. Examination eligibility for Alabama licensing is determined through the state licensing process. Candidates should complete the required applications and follow the current scheduling instructions for each examination.

Licensing Steps

  1. Determine the appropriate Alabama contractor classification. The requested classification should match the type of commercial construction work the applicant intends to perform.
  2. Review the Alabama licensing requirements. Applicants should understand the application, examination, financial, experience, and business documentation required for the requested license.
  3. Select the qualifying party. The business must identify the individual who will qualify the company for the requested contractor classification.
  4. Complete the required application forms. The licensing application must be submitted with accurate business, ownership, qualifying-party, and supporting information.
  5. Provide the required financial documentation. Alabama applicants must satisfy the financial requirements that apply to the requested contractor license.
  6. Receive examination eligibility. Eligibility for the Alabama examination process is determined by the Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors.
  7. Pass the Alabama Business and Law examination. Prime contractor applicants must complete the state-required Business and Law examination administered through PSI.
  8. Pass the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination. The NASCLA examination may be used for the applicable commercial general building trade examination requirement.
  9. Transmit the NASCLA result when required. Candidates who pass may send their examination transcript to a participating licensing agency through the NASCLA National Examination Database.
  10. Complete the state licensing review. The Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors reviews the application, examination results, financial documentation, and other required materials before issuing a license.

State Requirements

The Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors regulates general contractor licensing in the state. Alabama law requires licensing for general contracting work when the total cost of the undertaking, including labor and materials, reaches the threshold established by state law.

The contractor license is issued through the state licensing process, not solely by passing an examination. Applicants must submit the appropriate forms and meet the requirements connected with the business entity, qualifying party, requested classification, financial responsibility, and examination process.

All Alabama prime contractor applicants must complete a Business and Law examination administered through PSI. Candidates using the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination must still satisfy Alabama’s Business and Law examination requirement and the remaining application requirements.

The Alabama license application must be placed on file according to the Board’s application schedule. The Board meets on a quarterly basis, and applications must be submitted in time for review. Incomplete applications or missing supporting documents may delay the licensing process.

Passing the NASCLA examination can satisfy the applicable technical examination component for participating agencies, but it does not replace Alabama’s application review. Candidates must follow Alabama’s policies and procedures before they may receive authority to operate as a licensed general contractor in the state.

Begin by separating the books into major subject groups, including business and law, project management, safety, codes, accessibility, site work, concrete, masonry, structural systems, carpentry, roofing, and mechanical and electrical systems.

Practice identifying the reference most likely to contain an answer before opening a book. After choosing the reference, use the index, table of contents, chapter headings, and permanent tabs to locate the applicable material. Timed practice is especially valuable for an open-book examination. Candidates must read each question carefully, recognize the construction subject, select the correct book, locate the information, and choose the best answer within the available testing period.
