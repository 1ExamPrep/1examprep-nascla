Alabama Journeyman questions

What are the published time and question details for the NASCLA Journeyman Electrician exam?

The exam is published as 100 questions with 300 minutes allowed and a minimum passing score of 70. The exam also includes 10 non-scored experimental questions that do not count toward the final score.

Can I tab or highlight my reference books?

Reference materials may be tabbed/indexed with permanent tabs and may be highlighted, underlined, and/or indexed, but they must be otherwise unmarked (not written in) and may not contain additional loose or attached papers. Temporary tabs are not allowed.

How many questions are on the examination?

The examination includes 100 scored questions and 10 additional unscored experimental questions. Candidates have 300 minutes, and the minimum passing score is 70 correct scored answers.

Can every rental book be used inside the examination room?

Only reference materials authorized under the NASCLA examination-room rules may be brought into testing. The rental package includes technical study materials that support preparation across multiple electrical subjects.

Which agency oversees Alabama journeyman electrician licensing?

The Alabama Board of Electrical Contractors oversees statewide journeyman electrician licensing.

Does passing the NASCLA examination automatically provide an Alabama electrician license?

No. Applicants must satisfy the applicable qualifications and licensing requirements of the Alabama Board of Electrical Contractors.
