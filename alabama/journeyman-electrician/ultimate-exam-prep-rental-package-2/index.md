## State Requirements

The **Alabama Board of Electrical Contractors** regulates statewide electrical contractor and journeyman electrician licensing in Alabama.

Applicants for the statewide journeyman electrician examination must generally demonstrate **8,000 hours of qualifying electrical experience** involving the installation of wiring, apparatus, or equipment for lighting, power, or controls.

Alabama permits qualifying electrical education or an approved apprenticeship program to substitute for a portion of the experience requirement. Under the applicable rules, up to 2,000 hours may be credited toward the required experience.

Applicants must submit the required documentation and complete the Board's application procedures before receiving approval for the licensing examination.

The NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program provides a standardized examination recognized by participating licensing authorities. Alabama's licensing application materials identify a NASCLA journeyman examination option.

Passing the examination does not automatically issue an Alabama electrician license. Applicants must also satisfy the Board's experience, documentation, and other applicable licensing requirements.
