## About this product

The *Arizona NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Arizona 7th Edition* is an official reference manual authorized by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors and published by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA). This comprehensive guide, used in conjunction with Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep materials, provides essential information for contractors preparing for the Arizona Business Management exam. It covers state-specific laws, licensing requirements, financial management, contract administration, project supervision, safety standards, and labor regulations applicable to Arizona contractors. The 7th Edition reflects updated statutes, administrative rules, and NASCLA recommendations to ensure compliance and readiness for examination and business operations within the state’s construction industry.

Arizona NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Arizona 7th Edition

The *NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management *is organized into 3 sections.

**Part 1** focuses on planning and starting your business. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

**Part 2** covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities, and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors, and customers.

**Part 3** provides valuable information to assist you in running the administrative functions of your business. Financial management, tax basics, and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and can cause you serious problems if you do not give them proper attention.
