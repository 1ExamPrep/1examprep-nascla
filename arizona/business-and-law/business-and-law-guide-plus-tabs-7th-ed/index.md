## About this product

The *Arizona NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Arizona 7th Edition* is the official reference published by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA) for use in preparing for the Arizona state contractor licensing examination administered by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors (ROC). Distributed by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, this guide and tab bundle provides comprehensive coverage of Arizona-specific contractor business practices, construction law, lien laws, financial management, insurance, project management, bidding procedures, safety standards, and labor regulations. It is an essential reference for candidates pursuing General Commercial, Residential, or Specialty Contractor licenses, aligning directly with NASCLA’s exam content outline and Arizona’s statutory requirements for construction professionals. The 7th Edition ensures compliance with current state laws and industry standards, making it a critical component of Arizona contractor exam preparation materials offered through 1 Exam Prep.

In addition to receiving the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Arizona 7th Edition you will also receive our quick reference tabs.

Our permanent quick reference tabs will help you navigate quickly through this book during your exam. These tabs are self-adhesive and must be added at least one day prior for sufficient adhesive power.

Please note that the Testing Facility Proctor is the deciding factor on whether or not tabs will be accepted and allowed into the exam. If the tabs do not adhere properly, they will not be accepted.

The *NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management *is organized into 3 sections.

**Part 1** focuses on planning and starting your business. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

**Part 2** covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities, and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors, and customers.

**Part 3** provides valuable information to assist you in running the administrative fucntions of your business. Financial management, tax basics, and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and can cause you serious problems if you do not give them proper attention.
