## Arizona Residential Electrical questions

### How long should I allow for an Ultimate book package order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for ultimate book package orders. This allows time for processing and fulfillment of the rental materials included in the package.

### What topics should I study for this exam?

Candidates should study NEC code navigation, OSHA safety, NFPA 70E electrical safety, NFPA 72 fire alarm code, electrical theory, residential electrical installation, calculations, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, product safety signs and labels, IRC residential code concepts, and business and project management topics.

### Why choose 1 Exam Prep for Arizona R-11 exam preparation?

Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep supports candidates with an Ultimate preparation package that includes listed rental books, 1 year of course access, Application Service, and study guidance designed around the NASCLA residential electrical exam option for the Arizona R-11 contractor licensing goal.

### What does the R-11 classification cover?

The R-11 Electrical classification is a residential contractor classification connected to electrical work performed within the residential license scope.

### Does passing the exam automatically give me the Arizona R-11 license?

No. Passing the required exam is part of the licensing process, but the applicant must still complete the Arizona Registrar of Contractors application process and satisfy the state requirements for the license classification.

### How many questions are on the NASCLA Residential Electrical Contractors exam?

The exam contains 100 scored questions and 10 non-scored experimental questions. The minimum passing score is 70, and candidates are allowed 270 minutes.
