State Requirements

The Arizona Registrar of Contractors issues contractor licenses by classification. The R-11 classification is tied to residential electrical contracting within the allowed Arizona license scope. Applicants preparing for this path should understand residential electrical systems, residential code requirements, safety practices, and contractor responsibilities connected to electrical work in residential settings.

Residential electrical contractor work can involve installation, alteration, and repair of wiring, electrical materials, and equipment used in residential electrical systems within the allowed scope. Work may include service equipment, feeders, branch circuits, conductors, cables, raceways, grounding and bonding, lighting, receptacles, appliances, overcurrent protection, smoke alarm or fire alarm-related coordination, and equipment connections.

Contractors should understand where their classification begins and ends. Work outside the residential electrical contractor classification may require another properly licensed contractor. Passing the NASCLA residential electrical contractor examination is not the same as receiving an Arizona contractor license. Applicants are responsible for meeting the full Arizona licensing requirements that apply to the classification, qualifying party, business entity, bond, application, and related state requirements.
