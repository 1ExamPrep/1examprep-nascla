About this product

The Arkansas NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Arkansas 8th Edition is an essential reference published under the authority of the Arkansas Contractors Licensing Board and used by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep for contractor exam preparation. This NASCLA guide provides detailed information on state-specific laws, financial management, project supervision, and business practices relevant to Arkansas general contractors, construction managers, and specialty contractors. The pre-printed tabs correspond to chapters on licensing requirements, lien laws, contract administration, tax obligations, and safety regulations, ensuring efficient navigation during the open-book licensing examination. Developed by NASCLA to align with national contractor competency standards, the guide supports candidates preparing for the Arkansas Contractor Business and Law Exam and complements related materials such as the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination. It remains a primary study aid for obtaining an Arkansas contractor license through recognized institutions like Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep and similar contractor licensing schools nationwide.

Frequently asked questions

Is the NASCLA exam open book?

Yes. You bring the approved reference books, so knowing where things are in the books matters as much as knowing the material.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Real exam questions are confidential. Practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.
