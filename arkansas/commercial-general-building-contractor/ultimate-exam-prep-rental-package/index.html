Exam Details

The NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors is a standardized trade examination accepted by participating contractor licensing agencies. The Arkansas Contractors Licensing Board is one of the participating state agencies that accepts the NASCLA commercial examination for applicable licensing purposes.

The NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination contains 115 scored questions. Candidates must answer at least 81 questions correctly to achieve the required passing score. Candidates receive 330 minutes, or 5 hours and 30 minutes, to complete the examination.

The examination covers commercial construction knowledge across numerous technical and management areas, including:

  • Planning and estimating
  • Financial and project management
  • Procurement and contracting
  • Construction safety
  • Site construction
  • Concrete construction
  • Masonry
  • Structural metals
  • Wood construction
  • Thermal and moisture protection
  • Doors, windows, and glazing
  • Finishes
  • Mechanical and plumbing systems
  • Electrical systems

The breadth of the examination means candidates should prepare across both practical construction knowledge and formal reference material. Contractors with extensive field experience may still encounter questions involving code provisions, technical standards, business practices, management concepts, safety regulations, and subjects outside their regular specialty.

Studying from the examination references helps candidates become familiar with the language and organization used in these technical publications. Combined with practice questions and timed navigation exercises, this can create a more organized preparation strategy for the NASCLA commercial exam.

Licensing Steps

The Arkansas Contractors Licensing Board regulates applicable commercial contractor licensing in Arkansas. Passing the NASCLA Accredited Examination is an important trade-qualification step, but it does not automatically issue an Arkansas contractor license.

  1. Determine the appropriate Arkansas contractor classification. Select the commercial classification that corresponds with the scope of construction work the business intends to perform.
  2. Prepare for the applicable trade examination. Arkansas accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors for applicable commercial building licensing purposes.
  3. Complete Arkansas Business and Law requirements. Arkansas requires commercial contractor applicants to satisfy the applicable state Business and Law examination requirement.
  4. Identify the qualifying party. The contractor application must identify the individual responsible for satisfying the required qualification and examination requirements.
  5. Prepare the Arkansas contractor application. Applicants must provide the business, classification, qualifying-party, financial, and supporting information required by the Arkansas Contractors Licensing Board.
  6. Submit the required documentation. The application and supporting materials must be submitted for Board review.
  7. Complete the licensing process. The Arkansas Contractors Licensing Board determines whether the applicant satisfies the requirements for the requested license and classification.

The Application Service included with this Ultimate package provides support with the contractor application process. This allows candidates to combine exam preparation with assistance navigating the licensing paperwork instead of treating each part of the process as a completely separate project.
