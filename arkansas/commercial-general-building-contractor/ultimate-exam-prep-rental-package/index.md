## Licensing Steps

The Arkansas Contractors Licensing Board regulates applicable commercial contractor licensing in Arkansas. Passing the NASCLA Accredited Examination is an important trade-qualification step, but it does not automatically issue an Arkansas contractor license.

1. **Determine the appropriate Arkansas contractor classification.** Select the commercial classification that corresponds with the scope of construction work the business intends to perform.
2. **Prepare for the applicable trade examination.** Arkansas accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors for applicable commercial building licensing purposes.
3. **Complete Arkansas Business and Law requirements.** Arkansas requires commercial contractor applicants to satisfy the applicable state Business and Law examination requirement.
4. **Identify the qualifying party.** The contractor application must identify the individual responsible for satisfying the required qualification and examination requirements.
5. **Prepare the Arkansas contractor application.** Applicants must provide the business, classification, qualifying-party, financial, and supporting information required by the Arkansas Contractors Licensing Board.
6. **Submit the required documentation.** The application and supporting materials must be submitted for Board review.
7. **Complete the licensing process.** The Arkansas Contractors Licensing Board determines whether the applicant satisfies the requirements for the requested license and classification.
