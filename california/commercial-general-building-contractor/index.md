## California Commercial GC questions

### How long does order preparation take?

Please allow an additional 15 business days for highlighted and tabbed book package orders.

### Does California accept the NASCLA examination?

California recognizes the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination for qualifying out-of-state applicants seeking a waiver of the B – General Building trade examination.

### Who may qualify for the California NASCLA waiver?

A qualifying applicant must have passed the NASCLA commercial building examination and held a comparable out-of-state general building contractor license in good standing for the previous five years.

### Does passing NASCLA automatically provide a California license?

No. Applicants must submit a California license application and satisfy all applicable experience, examination, fingerprinting, bonding, insurance, business, and license-issuance requirements.

### Why are highlighted and tabbed books helpful?

Highlighting identifies useful information, while permanent tabs help candidates reach important chapters and technical subjects more efficiently during study and permitted open-book testing.

### Should I study with the prepared books before test day?

Yes. Repeated practice helps candidates learn which publication applies to each subject and become familiar with the tabbing and highlighting system.
