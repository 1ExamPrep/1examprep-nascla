## Quick answers

### Does California accept the NASCLA exam?

California is not on NASCLA's list of states that accept the commercial building exam in place of the state trade exam. The prep listed here is for the California exams named below; check the California licensing board for which exams it requires.

### Which NASCLA exams can I prepare for in California?

### How do I register for the NASCLA exam?

Apply through NASCLA at ned.nascla.org. You must be pre-approved before you can schedule, and then you apply to the California board with your passing record.
