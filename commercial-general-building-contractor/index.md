# NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor Exam Prep

The NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors is one standardized exam that participating states accept in place of their own commercial trade exam. Pass it once and send the same score to every accepting state where you want to bid work.

## Quick answers

### Which states accept the NASCLA commercial exam?

Alabama, Arizona, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Louisiana, Mississippi, Nevada, New Mexico, North Carolina, Oregon, South Carolina, Tennessee, Utah, Virginia, West Virginia and the U.S. Virgin Islands accept the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors. Each board sets its own rules for using the score.

### What is on the NASCLA commercial exam?

115 scored questions in 330 minutes, open book, with 70 percent needed to pass. It covers commercial building regulations, best practices and terminology, answered from the approved reference books.

### How should I prepare for the NASCLA commercial exam?

Get the approved reference books, tab and highlight them, work an online course or study guide, then sit timed practice exams. Prep here runs from $95.00 to $7,495.00, from a single online course to The 1 Package with licensing and business setup.
