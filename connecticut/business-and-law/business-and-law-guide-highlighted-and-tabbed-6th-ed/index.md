## About this product

The Connecticut NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, CT 6th Edition; Highlighted & Tabbed, offered by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, is the officially approved reference manual for contractor licensing examinations administered by PSI under the Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection (DCP). Developed by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA), this guide provides comprehensive instruction on business management, financial principles, project supervision, safety compliance, and Connecticut-specific licensing regulations. The Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep edition is pre-highlighted and tabbed according to PSI’s official exam content outline, enabling quick reference during open-book testing. This manual serves as the foundational resource for candidates pursuing Building, Electrical, Mechanical, or related trade licenses in Connecticut, ensuring alignment with NASCLA’s standardized exam framework and compliance with the Connecticut construction industry’s business and legal requirements.

NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Connecticut Edition is organized into 3 sections.

Part 1 focuses on business planning and start up. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

Part 2 is centered on fundamentals that you will need to operate a successful construction business. This section will cover estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors and customers.

Part 3 gives you valuable information for running the administrative function of your business. Financial management, tax basics and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and can cause you serious problems if you do not give them the proper attention.
