Connecticut Business & Law questions

What is the Connecticut NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 6th Edition?

It is a state-specific business, law, and project management reference publication for contractors, published by the National Association of State Contractor Licensing Agencies.

Who publishes the Connecticut Contractor's Guide?

The National Association of State Contractor Licensing Agencies, commonly known as NASCLA, publishes the guide.

Is the Connecticut NASCLA 6th Edition used for contractor examination preparation?

Yes. The book is used as a reference for applicable Connecticut contractor business and law examinations.

Are applicable Connecticut contractor examinations open book?

Yes. Applicable PSI contractor examinations identify open-book testing and permit designated reference publications, including the Connecticut NASCLA guide where listed.

Which Connecticut agency regulates contractor-related registrations?

The Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection administers several contractor and construction-related registration and licensing programs.

Do all Connecticut contractors need the same license?

No. Connecticut has different registration and licensing requirements depending on the work performed, including home improvement, new home construction, and regulated trade activities.
