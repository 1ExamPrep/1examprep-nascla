Does Connecticut accept the NASCLA exam?

Connecticut is not on NASCLA's list for the commercial building exam. The prep here is for Connecticut's own exams named below; check the Connecticut licensing board for which exams it requires.

Which NASCLA exams can I prepare for in Connecticut?

Business and Law (6 products from $19.95). Each has its own page with the books, tabbed sets, rentals and online courses for that exam.

How do I schedule my Connecticut exam?

Through the Connecticut licensing board and its testing vendor, not through NASCLA's commercial exam portal. The guide below names the board and vendor and walks through the steps.
