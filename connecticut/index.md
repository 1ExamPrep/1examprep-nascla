### Does Connecticut accept the NASCLA exam?

Connecticut is not on NASCLA's list of states that accept the commercial building exam in place of the state trade exam. The prep listed here is for the Connecticut exams named below; check the Connecticut licensing board for which exams it requires.

### How do I register for the NASCLA exam?

Apply through NASCLA at ned.nascla.org. You must be pre-approved before you can schedule, and then you apply to the Connecticut board with your passing record.
