## How is the NASCLA commercial exam set up?

The NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors has 115 scored questions and a 330-minute time limit. That works out to about 2.9 minutes per question. A score of 70 percent passes. The exam is open book, so fast lookup matters more than memorizing. NASCLA says the exam is accepted in 16 states plus the U.S. Virgin Islands. Most of those states still require their own business and law exam. Read the current rules on the [NASCLA website](https://www.nascla.org/) before you apply.

## Contact questions

### How fast will someone reply to my message?

Most messages sent through the form get a reply within one business day. Calls to (877) 775-9400 are answered during business hours.

### Can you tell me which books my state requires?

Yes. Send your state and exam and an advisor will list the references named in your candidate bulletin, with the matching book package or tabbed set.

### Do you schedule the NASCLA exam for me?

No. You apply and schedule through NASCLA and your testing vendor. The 1 Package adds help with the state license application and business setup.

### Can I return books after I pass?

Purchased books are not returnable. Rental packages are sent back after your exam, and the product page lists the rental period and deposit terms.

### Is the NASCLA commercial exam open book?

Yes. It has 115 scored questions in 330 minutes, about 2.9 minutes each, and 70% is a passing score. Tabbed and highlighted books are allowed in most states.

### Can I order by phone instead of online?

Yes. Call (877) 775-9400 and an advisor can place the order with you. Every online Buy now button also goes to the same secure checkout.

### Does buying prep make me eligible for the exam?

No. Eligibility and licensing are decided by NASCLA and your state board. Buying prep materials does not grant approval or a license.
