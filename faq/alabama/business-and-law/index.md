## The exam

### Is this the Alabama General Contractors edition?

Yes. This is the Alabama General Contractors, 3rd Edition, published by NASCLA.

### Who administers Alabama general contractor licensing?

The Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors administers the state's general contractor licensing program.

### Is this the Alabama Residential Construction edition?

Yes. This is the Alabama Residential Construction, 4th Edition, published by NASCLA.

### Is the NASCLA Alabama Residential 4th Edition approved for the Business and Law Examination?

Yes. The guide is an approved reference for the Alabama Home Builder Business and Law Examination.

### Who administers Alabama residential home builder licensing?

The Alabama Home Builders Licensure Board administers residential home builder licensing in the state.

### Is the NASCLA Alabama General Contractors, 3rd Edition an approved examination reference?

Yes. The NASCLA Alabama General Contractors, 3rd Edition, is an approved reference for the Alabama General Contractor Business and Law Examination.

### Are highlighted and tabbed books accepted during testing?

Approved references may be highlighted and fitted with compliant permanent tabs. All reference materials remain subject to examination rules and the testing facility proctor's final approval.

### Are highlighted and tabbed books automatically accepted in the exam room?

No. Reference materials must comply with examination rules, and the testing facility proctor makes the final decision regarding acceptance. Tabs must adhere securely.

### Who administers the examination?

Prov, Inc. provides examination services for the Alabama Home Builders Licensure Board.
