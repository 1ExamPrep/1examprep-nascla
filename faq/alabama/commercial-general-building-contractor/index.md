## The exam

### How many questions are on the NASCLA examination?

The examination presents 125 questions, including 115 scored questions and 10 unscored pretest questions. Candidates receive 330 minutes and must answer at least 81 scored questions correctly to pass.

### How long does it take to prepare my book order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for The 1 Package book orders.

### Does passing NASCLA automatically provide an Alabama license?

No. Applicants must complete the Alabama licensing process and satisfy all applicable classification, experience, business, financial, examination, and application requirements.

### Should I study with the prepared books before the exam?

Yes. Regular practice helps candidates learn the location of important information and become familiar with the navigation system before test day.

### Can I add loose notes to the books?

References must comply with the current testing rules. Candidates should not add loose papers, removable notes, or unauthorized inserts and should review the current candidate bulletin before the examination.

### Are books supplied at the testing center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all permitted references to the testing location.

### Can I use a different edition?

Candidates should use the listed editions because page numbers, code provisions, technical requirements, and book organization may differ between editions.

### Are the reference books provided at the testing center?

No. The testing center does not provide the reference library. Candidates are responsible for bringing the approved books permitted under the current testing rules.

### Who administers the Alabama Business and Law examination?

PSI administers the Alabama examination program. Eligibility is determined through the Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors.

### Should I study directly from the reference books?

Yes. Candidates should combine online course review with regular practice using the listed references. Familiarity with the books is an important part of open-book exam preparation.

### Can I use different editions of the books?

Candidates should prepare with the editions shown on the current examination reference list because page numbers, code sections, technical requirements, and book organization can differ between editions.

## Licensing

### Is the Alabama Business and Law examination still required?

Yes. Prime contractor applicants must complete the Alabama Business and Law examination even when using the NASCLA examination for the applicable trade requirement.

## More questions

### Does Alabama accept the NASCLA examination?

Yes. Alabama accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors for applicable trade examination requirements. Separate state licensing requirements still apply.

### How long is course access available?

The package includes 6 months of access to the Alabama Business and Law course and the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor trade course.

### How long does order processing take?

Please allow an additional 15 business days for highlighted and tabbed rental-book package orders.

### How long does preparation take?

Please allow an additional 15 business days for highlighted and tabbed book package orders.

### Why are highlighting and tabs useful?

Highlighting identifies useful information, while permanent tabs help candidates reach important chapters and subjects more efficiently during study and permitted open-book testing.
