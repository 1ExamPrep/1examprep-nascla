The exam

What are the published time and question details for the NASCLA Journeyman Electrician exam?

The exam is published as 100 questions with 300 minutes allowed and a minimum passing score of 70. The exam also includes 10 non-scored experimental questions that do not count toward the final score.

Can I tab or highlight my reference books?

Reference materials may be tabbed/indexed with permanent tabs and may be highlighted, underlined, and/or indexed, but they must be otherwise unmarked (not written in) and may not contain additional loose or attached papers. Temporary tabs are not allowed.

How many questions are on the examination?

The examination includes 100 scored questions and 10 additional unscored experimental questions. Candidates have 300 minutes, and the minimum passing score is 70 correct scored answers.

Can every rental book be used inside the examination room?

Only reference materials authorized under the NASCLA examination-room rules may be brought into testing. The rental package includes technical study materials that support preparation across multiple electrical subjects.

Does passing the NASCLA examination automatically provide an Alabama electrician license?

No. Applicants must satisfy the applicable qualifications and licensing requirements of the Alabama Board of Electrical Contractors.

How many questions are on the NASCLA Journey-level Electrician Exam?

The examination contains 110 questions. Ten are experimental questions that are not counted toward the overall examination score.

How much time is allowed for the exam?

Candidates are allowed five hours to complete the NASCLA Journey-level Electrician examination.

Why are highlighted and tabbed books useful for an open-book electrical exam?

Highlighted and tabbed references can make important sections easier to identify and reduce unnecessary searching. Candidates should still practice extensively with the books so they understand where information is located and which reference applies to different question types.

How long does it take to prepare the highlighted and tabbed books?

Please allow 15 business days for your books to be tabbed and highlighted.

Which NEC edition is used for the exam?

The current NASCLA Journey-level Electrician reference list includes NFPA 70 - National Electrical Code 2023. The Alabama Board of Electrical Contractors also states that its current examinations reference the 2023 NEC.

What is Ugly's Electrical References used for?

Ugly's Electrical References provides frequently used electrical formulas, tables, calculations, and field information. It is useful for reviewing practical electrical information and quickly locating common reference data.

Should I study with all of the books before exam day?

Yes. Because the exam covers code, theory, safety, troubleshooting, equipment, fire alarm, and other electrical subjects, candidates benefit from becoming familiar with the organization and purpose of every authorized reference in the package.

Licensing

Which agency oversees Alabama journeyman electrician licensing?

The Alabama Board of Electrical Contractors oversees statewide journeyman electrician licensing.

How much electrical experience is required to apply for the Alabama Journeyman examination?

Alabama requires applicants for the statewide Journeyman Electrician examination to document at least 8,000 hours of qualifying electrical experience. Approved electrical education or apprenticeship training may be substituted for up to 2,000 hours under the Board's requirements.

How much experience is required for the Alabama Journeyman Electrician examination?

Applicants for the statewide Alabama Journeyman Electrician examination must document at least 8,000 hours of qualifying electrical experience. Approved electrical education or apprenticeship training may substitute for up to 2,000 hours of the total experience requirement.
