## The exam

### Are these books highlighted and tabbed?

No highlighting or tabbing is included in this standard Alabama NASCLA Master Electrical Contractor Exam Book Package. Candidates looking for prepared references should select the highlighted and tabbed package instead.

### How many questions are on the NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam?

The NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination contains 110 questions. Ten are experimental items, and candidates are allowed 4.5 hours for the examination.

### How many questions are on the Alabama Electrical Contractor Business and Law exam?

The Alabama Electrical Contractor Business and Law examination contains 40 questions and allows 2 hours.

### Why are highlighted and tabbed books useful for an open-book exam?

Highlighting can make important information easier to identify, while tabs help candidates navigate major sections of the reference books more efficiently. Candidates should still practice extensively with the books before exam day.

### Should I use the highlighted and tabbed books while studying?

Yes. Using the same references throughout your preparation helps you learn where subjects are located and how each book is organized. Repeated practice is especially useful for developing the navigation skills needed during an open-book, timed examination.

### Should I study with all of the books before the exam?

Yes. Because the examination is open book and covers multiple subject areas, regular practice with the complete reference library can help you become familiar with which book applies to different types of questions.

### How should I prepare for an open-book master electrical exam?

Combine technical study with repeated reference navigation. Learn the organization of the NEC and supporting books, practice identifying which reference applies to each question, and incorporate timed practice as the examination approaches.

### Which NEC edition is currently used for Alabama electrical examinations?

Alabama currently references the 2023 NFPA 70 National Electrical Code. The Alabama Board of Electrical Contractors has announced that testing will move to the 2026 NEC beginning January 1, 2027.

### Is OSHA material part of the reference library?

Yes. Code of Federal Regulations 29 CFR Part 1926 is included among the references provided for preparation and supports study of construction safety requirements.

### How should I use an online course to prepare for an open-book exam?

Use the course to build subject knowledge, identify weaker areas, and create a structured study routine. At the same time, work directly with the applicable books so you practice selecting the correct reference and finding information efficiently.

## Licensing

### Does Alabama require a Business and Law exam?

Yes. Candidates using the NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination route must satisfy the applicable Alabama Business and Law examination requirement before the Electrical Contractor license is issued.

### Does Alabama require a Business and Law exam with the NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam?

Yes. Candidates using the NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination path must also satisfy the applicable Alabama Electrical Contractor Business and Law examination requirement.

### How much experience is required for an Alabama Electrical Contractor license?

Applicants seeking licensure through examination must document a minimum of 8,000 hours of qualifying electrical experience.

### Can electrical education or apprenticeship count toward the experience requirement?

Yes. Qualifying education or approved apprenticeship training may substitute for 1,000 hours per qualifying year, up to a maximum of 2,000 hours toward the experience requirement.

### What should I focus on when preparing for the NASCLA Master Electrical Contractor exam?

Focus on the published examination categories, including general code requirements, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, electrical theory, equipment, safety, project design and management, and specialized electrical installations. Combine subject review with repeated reference-navigation practice.

### What subjects should I study for the NASCLA Master Electrical Contractor exam?

Major subjects include project design and management, safety, electrical theory and principles, general code requirements, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, general equipment use, special occupancies and equipment, special conditions, and communication systems.
