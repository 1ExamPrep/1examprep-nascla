Arizona Commercial General Building Contractor exam: frequently asked questions

11 answers about the Arizona commercial general building contractor exam and license, compiled from our product descriptions for this exam.

The exam

Are the books highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. The rental references are highlighted and tabbed to support organized study and efficient open-book navigation.

Does passing NASCLA automatically provide an Arizona license?

No. Applicants must complete the Arizona contractor licensing process and satisfy all applicable classification, experience, statutes and rules, background, bond, application, and business requirements.

Does passing the NASCLA exam automatically give me an Arizona contractor license?

No. Passing the exam is part of the licensing process when accepted for the classification being pursued, but the applicant must still complete the Arizona Registrar of Contractors application process and satisfy the state requirements for the license.

How many questions are on the NASCLA commercial general building exam?

The exam contains 115 questions. Candidates are allowed 5-1/2 hours, and the minimum passing score is 81 correct answers.

Can I highlight and tab my NASCLA books before the exam?

Yes. Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the exam. Permanent tabs are allowed. Temporary tabs, loose papers, attached papers, and writing in the books during the exam are not allowed.

Licensing

Can Arizona commercial contractor applicants use the NASCLA exam?

Yes. Arizona applicants applying for the B-1 or B-2 General Commercial classifications may choose the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors as an option instead of the Arizona ROC General Commercial trade exam.

More questions

How long is course access available?

The Ultimate package includes 1 year of access to the Arizona business course and the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor trade course.

Does Arizona accept the NASCLA examination?

Yes. Arizona accepts the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination as an alternative to the applicable state trade examination for qualifying B-1 and B-2 license applicants.

How long does order processing take?

Please allow an additional 15 business days for highlighted and tabbed rental-book package orders.

What topics are covered on the NASCLA commercial general building exam?

The exam covers general requirements, site construction, concrete, masonry, metals, wood, thermal and moisture protection, doors, windows, glazing, finishes, mechanical and plumbing systems, electrical systems, and procurement and contracting requirements.

Can I use a calculator on the NASCLA commercial general building exam?

Yes. Candidates may use a silent, non-printing, non-programmable calculator during the examination.
