The exam

How long should I allow for an Ultimate book package order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for ultimate book package orders. This allows time for processing and fulfillment of the rental materials included in the package.

Does passing the exam automatically give me the Arizona C-11 or CR-11 license?

No. Passing the required exam is part of the licensing process, but the applicant must still complete the Arizona Registrar of Contractors application process and satisfy the state requirements for the license classification.

How many questions are on the NASCLA electrical contractor exam?

The exam contains 100 scored questions and 10 non-scored experimental questions. The minimum passing score is 75, and candidates are allowed 270 minutes.

Can I highlight and tab my books before the exam?

Yes. Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the exam. Permanent tabs are allowed. Temporary tabs, loose papers, attached papers, and writing in the references are not allowed.

How much time is allowed for the exam?

Candidates are allowed 270 minutes to complete the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors.

What is the passing score?

The minimum passing score is 75.

Can I use temporary tabs in my books during the exam?

No. Temporary tabs are not allowed. Approved references may be tabbed or indexed with permanent tabs only, and removable notes or temporary tabs must be removed before the exam begins.

Does passing the NASCLA electrical exam automatically issue an Arizona license?

No. Passing the exam supports the trade examination portion of the licensing process. Applicants must still complete the Arizona contractor license application requirements that apply to their classification.

Can online practice questions help with an open-book electrical contractor exam?

Yes. Practice questions can help improve code navigation, topic recognition, calculation confidence, timing, reference use, and comfort with contractor exam wording before test day.

Licensing

What topics are covered on the NASCLA electrical contractor exam?

The exam covers project design and management, safety, electrical theory and principles, general code requirements, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, equipment for general use, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, and communication systems.

What subjects should I study for the NASCLA electrical contractor exam?

Candidates should study NEC code application, electrical theory, wiring methods, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding, bonding, overcurrent protection, motors, equipment, OSHA safety, NFPA 70E, project management, business law, and energy standards.

What is the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors?

The NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors is an electrical contractor exam for candidates who conduct business in the installation, maintenance, and repair of electrical work conforming to applicable standards and codes.

Can Arizona C-11 and CR-11 applicants take the NASCLA electrical contractor exam?

Yes. Arizona applicants applying for the C-11 or CR-11 electrical classifications may elect to take the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors instead of the AZ ROC Electrical Commercial exam.

What is the difference between C-11 and CR-11?

The C-11 Electrical classification is connected to commercial electrical contracting. The CR-11 Electrical classification is connected to residential and commercial electrical contracting. Students should choose the classification that matches the work they plan to perform.

Arizona applicants pursuing C-11 or CR-11 electrical licensing may have the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors available as a trade exam option for the applicable licensing path.
