## The exam

### How long should I allow for an Ultimate book package order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for ultimate book package orders. This allows time for processing and fulfillment of the rental materials included in the package.

### Does passing the exam automatically give me the Arizona R-11 license?

No. Passing the required exam is part of the licensing process, but the applicant must still complete the Arizona Registrar of Contractors application process and satisfy the state requirements for the license classification.

### How many questions are on the NASCLA Residential Electrical Contractors exam?

The exam contains 100 scored questions and 10 non-scored experimental questions. The minimum passing score is 70, and candidates are allowed 270 minutes.

### Can I write in my books during the exam?

No. References may be highlighted, underlined, annotated, and indexed before the exam, but candidates may not write, highlight, underline, or index references during the examination session.

### Does passing the NASCLA residential electrical exam automatically issue an Arizona license?

No. Passing the trade exam is one step in the licensing process. Applicants must still complete the Arizona contractor license application process and meet the requirements of the Arizona Registrar of Contractors.

### Can I highlight and tab my books before the exam?

Yes. Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the exam. Permanent tabs are allowed. Temporary tabs, loose papers, attached papers, and writing in the references during the exam are not allowed.

### Does passing the NASCLA residential electrical exam automatically give me an Arizona contractor license?

No. Passing the exam supports the trade-exam requirement, but applicants must still meet Arizona Registrar of Contractors licensing requirements and receive approval for the R-11 license classification.

### How many questions are on the NASCLA residential electrical contractor exam?

The exam contains 100 questions.

### How much time is allowed for the exam?

Candidates are allowed 270 minutes to complete the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Residential Electrical Contractors.

### What is the passing score?

The minimum passing score is 70.

### Which books are approved for the exam room?

The exam-room-approved references include National Electrical Code, 2023; Code of Federal Regulations – 29 CFR Part 1926; NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 14th Edition; NFPA 70E, 2024; Ugly’s Electrical References; NFPA 72, 2022; Understanding Electrical Theory for NEC Applications; ANSI Z535.4-2023; and International Residential Code, 2024.

### Can I use temporary tabs in my books during the exam?

No. Temporary tabs are not allowed. Approved references may be tabbed or indexed with permanent tabs only, and removable notes or temporary tabs must be removed before the exam begins.

### Can online practice questions help with an open-book residential electrical contractor exam?

Yes. Practice questions can help improve code navigation, topic recognition, calculation confidence, timing, reference use, and comfort with contractor exam wording before test day.

## Licensing

### Can Arizona R-11 applicants take the NASCLA residential electrical contractor exam?

Yes. Arizona applicants applying for the R-11 Electrical residential classification may elect to take the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Residential Electrical Contractors instead of the AZ ROC Electrical Residential exam.

### Can Arizona R-11 applicants use the NASCLA Residential Electrical Contractors exam?

Yes. Arizona applicants applying for the R-11 Electrical Residential classification may choose either the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Residential Electrical Contractors or the Arizona ROC Electrical Residential exam.

### What is the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Residential Electrical Contractors?

The NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Residential Electrical Contractors is a trade exam for candidates preparing to demonstrate knowledge of residential electrical installation and repair, code requirements, electrical theory, safety, wiring methods, grounding and bonding, tools, equipment, and project-related topics.

### What subjects should I study for the NASCLA residential electrical contractor exam?

Candidates should study NEC code application, residential wiring, electrical theory, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding, bonding, overcurrent protection, OSHA safety, NFPA 70E, NFPA 72, safety labeling, residential code, project management, and business law.
