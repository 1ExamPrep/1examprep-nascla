The exam

Does passing NASCLA automatically provide a California license?

No. Applicants must submit a California license application and satisfy all applicable experience, examination, fingerprinting, bonding, insurance, business, and license-issuance requirements.

Why are highlighted and tabbed books helpful?

Highlighting identifies useful information, while permanent tabs help candidates reach important chapters and technical subjects more efficiently during study and permitted open-book testing.

Should I study with the prepared books before test day?

Yes. Repeated practice helps candidates learn which publication applies to each subject and become familiar with the tabbing and highlighting system.

Are the books supplied by the examination center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all approved references to the NASCLA examination location.

Can I use older editions?

Candidates should use the editions shown on the current reference list because code provisions, page numbers, technical requirements, and book organization may differ between editions.

Can I add loose notes to the books?

Books must comply with the current testing rules. Candidates should avoid loose papers, removable notes, unauthorized inserts, and other prohibited materials.

Are the books provided at the testing center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all permitted references to the NASCLA examination location.

Should I study directly from the reference books?

Yes. Candidates should combine course review with regular practice using the listed editions so the books are familiar before examination day.

Licensing

Does NASCLA replace the California Law and Business exam?

No. A waiver of the B – General Building trade examination does not automatically waive the California Law and Business examination.

Does NASCLA waive the California Law and Business exam?

No. The NASCLA pathway concerns the qualifying B – General Building trade examination. Applicants must still complete the California Law and Business examination unless a separate waiver applies.

More questions

How long does order preparation take?

Please allow an additional 15 business days for highlighted and tabbed book package orders.

Does California accept the NASCLA examination?

California recognizes the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination for qualifying out-of-state applicants seeking a waiver of the B – General Building trade examination.

Who may qualify for the California NASCLA waiver?

A qualifying applicant must have passed the NASCLA commercial building examination and held a comparable out-of-state general building contractor license in good standing for the previous five years.

Who can request the California NASCLA trade-exam waiver?

The pathway applies to qualifying out-of-state contractors who have passed the NASCLA commercial building examination, hold an eligible license in good standing for the required period, and meet CSLB’s waiver conditions.
