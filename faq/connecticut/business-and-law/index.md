## The exam

### Is this the Connecticut-specific edition?

Yes. This is the Connecticut 6th Edition published by NASCLA.

### Is this the Connecticut 6th Edition?

Yes. The included book is the Connecticut-specific 6th Edition published by NASCLA.

### Is the NASCLA Connecticut 6th Edition an examination reference?

Yes. The Connecticut NASCLA 6th Edition is identified as a reference for the applicable Connecticut Business and Law Examination.

### Are highlighted and tabbed books automatically accepted during testing?

No. Reference materials must comply with examination rules, and the testing facility proctor makes the final decision regarding acceptance. Tabs must adhere securely.
