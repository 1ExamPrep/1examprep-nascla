Florida Commercial General Building Contractor exam: frequently asked questions

The exam

Does passing NASCLA automatically provide a Florida license?

No. Candidates must complete the applicable Florida licensing process and satisfy the state’s examination, experience, financial, fingerprinting, insurance, and application requirements.

Should I study with the prepared books before the exam?

Yes. Regular practice helps candidates learn which publication applies to each subject and become familiar with the highlighting and tabbing system.

Are the books supplied at the testing center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all permitted references to the testing location.

Can I add loose notes to the books?

Books must comply with current testing rules. Candidates should avoid loose papers, removable notes, unauthorized inserts, and other prohibited materials.

Should I use the listed editions?

Yes. Code provisions, technical requirements, page numbers, and book organization may differ between editions.

Must I pass Florida Business and Finance?

Yes. Applicants using the NASCLA endorsement pathway must pass the Florida Business and Finance examination.

Should I study directly from the listed books?

Yes. Candidates should combine online course review with regular practice using the listed editions so the reference library is familiar before examination day.

Licensing

Must I take the Florida Business and Finance examination?

Yes. Applicants using the NASCLA endorsement pathway must pass the Florida Business and Finance examination.

More questions

How long does order preparation take?

Please allow an additional 15 business days for highlighted and tabbed book package orders.

Why are highlighting and tabs helpful?

Highlighting identifies useful material, while permanent tabs help candidates reach major subjects, chapters, code sections, and technical information more efficiently.

Does Florida accept the NASCLA examination?

Yes. Florida accepts the NASCLA commercial building examination through the endorsement process for eligible Certified General, Building, and Residential Contractor applicants.

Is Florida Building Code examination coverage required?

Yes. NASCLA endorsement applicants must affirm that they have passed an examination covering the Florida Building Code.

How should I organize my study plan?

Study the Florida Business and Finance materials separately, group the NASCLA references by trade subject, and practice timed reference searches with the listed books.
