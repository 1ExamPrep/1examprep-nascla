## The exam

### Does passing NASCLA automatically provide a Florida contractor license?

No. Applicants must complete Florida's applicable licensing requirements, including Business and Finance and Florida Building Code examination requirements for the NASCLA endorsement pathway.

### Should I study with the prepared books before the exam?

Yes. Regular practice helps candidates learn which publication applies to each subject and become familiar with the highlighting and tabbing system.

### Are the books supplied at the testing center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all permitted references to the testing location.

### Can I add loose notes to the books?

Books must comply with current testing rules. Candidates should avoid loose papers, removable notes, unauthorized inserts, and other prohibited materials.

### Should I use the listed editions?

Yes. Code provisions, technical requirements, page numbers, and book organization may differ between editions.

### Does passing the NASCLA examination automatically provide a Florida contractor license?

No. Applicants must complete the applicable Florida endorsement process and satisfy the remaining examination, experience, financial, insurance, fingerprinting, and application requirements.

### How should candidates prepare for an open-book NASCLA examination?

Study with the listed references, learn their organization, practice identifying the appropriate publication for each subject, and develop consistent technical lookup skills.

### How many questions are on the NASCLA examination?

The examination includes 115 scored questions and 10 additional unscored pretest questions, for a total of 125 administered questions.

### What is the NASCLA examination passing score?

Candidates must answer at least 81 of the 115 scored questions correctly.

### Who administers the NASCLA examination?

PSI Services LLC administers the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors.

### What books are used for the NASCLA exam?

The authorized reference library includes the NASCLA Contractors Guide, International Building Code, OSHA construction standards, construction management publications, and specialized technical references.
