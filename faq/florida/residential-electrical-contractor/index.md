## The exam

### How many questions are on the NASCLA Residential Electrician examination?

The examination includes 100 scored questions and 10 additional unscored experimental questions.

### What is the passing score for the NASCLA Residential Electrician exam?

Candidates must answer at least 70 of the 100 scored questions correctly.

### Does passing NASCLA automatically provide a Florida electrical contractor license?

No. Applicants must satisfy Florida's separate licensing requirements. The NASCLA endorsement pathway also requires an existing qualifying license from another jurisdiction.

### Who administers the NASCLA Residential Electrician examination?

PSI Services LLC administers the examination through the NASCLA Accredited Examination Program.

### Which subject has the most NASCLA Residential Electrician questions?

Wiring Methods and Practices is the largest individual category, containing 17 scored questions.

### What NEC editions are used for the examination?

The authorized National Electrical Code editions are 2020 and 2023, with the corresponding NEC Handbook accepted as an alternative.

## Licensing

### Can experienced residential electricians benefit from ACE?

Yes. Experienced electricians may have strong installation skills but benefit from additional review of electrical theory, code navigation, calculations, grounding and bonding, or project management.

### Does ACE issue NASCLA credentials or Florida electrical contractor licenses?

No. ACE is an educational examination preparation resource. NASCLA manages its accredited examination program, and participating state licensing agencies determine professional licensure.

### How long is the NASCLA Residential Electrician exam?

The examination allows 270 minutes, equivalent to four hours and thirty minutes.

### Does Florida accept the NASCLA Residential Electrician examination?

Yes. Florida recognizes the NASCLA Residential Electrical Contractor examination for eligible Certified Residential Electrical Specialty Contractor endorsement applicants.

### What subjects are covered on the NASCLA Residential Electrician exam?

The examination covers electrical theory, jobsite safety, project management, grounding and bonding, circuit protection, wiring methods, equipment, special systems, testing tools, and energy efficiency.

### Can NASCLA Residential Electrician results be used in other states?

Yes. Participating licensing agencies may recognize NASCLA examination results as part of their licensing processes. Candidates must satisfy the requirements of each jurisdiction.
