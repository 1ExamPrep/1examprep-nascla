## The exam

### How many questions are on the Georgia Business & Law exam, and how long do I have?

The exam format is commonly presented as 50 questions with a 120-minute time limit. Follow your PSI approval and exam confirmation details for the most current testing information.

### Which reference book should I use to prepare?

Use the Georgia NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, GA State Licensing Board for Residential and General Contractors (3rd Edition) to study the business, law, and project-management topics commonly tested on the Georgia Business & Law exam.

### What score do I need to pass?

The PSI bulletin lists a minimum passing score of 70% (35 correct).

### Which book is allowed in the exam room?

PSI lists the Georgia-NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Georgia State Licensing Board for Residential and General Contractors (3rd Edition) as allowed in the examination center for the Business & Law exam.

### Are these exams open book?

It depends on the specific trade exam. Georgia reference lists for several divisions indicate the listed references may be used during the examination. For Plumbing, the reference list specifically states the Journeyman Plumber exam is open-book, and that for all other plumbing exams the listed references may not be used during the examination. Always verify using the current reference list for your exact exam.

### How many questions are on the Georgia Business and Law Examination?

The examination contains 50 questions .

### How much time is allowed for the examination?

Candidates have 120 minutes to complete the examination.

### What is the passing score?

The minimum passing score is 70%, or 35 correct answers .

### Who administers the Georgia Business and Law Examination?

PSI Services administers the examination.

### Is this the correct reference for the Georgia Business and Law Examination?

Yes. The Georgia NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 3rd Edition, is an authorized reference for the examination administered by PSI Services.

### How many questions are on the examination?

The examination contains 50 multiple-choice questions .

### Are permanent reference tabs permitted during testing?

PSI permits compliant permanent reference tabs, but the testing facility proctor makes the final decision about acceptance. Tabs must adhere securely.

### Are reference tabs accepted during Georgia examinations?

Permanent reference tabs are permitted under applicable examination rules, but acceptance depends on the examination and testing facility proctor.

### Are Georgia Construction Industry Licensing Board examinations open book?

Several applicable examinations permit authorized references, including conditioned air and utility manager examinations. Reference-use rules vary by trade and classification.

### Is highlighting allowed in the exam reference?

Yes. PSI states reference material may be highlighted, underlined, and/or indexed, but it must be otherwise unmarked (not written in) and may not contain additional papers (loose or attached).

### What reference book is allowed in the exam room for Georgia Business & Law?

PSI lists the Georgia-NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Georgia State Licensing Board for Residential and General Contractors (3rd Edition) as the reference allowed in the examination center for the Georgia Business & Law exam.

### What score do I need to pass the Georgia Business & Law exam?

PSI states the minimum passing score is 70% (35 correct).

### Are Georgia plumbing exams open book?

Georgia’s official plumbing exam suggested reference list states the Journeyman Plumber exam is open-book format. The same document states that for all other plumbing exams , the reference materials may not be used during the examination.

### What edition is this?

### Which licensing board is associated with this edition?

## Licensing

### Is the Georgia Business & Law exam required?

PSI’s Georgia contractor bulletin states all candidates must pass the Business & Law exam for licensure in Georgia as a Residential or General Contractor. After passing it, you have met the Business & Law exam requirement for all other Residential and General Contractor licenses.

### Which agency oversees Georgia residential and general contractor licensing?

The Georgia State Licensing Board for Residential and General Contractors oversees these contractor licensing programs.

### Is the Georgia Business and Law Examination required for residential contractors?

Yes. Georgia residential contractor applicants must satisfy the applicable Business and Law examination requirement.

### Is the Business and Law Examination required for general contractors?

Yes. Georgia general contractor applicants must also satisfy the Business and Law examination requirement.

## More questions

### Do I need to take a trade exam too?

Some Georgia contractor license classifications require a trade exam in addition to Business & Law. Your exact requirement depends on the license type and application path you select.

### How long is my exam approval valid in Georgia?

PSI states you are eligible to take the examination(s) for 1 year from the date of your exam approval letter, and you must pass both the Business & Law and any required trade exam within that eligibility period.

### Are pre-printed tabs allowed in the exam room?

Georgia reference lists for these trade exams state that references may be highlighted, underlined, or tabbed with permanent tabs . Keep tabs permanent and avoid adding any extra materials that are not permitted.

### Are tabs and highlighting allowed?

Georgia reference lists for several divisions include a candidate notice stating that references may be highlighted, underlined, or tabbed with permanent tabs and warn against bringing photocopied materials or handwritten notes (even if pasted into a book). Always follow the current notice and reference list for your exact exam.
