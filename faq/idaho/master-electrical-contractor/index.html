Idaho Master Electrical Contractor exam: frequently asked questions

The exam

Are all rental books highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. All rental books included in this package are highlighted and tabbed for organized study and reference navigation.

Does an Idaho Master Electrician applicant need to pass the exam before applying?

Yes. Idaho's Master Electrician application requires initial applicants to pass the applicable examination before submitting the license application.

Does passing the NASCLA examination automatically issue an Idaho license?

No. Passing an applicable NASCLA examination can satisfy an examination requirement, but applicants must still complete the Idaho licensing process and meet the experience, application, supervision, and other requirements associated with the credential they are seeking.

Why use highlighted and tabbed rental books?

Highlighted and tabbed references provide an organized way to study large technical books. Tabs help candidates reach important sections efficiently, while highlighting makes key material easier to recognize during repeated study and practice.

How many questions are on the NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination?

The examination contains 100 scored questions.

What score is required to pass the NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination?

The minimum passing score is 70.

How much time is allowed for the NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam?

Candidates are allowed 270 minutes, or four hours and thirty minutes, to complete the examination.

Can NASCLA electrical books be highlighted and tabbed?

Approved NASCLA references may be highlighted, underlined, and permanently indexed in accordance with current examination rules. Temporary markers and removable notes are not permitted.

Who administers electrical examinations in Idaho?

Idaho currently offers electrical examinations through PSI. Candidates select their applicable examination and can access the exam-specific candidate bulletin through the Idaho PSI testing system.

How long should I allow for a highlighted and tabbed book package order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for tabbed and highlighted book package orders.

Can I highlight and index my exam books?

Idaho's current electrical examination guidance permits pre-exam highlighting, underlining, and permanent indexing of permitted references. Candidates should follow the current candidate bulletin because rules governing reference preparation and testing materials may vary by examination.

Does passing the NASCLA exam automatically give me an Idaho license?

No. A NASCLA examination result can satisfy an examination requirement accepted by Idaho, but candidates must still complete the applicable Idaho licensing process and meet all qualification, application, and supervision requirements.

Where can I view the live class schedule?

View the current schedule on the Live Class Schedule page.

Does passing the NASCLA exam automatically issue an Idaho license?

No. NASCLA examination results can satisfy an examination requirement accepted by a participating agency, but applicants must still complete Idaho's licensing process and meet all applicable state qualifications.

Why is NEC navigation important for an open-book examination?

The NEC contains a large amount of technical material. Practicing navigation helps candidates identify the correct article, section, table, exception, or definition more efficiently instead of spending unnecessary examination time searching.

Licensing

Does Idaho accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination?

Yes. The Idaho Electrical Board, Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses, participates in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and accepts the Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination.

Is the NASCLA exam an Idaho electrical license?

No. The NASCLA examination can satisfy an accepted trade-examination requirement, but applicants must still complete Idaho's licensing process and receive approval for the applicable credential.

Does an Idaho contractor business need liability insurance?

Idaho contractor registration requirements include general liability insurance of at least $300,000 single limit, including products and continued operations coverage.

Does Idaho require workers' compensation documentation for contractor registration?

Yes. Applicants must provide proof of workers' compensation insurance when required or provide the applicable statement explaining why coverage is not required.

What does an individual need to qualify for an Idaho Electrical Contractor license?

An individual contractor applicant must have completed 2,000 hours of work while holding an Idaho Electrical Journeyman license or hold an active Idaho Master Electrician license.

Can a business entity obtain an Idaho Electrical Contractor license?

Yes. A business entity may apply for an Electrical Contractor license and must designate a supervisory employee to represent the entity for examination purposes.

Does an Idaho electrical contractor need a supervising electrician?

Yes. Electrical contracting work must be performed under the supervision of an appropriately licensed electrical professional within the applicable scope of work. An individual contractor may serve as the supervising electrician when the individual holds the required active electrical license.

What insurance is required before an Idaho Electrical Contractor license is issued?

After passing the examination, contractor applicants must provide proof of $300,000 in liability insurance and workers' compensation insurance unless exempt from the workers' compensation requirement.

Does an Idaho electrical contractor need a master electrician?

Yes. Idaho requires an electrical contractor to have at least one full-time employee who holds a valid master electrician license and serves as the supervising electrician. An individual contractor who holds the appropriate master license may serve as the contractor's own supervising master electrician.

How much experience is required for an Idaho Master Electrician license?

Idaho requires an applicant using the standard experience pathway to have at least four years of experience as a licensed journeyman electrician before qualifying for the Master Electrician licensing process.

Does Idaho recognize the NASCLA examination for Master Electrician licensing?

Yes. Idaho's current Master Electrician license application specifically asks applicants whether they have taken and passed the applicable NASCLA examination through PSI.

Can a contractor's scope exceed the supervising electrician's license scope?

No. Idaho rules state that the contractor's allowable scope of work follows the scope of its licensed supervising employee.

Can an individual contractor serve as the supervising electrician?

Yes. An individual electrical contractor may act as the supervising electrician when the individual holds the appropriate active electrical license for the work being performed.

What subjects are covered on the NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination?

The published examination domains include Project Design and Management, Safety, Electrical Theory, General Code Requirements, Wiring and Protection, Wiring Methods and Materials, Equipment for General Use, Special Occupancies, Equipment and Conditions, and Communication Systems.

How much experience does Idaho require for a master electrician license?

Idaho's experience pathway requires a master electrician applicant to have held an electrical journeyman license for at least four years before qualifying for master licensure.

How should I study for the Idaho NASCLA Master Electrical Contractor exam?

Combine trade-focused review with electrical calculations, NEC navigation, safety preparation, project-management study, and timed reference practice. Working actively with the books can help build the familiarity needed for an open-book professional electrical examination.

More questions

How long should I allow for my rental package order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for book & course rental package orders.

Does Idaho also accept the NASCLA Journeyman Electrician examination?

Yes. Idaho accepts the NASCLA Journeyman Electrician examination as part of its participation in the NASCLA electrical program.

What happens if I took the NASCLA exam outside Idaho?

Idaho's Master Electrician application requires applicants who took the applicable NASCLA examination outside Idaho to include a copy of their test scores.

Why combine live training with self-study?

Live instruction provides structured technical review, while self-study gives candidates additional time to repeat calculations, practice NEC navigation, review safety references, and reinforce difficult subjects between scheduled classes.

Can I use NASCLA examination results in other states?

Passing results can be transmitted through the NASCLA National Examination Database to participating regulatory agencies that accept the applicable examination. Each jurisdiction maintains its own licensing qualifications and application requirements.
