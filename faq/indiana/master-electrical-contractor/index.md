## The exam

### Are all rental books highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. All books included in this rental package are highlighted and tabbed for organized study and reference navigation.

### Who administers the Vanderburgh County NASCLA electrical examination?

Prov administers the NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination for Vanderburgh County candidates.

### How many questions are on the exam?

The current Vanderburgh County NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination contains 110 total questions, including 10 experimental questions.

### How much time is allowed for the NASCLA electrical exam?

Candidates are allowed 270 minutes, or four hours and thirty minutes, to complete the examination.

### What is the passing score?

The current passing score for the Vanderburgh County NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination is 75%.

### Can the exam books be highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. Current Prov rules allow highlighting, underlining in pen, and permanent tabs. Handwritten notes, moveable tabs, Post-it notes, and added photocopied documents are not permitted.

### Does passing the NASCLA exam automatically issue a Vanderburgh County license?

No. Passing the examination addresses an accepted examination component. Candidates must still complete the local licensing process and satisfy the applicable experience, application, insurance, bond, workers' compensation, and other requirements.

### What passing score does Vanderburgh County require for the master-level contractor examination?

The current candidate bulletin establishes a 75% cut score for master-level contractor examinations.

### Can I add notes to my books during the examination?

No. Candidates may not highlight, underline, mark, or write information in their references during testing.

### Do I schedule directly with Prov before registering with Vanderburgh County?

No. Candidates first register with the local authority and receive examination eligibility. After the candidate's testing information has been provided to Prov, the examination can be scheduled.

### How long should I allow for a highlighted and tabbed book package order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for tabbed and highlighted book package orders.

### Does passing the examination automatically issue a Vanderburgh County electrical contractor license?

No. Passing the examination satisfies the applicable testing component, but candidates must still complete the local licensing process and meet the additional requirements established by Vanderburgh County.

### How many scored questions are on the NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam?

The examination contains 100 scored questions covering electrical Code requirements, theory, safety, project management, wiring methods, equipment, special conditions, and related professional subjects.

### What is the passing score for the NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination?

The current NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination requires a minimum passing score of 70.

### Who handles scheduling for the Vanderburgh County NASCLA electrical exam?

Vanderburgh County candidates can schedule the applicable NASCLA electrical examination through Prov.

Yes. Current Prov rules allow highlighting, underlining in pen, and permanent tabs in approved references. Moveable tabs, Post-it notes, handwritten notes, and additional photocopied materials are not permitted.

### What score is required to pass the NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam?

The minimum passing score for the NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination is 70.

## Licensing

### Does Vanderburgh County, Indiana accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician exam?

Yes. Vanderburgh County is an official participating agency in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and accepts the Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination.

### How much electrical experience is required for the local electrical licensing process?

The Evansville-Vanderburgh County Building Commission's current licensing process requires electrical applicants to document at least four years or 6,200 hours of qualifying experience in the electrical field.
