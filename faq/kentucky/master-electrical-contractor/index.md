## The exam

### Are all rental books highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. All books included in this rental package are highlighted and tabbed for organized study and reference navigation.

### What National Electrical Code edition is Kentucky currently using?

Kentucky currently uses the 2023 National Electrical Code. Beginning January 1, 2026, Kentucky electrical licensing examinations are based on the 2023 NEC.

### Does passing the NASCLA examination automatically issue a Kentucky license?

No. Passing the NASCLA examination can satisfy an accepted examination component, but applicants must still complete Kentucky's licensing process and meet the applicable experience, application, master-electrician affiliation, insurance, and other requirements.

### Why use highlighted and tabbed rental books?

Highlighted and tabbed references provide an organized way to study large technical books. Tabs help candidates reach major sections more efficiently, while highlighting makes important information easier to identify during regular study and practice.

### How many questions are on the NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam?

The examination contains 100 scored questions covering technical electrical knowledge, Code requirements, safety, theory, project management, equipment, wiring methods, and related subjects.

### What score is required to pass the NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam?

The current examination information requires candidates to answer 75 of the 100 scored questions correctly.

### How much time is allowed for the NASCLA electrical exam?

Candidates are allowed 270 minutes, or four hours and thirty minutes, to complete the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors.

### Does passing the NASCLA exam automatically give me a Kentucky license?

No. Passing the NASCLA examination can satisfy an accepted examination requirement, but applicants must still complete the Kentucky licensing process and meet all applicable experience, application, and contractor requirements.

### Why should I practice with the reference books before the examination?

The examination is open book but timed. Practicing with the references helps candidates learn which book to use, where important Code sections and formulas are located, and how to navigate technical information more efficiently.

### Where can I view the live class schedule?

View the current schedule on the Live Class Schedule page.

### What NEC edition does Kentucky currently use?

Kentucky electrical licensing examinations are based on the 2023 National Electrical Code as of January 1, 2026.

### Why is reference navigation important for the NASCLA electrical exam?

The examination is open book, but the references contain extensive technical information. Practicing navigation can help candidates identify the appropriate reference and locate applicable information more efficiently during timed preparation.

### What is the passing score for the NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam?

The current NASCLA examination information lists a minimum passing score of 70 for the Electrical Contractor examination.

### Who administers the NASCLA examination?

Approved candidates receive scheduling information for the NASCLA examination through PSI. NASCLA manages the examination application process and maintains passing results through the NASCLA National Examination Database.

### Does passing the NASCLA exam automatically make me a Kentucky master electrician?

No. Passing the NASCLA examination addresses an accepted examination requirement. Applicants must still complete the Kentucky licensing process and meet the state's experience and documentation requirements before a license can be issued.

### Why should I practice finding information in the reference books?

Because the examination is timed, familiarity with the references can help reduce unnecessary searching. Practice teaches you which book to use, where common topics are located, and how to confirm an answer efficiently.

## Licensing

### Does Kentucky accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam for master electrician licensing?

Yes. Kentucky's Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction accepts the NASCLA examination as an examination option for master electrician applicants. Applicants must still satisfy Kentucky's separate experience, application, and licensing requirements.

### How much experience is required for a Kentucky Master Electrician license?

Kentucky provides multiple qualifying pathways. One pathway requires eight years of verifiable electrical-trade experience. Other pathways allow qualifying training or education to reduce the amount of experience required when the applicant meets the applicable statutory conditions.

### Is a Kentucky master electrician license the same as an electrical contractor license?

No. Kentucky regulates master electricians and electrical contractors as separate credentials. An electrical contractor must meet the contractor licensing requirements and identify the Kentucky licensed master electrician affiliated with the contractor.

### Does Kentucky separately license Electrical Contractors and Master Electricians?

Yes. Kentucky has separate licenses for Electrical Contractors, Master Electricians, and Electricians.

### How much experience does Kentucky require for a master electrician license?

Kentucky requires 16,000 hours of verifiable electrical-trade experience for a master electrician applicant. Qualifying education, training, or teaching experience may substitute for portions of that requirement under Kentucky law.

### Can NASCLA examination results be used for licensing in other states?

Passing NASCLA examination results may be transmitted to participating state agencies through the NASCLA National Examination Database. Each jurisdiction maintains its own licensing requirements, so acceptance of the examination does not create a national electrical contractor or master electrician license.
