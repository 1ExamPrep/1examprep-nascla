## The exam

### How long does book preparation take?

Please allow an additional 15 business days for highlighted and tabbed book package orders.

### Does passing NASCLA automatically issue a Louisiana license?

No. Applicants must complete the Louisiana licensing process and satisfy all applicable business, qualifying-party, financial, insurance, classification, and documentation requirements.

### Should I study with the prepared books before test day?

Yes. Repeated practice helps candidates learn which reference applies to each subject and become familiar with the highlighting and tabbing system.

### Are the books supplied at the examination center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all permitted references to the testing location.

### Can I add loose notes to the books?

Books must comply with current testing rules. Candidates should avoid loose papers, removable notes, unauthorized inserts, and other prohibited materials.

### Does passing NASCLA automatically provide a Louisiana license?

No. Applicants must complete the Louisiana license application and satisfy the applicable business, financial, qualifying-party, insurance, classification, and licensing requirements.

### Are the books supplied at the testing center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all permitted references to the testing location.

### Should I use the listed editions?

Yes. Code provisions, technical requirements, page numbers, illustrations, and book organization may differ between editions.

### Should I study directly from the listed books?

Yes. Candidates should combine course review with regular practice using the listed references so the library is familiar before examination day.

## Licensing

### Is Louisiana Business and Law required?

Yes. Each applicant or licensee must have at least one approved qualifying party for Louisiana Business and Law. The current requirement is an online course with an examination at the conclusion.

## More questions

### Does Louisiana accept the NASCLA examination?

Yes. The Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors for the applicable trade-examination requirement.

### Why are highlighting and tabs useful?

Highlighting identifies useful material, while permanent tabs help candidates reach important chapters, code sections, tables, and technical subjects more efficiently.

### Does the NASCLA result expire?

NASCLA examination results remain in the National Examination Database. Candidates must still follow the Louisiana application policies and any timing requirements associated with their state licensing file.
