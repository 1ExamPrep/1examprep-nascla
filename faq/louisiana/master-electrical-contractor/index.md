## The exam

### Are all rental books highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. All rental books in this package are highlighted and tabbed for organized study and reference navigation.

### Does passing the NASCLA exam automatically issue a Louisiana contractor license?

No. The NASCLA examination can satisfy an accepted trade-examination component, but the contractor business and qualifying party must still complete Louisiana's licensing process and receive approval from the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors.

### How many questions are on the NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination?

The NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination contains 100 scored questions.

### What is the minimum passing score?

The minimum passing score for the NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination is 70.

### How much time is allowed for the examination?

Candidates are allowed 270 minutes, or four hours and thirty minutes, to complete the examination.

## Licensing

### Does Louisiana accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination?

Yes. The Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors participates in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and accepts the Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination.

### When is a Louisiana commercial contractor license required for electrical work?

Louisiana requires a commercial contractor license for electrical projects exceeding $10,000 in value, including labor and materials, when the work falls within the state's regulated contractor licensing requirements.

### Does Louisiana have separate Business and Law requirements?

Yes. Louisiana contractor licensing includes applicable business and law requirements in addition to the electrical trade qualification.

### Can I use a NASCLA transcript when applying in Louisiana?

Yes. Louisiana's contractor licensing application allows applicants requesting NASCLA examination credit for Electrical to have their passing NASCLA transcript electronically sent to the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors.

### Is Business and Law separate from the NASCLA electrical examination?

Yes. Louisiana's Business and Law requirement is separate from the NASCLA Electrical Contractor trade examination and must be satisfied as part of the state's contractor licensing process.

### Is there a Louisiana Business and Law requirement in addition to the electrical examination?

Yes. Qualifying parties for commercial contractor applicants must complete the Louisiana Business and Law requirement unless it has already been satisfied. The current online Business and Law examination contains 50 questions and requires a score of 70 or higher.

### Does Louisiana require a contractor license for electrical work over $10,000?

Yes. Louisiana requires the applicable commercial contractor license for Electrical projects exceeding $10,000 in value, including labor and materials.

### Does Louisiana require Business and Law when using a NASCLA result?

Yes. Candidates using NASCLA examination credit must satisfy Louisiana's applicable Business and Law requirement as part of the contractor licensing process.

### How is the current Louisiana Business and Law requirement structured?

The current Louisiana Business and Law test is completed through the contractor licensing portal. It contains 50 questions and requires a score of 70 or higher.

## More questions

### How long should I allow for my rental package order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for book & course rental package orders.

### Is Electrical a major classification in Louisiana?

Yes. Electrical is a major commercial contractor classification administered by the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors.

### Does Louisiana require a qualifying party?

Yes. Louisiana contractor applicants must designate a qualifying party who represents the licensed entity for Business and Law and for the requirements associated with each contractor classification.

### What should I focus on during my one year of course access?

Study should include National Electrical Code navigation, electrical theory, calculations, grounding and bonding, wiring methods, circuit protection, equipment, construction safety, workplace electrical safety, project management, energy topics, and timed reference practice.

### Does Louisiana need my NASCLA transcript?

Yes. Applicants requesting credit from a passing NASCLA Electrical examination must have the applicable transcript released to the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors through the NASCLA examination database.

### Can the contracting company submit the NASCLA transcript directly?

No. Louisiana requires the NASCLA transcript to be released through the NASCLA examination system so the Board can access the passing result.

### Why combine live instruction with self-study?

Live instruction provides structure and guided technical review, while self-study gives candidates additional time to repeat calculations, practice reference navigation, revisit difficult Code sections, and reinforce concepts between scheduled classes.
