## The exam

### Is the NASCLA 7th Edition approved for the Maryland Home Improvement Contractor exam?

Yes. The NASCLA Contractor's Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Maryland Home Improvement Commission, 7th Edition, is an approved reference for the Maryland Home Improvement Contractor examination administered by PSI.

### How many questions are on the Maryland MHIC Contractor exam?

The contractor examination contains 55 questions. The passing score is 39 correct answers, and candidates receive 150 minutes to complete the examination.

### Can I use the NASCLA 6th Edition instead of the 7th Edition?

No. Beginning January 1, 2026, the Maryland Home Improvement Contractor examination uses the NASCLA Maryland Home Improvement Commission 7th Edition as its designated reference.

### Can I highlight or tab my NASCLA reference book?

Yes. PSI permits highlighting, underlining, and acceptable permanent indexing tabs. Books must otherwise remain unmarked and cannot contain additional loose or attached papers. Removable tabs are not permitted.

### Who administers the Maryland Home Improvement Contractor exam?

PSI Services administers the examination on behalf of the Maryland Home Improvement Commission. Candidates must obtain the required examination approval and schedule their test through PSI.

### How many questions are on the Maryland Home Improvement Contractor exam?

The contractor examination contains 55 questions and requires a passing score of at least 70%. Questions cover home improvement law, door-to-door sales regulations, labor laws, safety regulations, and estimating.

### Is this the correct NASCLA edition for the Maryland MHIC contractor examination?

Yes. The Maryland Home Improvement Commission, 7th Edition, is the designated NASCLA contractor reference for examinations beginning January 1, 2026.

### Are highlighted and tabbed reference books allowed during the examination?

Yes. PSI permits approved reference books to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed. Tabs must be securely attached and comply with the testing provider's requirements. The examination proctor makes the final determination regarding tab acceptance.

## Licensing

### Do I need construction experience to obtain a Maryland Home Improvement Contractor license?

Maryland generally requires at least two years of qualifying trade experience or acceptable educational training. The Maryland Home Improvement Commission reviews applicants' qualifications under its established licensing requirements.

### How long are Maryland Home Improvement Contractor examination results valid?

Passing examination results remain valid for two years. Candidates must complete the applicable license application process during that period.

### How much trade experience is required for Maryland Home Improvement Contractor licensing?

Maryland generally requires at least two years of qualifying trade experience or comparable educational training accepted by the Maryland Home Improvement Commission.

## More questions

### What subjects are covered in the Maryland Home Improvement exam?

The examination covers Home Improvement Law, the Door-to-Door Sales Act, Labor Laws, Safety Regulations, and Estimating. Home Improvement Law accounts for the largest portion of the contractor examination.

### Can I use removable sticky tabs instead?

Removable sticky notes and detachable markers are not permitted as examination tabs. Permanent-adhesive tabs must be securely attached and meet the testing center's examination requirements.

### Can the flash cards be brought into the examination room?

No. The flash cards are supplemental study materials intended for preparation before examination day. They are not among the approved examination-room references.

### What subjects are covered on the Maryland MHIC examination?

The examination covers Home Improvement Law, the Door-to-Door Sales Act, Labor Laws, Safety Regulations, and Estimating. Home Improvement Law represents the largest portion of the examination.
