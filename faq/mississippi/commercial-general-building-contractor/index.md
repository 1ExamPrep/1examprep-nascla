## The exam

### How long does book preparation take?

Please allow an additional 15 business days for highlighted and tabbed book package orders.

### Does passing NASCLA automatically issue a Mississippi license?

No. Applicants must complete the Mississippi licensing process and satisfy all applicable business, qualifying-party, financial, insurance, classification, and application requirements.

### Should I study with the prepared books before the examination?

Yes. Repeated practice helps candidates learn which reference applies to each subject and become familiar with the highlighting and tabbing system.

### Are the books provided at the testing center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all permitted references to the testing location.

### Can I add loose notes to the books?

Books must comply with current testing rules. Candidates should avoid loose papers, removable notes, unauthorized inserts, and other prohibited materials.

### Who administers Mississippi contractor examinations?

PSI administers the Mississippi contractor examination program after the candidate receives eligibility through the Mississippi State Board of Contractors.

### Are books provided at the examination center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all permitted reference books to the testing location.

### Should I use the listed editions?

Yes. Code sections, technical requirements, page numbers, illustrations, and book organization may differ between editions.

### Does passing NASCLA automatically provide a Mississippi license?

No. Applicants must complete the Mississippi licensing process and satisfy the applicable qualifying-party, financial, insurance, classification, and documentation requirements.

### Should I study directly from the listed books?

Yes. Candidates should combine the online course with regular practice using the listed references so the examination library becomes familiar before test day.

## Licensing

### Is Mississippi Law and Business Management still required?

Yes. Mississippi contractor applicants must satisfy the state Law and Business Management examination requirement even when using the NASCLA examination for the trade portion.

## More questions

### Does Mississippi accept the NASCLA examination?

Yes. Applicants seeking the Mississippi Building Construction major classification may choose the NASCLA accredited examination instead of the Mississippi Building Construction trade examination.

### Why are highlighting and tabs helpful?

Highlighting identifies useful material, while permanent tabs help candidates reach important chapters, code sections, tables, and technical subjects more efficiently.

### Does Mississippi accept the NASCLA commercial building examination?

Yes. Mississippi accepts the NASCLA examination as an alternative to the state Building Construction trade examination for the Building Construction major classification.
