## The exam

### How many questions are on the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor exam?

The examination contains 115 scored questions plus 10 non-scored pretest questions.

### How many questions do I need to answer correctly to pass?

Candidates must answer at least 81 of the 115 scored questions correctly .

### Can I highlight and tab my NASCLA books?

Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed. Permanent tabs are permitted. Temporary tabs such as Post-it notes are not allowed, and written notes or additional papers may not be added to the references.

### Does passing the NASCLA exam give me a national contractor license?

No. The NASCLA examination is not a national contractor license. Participating licensing agencies may accept the examination in place of their applicable trade exam, but candidates must still meet the licensing requirements of each jurisdiction where they apply.

### Who administers the NASCLA examination?

PSI Services LLC delivers the examination after a candidate has received examination approval through NASCLA.

### How many times can I take the exam during my eligibility period?

NASCLA currently permits an eligible candidate to sit for the examination up to three times during the one-year eligibility period that begins with application approval.

### Should I study the approved books in addition to these flash cards?

Yes. The flash cards are a study supplement. Candidates should also work extensively with the approved references because the examination is open book and effective book navigation is an important part of preparation.

## More questions

### What NASCLA examination are these flash cards designed for?

They are designed to support preparation for the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors.

### How long is the NASCLA exam?

Candidates receive 330 minutes , or 5½ hours, to complete the examination.

### Is the NASCLA exam computer based?

Yes. The NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination is administered by computer at authorized testing locations.

### How should I use the 500 flash cards?

Divide the cards into manageable study groups, test yourself before checking each answer, and isolate difficult cards for more frequent review. Combine flash-card study with approved-reference navigation, calculations, plan reading, code review, and practice questions.
