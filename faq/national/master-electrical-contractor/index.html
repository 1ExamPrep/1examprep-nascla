Nationwide Master Electrical Contractor exam: frequently asked questions

12 answers about the Nationwide master electrical contractor exam and license, compiled from our product descriptions for this exam.

The exam

How many questions are on the NASCLA Master Electrician exam?

The examination contains 100 scored questions plus 10 additional non-scored experimental questions.

What score do I need to pass the NASCLA Master Electrician exam?

The current examination bulletin identifies a minimum passing score of 75 .

Can I highlight and tab my electrical reference books?

Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed. Permanent tabs are permitted. Written notes, additional papers, and temporary Post-it-style tabs are not allowed.

Does passing the NASCLA exam automatically give me an electrical contractor license?

No. The NASCLA examination can satisfy the trade-examination requirement for participating licensing agencies, but candidates must still meet the individual jurisdiction’s licensing requirements.

How many times can I take the NASCLA exam?

Candidates applying through NASCLA currently receive up to three attempts during the one-year eligibility period .

Should I study the approved references in addition to using flash cards?

Yes. The flash cards are a study supplement. Candidates should also practice extensively with the NEC and other approved references because the examination is open book and efficient reference navigation is an important preparation skill.

More questions

What NASCLA exam do these flash cards prepare me for?

They are designed to support preparation for the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Contractor examination for Master Electricians and Unlimited Electricians.

How long is the NASCLA Master Electrical exam?

Candidates receive 270 minutes , or 4½ hours, to complete the examination.

Is the NASCLA Master Electrician exam computer based?

Yes. The examination is administered by computer through PSI.

What subjects are covered on the NASCLA Master Electrical exam?

The content outline includes project design and management, safety, electrical theory and principles, general code requirements, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, equipment for general use, special occupancies and equipment, special conditions, and communication systems.

Can I use the National Electrical Code during the examination?

Yes. The current approved reference list permits the 2020 or 2023 National Electrical Code or National Electrical Code Handbook.

How should I use the 250 flash cards?

Divide the cards into smaller study groups, test yourself before checking each answer, and separate difficult cards for additional review. Combine flash-card practice with NEC navigation, calculations, safety study, plan interpretation, and work with the other approved examination references.
