The exam

Are all rental books highlighted and tabbed?

What National Electrical Code edition does Nebraska currently use?

Nebraska adopted the 2023 National Electrical Code effective August 1, 2024, and electrical examinations transitioned to the 2023 NEC at that time.

Does passing the NASCLA examination automatically issue a Nebraska Electrical Contractor license?

No. Passing an accepted NASCLA examination satisfies an examination component, but applicants must still complete Nebraska's application and licensing requirements and receive approval from the Nebraska State Electrical Division.

Licensing

What are the Nebraska Electrical Contractor qualification pathways?

The Nebraska State Electrical Division currently lists qualification through a four-year electrical engineering degree from an accredited college or university plus at least one year of acceptable licensed journeyman experience, or at least one year of experience acceptable to the Board as a licensed journeyman electrician.

What experience is required for a Nebraska Electrical Contractor license?

An applicant may qualify with at least one year of experience acceptable to the Board as a licensed journeyman electrician. Nebraska also provides an education-and-experience pathway for graduates of an accredited four-year electrical engineering program who have at least one year of acceptable licensed journeyman experience.

Does Nebraska accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination?

Yes. The Nebraska State Electrical Division participates in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and administers the Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination for qualified applicants.

What experience can qualify an applicant for a Nebraska Electrical Contractor license?

Nebraska provides multiple qualification pathways. These include qualifying electrical-engineering education plus acceptable journeyman experience, qualifying licensed journeyman experience, or an experience pathway based on acceptable planning, layout, supervision, and installation work.

What experience does Nebraska require for an Electrical Contractor license?

Nebraska provides multiple qualification pathways. These include qualifying electrical engineering education combined with journeyman experience, qualifying licensed journeyman experience, or an experience pathway based on substantial acceptable work planning, laying out, supervising, and installing electrical wiring and equipment.

Does Nebraska participate in the NASCLA electrical program?

Yes. The Nebraska State Electrical Division participates in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and administers the Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination.

Does the NASCLA exam have to be taken through Nebraska?

Yes. Nebraska's current NASCLA participation requirements state that the examination must be taken through the Nebraska State Electrical Division for the result to be accepted under this pathway.

Is there a date requirement for Nebraska NASCLA exam acceptance?

Yes. Nebraska requires the NASCLA examination date to be after August 1, 2025 for acceptance under its current electrical examination pathway.

Does Nebraska have a special requirement for accepting the NASCLA electrical examination?

Yes. The exam must be taken through the Nebraska State Electrical Division, and the examination date must be after August 1, 2025 for Nebraska acceptance.

Does Nebraska use the 2023 National Electrical Code?

Yes. Nebraska adopted the 2023 National Electrical Code effective August 1, 2024, and Nebraska electrical examinations transitioned to the 2023 NEC beginning on that date.

Does Nebraska require the NASCLA examination to be taken through the state?

Yes. Nebraska requires the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination to be taken through the Nebraska State Electrical Division.

Is there a required examination date for Nebraska NASCLA acceptance?

Yes. Nebraska requires the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination date to be after August 1, 2025 for the result to be accepted.

Does Nebraska's state examination cover more than the NEC?

Yes. Nebraska states that its electrical license examinations may include the 2023 National Electrical Code, basic electricity and theory, the Nebraska State Electrical Act, and emergency circuits, including fire-alarm circuits.
