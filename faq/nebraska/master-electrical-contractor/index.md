## Licensing

### What are the Nebraska Electrical Contractor qualification pathways?

The Nebraska State Electrical Division currently lists qualification through a four-year electrical engineering degree from an accredited college or university plus at least one year of acceptable licensed journeyman experience, or at least one year of experience acceptable to the Board as a licensed journeyman electrician.

### What experience is required for a Nebraska Electrical Contractor license?

An applicant may qualify with at least one year of experience acceptable to the Board as a licensed journeyman electrician. Nebraska also provides an education-and-experience pathway for graduates of an accredited four-year electrical engineering program who have at least one year of acceptable licensed journeyman experience.

### Does Nebraska accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination?

Yes. The Nebraska State Electrical Division participates in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and administers the Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination for qualified applicants.

### What experience can qualify an applicant for a Nebraska Electrical Contractor license?

Nebraska provides multiple qualification pathways. These include qualifying electrical-engineering education plus acceptable journeyman experience, qualifying licensed journeyman experience, or an experience pathway based on acceptable planning, layout, supervision, and installation work.

### What experience does Nebraska require for an Electrical Contractor license?

Nebraska provides multiple qualification pathways. These include qualifying electrical engineering education combined with journeyman experience, qualifying licensed journeyman experience, or an experience pathway based on substantial acceptable work planning, laying out, supervising, and installing electrical wiring and equipment.
