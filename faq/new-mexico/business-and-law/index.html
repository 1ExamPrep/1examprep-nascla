The exam

What is the New Mexico NASCLA Contractor's Guide Basic 14th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Basic 14th Edition, a reference publication identified for New Mexico contractor Business and Law examination preparation.

Is the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition an approved New Mexico examination reference?

Yes. NASCLA identifies the Basic 14th Edition as a reference for the New Mexico Business and Law examination.

Who administers New Mexico contractor examinations?

PSI Services provides examination services for contractor licensing administered through the New Mexico Construction Industries Division.

What is the ISBN of the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition?

The ISBN-13 is 978-1-948558-39-6.

Can I use tabs in my New Mexico contractor examination reference book?

Permanent tabs are permitted when they comply with examination requirements. Temporary tabs and removable sticky notes are prohibited. Testing personnel determine whether the reference materials meet the applicable rules.

Does New Mexico require every qualifying party to pass the Business and Law examination?

New Mexico permits the Business and Law requirement to be satisfied through the examination or an approved Business and Law course.

Is the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition approved for the New Mexico Business and Law exam?

Yes. The NASCLA Basic 14th Edition is identified as an approved reference for the New Mexico Business and Law examination.

What subjects does the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition cover?

The guide covers business planning, licensing, insurance, estimating, contracts, scheduling, project management, workplace safety, financial administration, taxation fundamentals, and lien laws.

What is the New Mexico NASCLA Highlighted & Tabbed Basic 14th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Basic 14th Edition, professionally prepared with highlighted content and permanent reference tabs for organized New Mexico Business and Law examination preparation.

Are highlighted and tabbed references permitted during the examination?

Approved reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed with permanent tabs when they comply with PSI examination rules.

Are highlighted and tabbed reference books permitted during the examination?

Approved reference materials may be highlighted and indexed when they comply with the examination rules. Testing personnel determine whether the materials and tabs are acceptable.

Licensing

Does New Mexico offer a Business and Law course alternative?

Yes. New Mexico permits the Business and Law qualification requirement to be satisfied through an approved course instead of the examination.

More questions

Can removable sticky tabs be used during the examination?

No. New Mexico's PSI examination rules permit permanent reference tabs but do not allow temporary tabs such as removable sticky notes.
