## The exam

### Is the North Carolina NASCLA 10th Edition an approved examination reference?

Yes. PSI identifies the NASCLA North Carolina General Contractors 10th Edition as an approved reference for the North Carolina Business and Law examination and several general contractor examination classifications.

### How many questions are on the North Carolina Business and Law examination?

The examination contains 40 questions, allows 90 minutes, and requires a passing score of 70%.

### Who administers North Carolina electrical contractor examinations?

PSI Services administers the examinations on behalf of the North Carolina State Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors.

### What is the ISBN of the North Carolina NASCLA 10th Edition?

The ISBN-13 is 978-1-948558-38-9.

### Can I highlight and tab my NASCLA reference book?

Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed with permanent tabs when they meet PSI examination requirements. Removable sticky notes and unauthorized handwritten notes are prohibited.

### Is the North Carolina Electrical NASCLA 14th Edition used for licensing examination preparation?

Yes. The North Carolina State Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors identifies the NASCLA North Carolina Electrical guide as study material for its electrical contractor examinations.

### What is the North Carolina NASCLA Highlighted & Tabbed 10th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, North Carolina General Contractors Edition, 10th Edition, professionally prepared with highlighted content and permanent chapter tabs.

### Are highlighted and tabbed references permitted during the examination?

Yes. The Board permits highlighting and permanent indexing tabs on designated examination references when they meet the applicable requirements. Testing personnel determine whether materials and tabs are acceptable.

### What is the North Carolina Electrical NASCLA Highlighted & Tabbed 14th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, North Carolina Electrical, 14th Edition, professionally prepared with highlighted content and installed chapter tabs.

### What subjects does the NASCLA North Carolina 10th Edition cover?

The guide covers business planning, licensing, insurance, estimating, contracts, scheduling, project management, workplace safety, financial administration, taxation fundamentals, and lien laws.

### Are North Carolina electrical contractor exams open book?

The examinations permit designated technical electrical code references. Questions based on the NASCLA guide, electrical licensing law, and Board rules are closed book.

## Licensing

### What subjects are tested on the North Carolina Business and Law exam?

The examination covers licensing, liens and bonds, subcontractor payment requirements, excavation notification responsibilities, and erosion and sedimentation control.

## More questions

### Can temporary sticky tabs be used during the examination?

No. Temporary tabs and removable sticky notes are not permitted under the applicable PSI reference-material requirements.
