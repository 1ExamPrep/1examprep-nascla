## The exam

### How many questions are on the examination?

The examination contains 115 scored questions and ten additional unscored pretest questions.

### What score is required to pass?

Candidates must answer at least 81 of the 115 scored questions correctly.

### Who administers the NASCLA examination?

PSI administers the examination through NASCLA's accredited examination program.

### Which reference books are allowed in the examination room?

The examination permits the 24 designated technical and construction reference publications listed in the official NASCLA examination reference library.

### Are the reference books provided at the testing center?

No. Candidates must bring their own approved books. PSI supplies the physical diagram and blueprint packet used for applicable questions.

### Does passing the NASCLA examination automatically grant a North Carolina license?

No. Applicants must complete the North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors' application and licensing requirements.

### Does North Carolina accept the NASCLA General Contractor examination?

Yes. The North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors accepts the NASCLA Commercial General Building examination for the Building Contractor classification.

### Can I use the NASCLA examination for licensing in other states?

Yes. Participating jurisdictions accept NASCLA examination results for applicable license classifications, subject to their individual licensing requirements.

### How long is NASCLA examination eligibility valid?

NASCLA examination eligibility is valid for one year from approval, with up to three examination attempts during that period.
