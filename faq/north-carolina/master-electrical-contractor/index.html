No. Passing the examination can satisfy an accepted examination component, but applicants must still meet the experience, application, financial, bonding, and other requirements associated with the selected North Carolina classification.

Licensing

Does North Carolina accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination?

Yes. The North Carolina State Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors accepts the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination for eligible Special Restricted, Limited, Intermediate, and Unlimited electrical contracting license applications.

How much experience is required for a North Carolina Limited license?

The Limited classification requires at least 3,000 hours of qualifying experience, including at least 2,000 hours of primary electrical experience.

How much experience is required for the Intermediate classification?

The Intermediate classification requires at least 5,750 hours of qualifying experience, including at least 5,000 hours of primary electrical experience.

How much experience is required for the Unlimited classification?

The Unlimited classification requires at least 9,000 hours of qualifying experience, including at least 8,000 hours of primary electrical experience. Unlimited applicants must also provide the required statements regarding their ability to supervise and direct electrical installation work.

What are the current project limits for North Carolina Limited and Intermediate licenses?

The Limited classification currently allows a single electrical contracting project valued at no more than $60,000, subject to the applicable voltage limitation. The Intermediate classification currently allows a single electrical contracting project valued at no more than $150,000. Unlimited license holders may engage in electrical contracting projects regardless of project value.

Which North Carolina electrical licenses can I pursue with the NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam?

Applicants who pass the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination may apply for eligible North Carolina Special Restricted, Limited, Intermediate, or Unlimited electrical contracting classifications, subject to the requirements for the selected license.

How much experience is required for a North Carolina Unlimited electrical contractor license?

The Unlimited classification requires at least 9,000 hours of qualifying experience, including at least 8,000 hours of primary electrical experience. Additional experience-verification requirements apply to Unlimited applicants.

Which North Carolina licenses can I apply for with the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician exam?

Applicants who have passed the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination may apply for eligible North Carolina electrical contracting classifications including Limited, Intermediate, Unlimited, and applicable Special Restricted classifications, subject to the requirements for the selected license.

More questions

Does North Carolina require my NASCLA transcript?

Yes. Applicants using the NASCLA examination pathway must have their NASCLA examination transcript electronically transmitted to the North Carolina State Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors as part of the application process.

Does North Carolina require continuing education?

Yes. Qualified individuals listed on Limited, Intermediate, Unlimited, and Special Restricted Single-Family Dwelling licenses must complete eight hours of approved continuing education for renewal under current requirements.
