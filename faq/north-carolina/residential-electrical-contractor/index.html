The exam

How many questions are on the examination?

The examination contains 100 scored questions and ten additional unscored experimental questions.

What score is required to pass?

A minimum of 70 correct answers out of 100 scored questions is required.

Which NEC editions are approved?

The examination permits the 2020 or 2023 National Electrical Code or the corresponding NEC Handbook.

Does passing NASCLA automatically provide an electrical contractor license?

No. Applicants must satisfy the North Carolina Board's requirements before a license is issued.

What North Carolina license can I obtain using this NASCLA examination?

The NASCLA Residential Electrical Contractor examination is accepted for the SP-SFD classification, subject to satisfying North Carolina's licensing requirements.

Can I use this examination to obtain a North Carolina Unlimited Electrical Contractor license?

No. North Carolina accepts the NASCLA Master Electrician/Electrical Contractor examination, rather than the residential examination, for the Unlimited classification.

More questions

Does North Carolina accept the NASCLA Residential Electrical examination?

Yes. North Carolina accepts this examination for the Special Restricted Single-Family Residential Dwelling (SP-SFD) electrical contractor classification.

How long is the examination?

Candidates have 270 minutes, or four hours and 30 minutes.

What is the largest examination category?

Wiring Methods and Practices is the largest category, accounting for 17 questions.

Does North Carolina require an additional state examination?

Yes. Applicants using NASCLA examination results must complete the North Carolina Laws, Rules and Business Practices examination.
