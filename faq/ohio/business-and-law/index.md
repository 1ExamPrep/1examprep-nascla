## The exam

### What is the Ohio NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 4th Edition?

It is a state-specific contractor business, law, and project management reference published by NASCLA for Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board examination preparation.

### Is the Ohio NASCLA 4th Edition an approved examination reference?

Yes. The Ohio NASCLA 4th Edition is the identified reference for the Ohio Contractor Business and Law examination.

### When did the Ohio NASCLA 4th Edition replace the previous edition?

The Ohio examination program began using the 4th Edition exclusively on July 7, 2025.

### What is the ISBN of the Ohio NASCLA 4th Edition?

The ISBN-13 is 978-1-948558-42-6.

### Is the Ohio NASCLA 4th Edition the correct Business and Law examination reference?

Yes. The Ohio examination program has used the NASCLA 4th Edition since July 7, 2025.

### Who administers Ohio contractor licensing examinations?

PSI Services is the examination provider identified for the Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board examination program.

### What is the Ohio NASCLA Highlighted & Tabbed 4th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board, 4th Edition, professionally prepared with highlighted content and permanent chapter tabs.

### Are highlighted and tabbed references permitted during the examination?

Approved references may be highlighted and indexed when they comply with the examination requirements. The testing facility proctor determines whether reference tabs are acceptable.

## More questions

### Are permanent chapter tabs allowed during the examination?

Reference tabs must meet the testing provider's examination requirements. The testing facility proctor determines whether the tabs are acceptable.
