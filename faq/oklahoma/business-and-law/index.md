## The exam

### What is the Oklahoma NASCLA Contractor's Guide, Basic 14th Edition?

It is a contractor business, law, and project management reference published by NASCLA and identified for applicable Oklahoma Business and Law examinations.

### Is the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition an approved Oklahoma examination reference?

Yes. PSI identifies the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition as an examination reference for Oklahoma contractor Business and Law testing.

### What is the ISBN of the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition?

The ISBN-13 is 978-1-948558-39-6.

### Who administers Oklahoma contractor examinations?

PSI Services administers the examinations associated with the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board.

### How many questions are on the Oklahoma Business and Law exam?

The electrical, mechanical, and plumbing Business and Law examinations each contain 50 questions and allow 120 minutes. Passing scores vary by trade.

### What subjects does the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition cover?

The guide covers business planning, licensing, insurance, estimating, contracts, scheduling, project management, workplace safety, financial management, taxation fundamentals, and lien laws.

### Is the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition used for Oklahoma Business and Law exams?

Yes. NASCLA identifies Oklahoma Business and Law among the examinations that use the Basic 14th Edition.

### What is the Oklahoma NASCLA Highlighted & Tabbed Basic 14th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Basic 14th Edition, professionally prepared with highlighted content and installed chapter tabs for organized contractor examination study.

### Are highlighted and tabbed books permitted during the examination?

Approved reference materials may be highlighted and indexed with permanent tabs when they satisfy the testing provider's examination rules.

### Which Oklahoma trades use the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition?

The publication supports Business and Law preparation for applicable Oklahoma electrical, mechanical, and plumbing contractor examinations.

## Licensing

### Does Oklahoma require a statewide general contractor license?

Oklahoma does not currently require a state license solely for general contracting. Regulated trades require appropriate licensing or registration, and local requirements may also apply.

## More questions

### Can the flash cards be used during the examination?

No. The included flash cards are supplemental study materials intended for preparation before examination day, not approved examination-room references.
