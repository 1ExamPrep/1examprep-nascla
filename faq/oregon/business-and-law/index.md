## The exam

### What is the Oregon NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 2nd Edition?

It is the Oregon-specific NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management used as the reference manual for the Oregon Construction Contractors Board licensing examination.

### Is the Oregon NASCLA 2nd Edition an approved examination reference?

Yes. The Oregon Construction Contractors Board identifies the NASCLA Oregon Construction Contractors 2nd Edition as the authorized reference for its contractor examination.

### How many questions are on the Oregon contractor examination?

The examination has 80 scored multiple-choice questions.

### What score is required to pass the Oregon contractor examination?

The passing score is 70%, which means answering at least 56 of the 80 questions correctly.

### What is the ISBN of the Oregon NASCLA 2nd Edition?

The ISBN-13 is 978-1-948558-02-0.

### How soon must I apply for an Oregon CCB license after passing the exam?

Candidates must apply for their CCB license within 24 months of passing the examination.

### Are reference tabs allowed during the Oregon CCB examination?

Oregon permits the authorized reference manual to be tabbed or indexed before examination day. The testing facility proctor makes the final determination about the acceptance and condition of the tabs.

### What score is required to pass?

The passing score is 70%, or 56 correct answers out of 80 scored questions.

### What is the Oregon NASCLA Highlighted & Tabbed 2nd Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Oregon Construction Contractors, 2nd Edition, professionally prepared with highlighted passages and installed chapter tabs for organized examination study.

### Are highlighted and tabbed references permitted during the examination?

Yes. Oregon permits the approved reference manual to be highlighted, underlined, tabbed, or indexed before examination day, subject to examination-center requirements.

### Is the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition used for Oklahoma Business and Law examinations?

Yes. NASCLA identifies Oklahoma Business and Law among the examinations that use the Basic 14th Edition.

### What is the ISBN of the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition?

The ISBN-13 is 978-1-948558-39-6.

### Which Oklahoma trades use the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition?

The publication supports Business and Law preparation for applicable Oklahoma electrical, mechanical, and plumbing contractor examinations.

### Who administers Oklahoma contractor licensing examinations?

PSI Services administers examinations associated with the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board.

## Licensing

### How long is the Oregon contractor examination?

Candidates have three hours to complete the scored examination questions.

### Is pre-license education required in Oregon?

Yes. The standard Oregon CCB licensing pathway requires 16 hours of approved pre-license education and successful completion of the contractor examination. Certain qualification pathways have different training provisions.

### Who needs to complete Oregon's contractor examination?

The Responsible Managing Individual for the contractor business completes the applicable pre-license education and examination requirements under the standard licensing pathway.

### Who must complete the Oregon contractor examination?

Under the standard licensing pathway, the business's Responsible Managing Individual completes the required pre-license education and examination. The RMI must be an owner or employee responsible for managing or supervising construction activities.

## More questions

### Can I highlight, underline, or tab the Oregon NASCLA manual?

Yes. Oregon permits highlighting, underlining, tabbing, indexing, and handwritten notes in the approved reference before the examination. Candidates may not add markings during testing or bring additional loose papers.

### Does the Oregon NASCLA manual come already highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. This product includes the professionally highlighted and tabbed Oregon NASCLA 2nd Edition reference manual.

### How long is the Oregon CCB examination?

Candidates have three hours to complete the examination.

### Can the flash cards be used during the examination?

No. The included flash cards are supplemental study materials intended for preparation before examination day, not approved examination-room references.
