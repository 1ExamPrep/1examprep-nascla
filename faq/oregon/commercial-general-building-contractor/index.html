The exam

Does passing NASCLA eliminate Oregon pre-license training?

Yes. Applicants who have passed the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination are exempt from Oregon’s standard 16-hour pre-license training requirement.

Why are the books highlighted and tabbed?

Highlighting identifies useful material, while permanent tabs help candidates reach important chapters, code sections, definitions, tables, and technical subjects more efficiently.

Should I study with the prepared books before test day?

Yes. Repeated practice helps candidates learn which reference applies to each subject and become familiar with the highlighting and tabbing system.

Are the books provided at the testing center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all permitted references to the examination location.

Does passing NASCLA automatically issue an Oregon contractor license?

No. Applicants must complete the Oregon CCB licensing process and satisfy all applicable RMI, examination, endorsement, business-registration, bond, insurance, and documentation requirements.

Are the books provided at the examination center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all permitted references to the testing location.

More questions

Does Oregon accept the NASCLA examination?

Yes. The Oregon Construction Contractors Board accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors.

Is the Oregon examination still required?

Yes. NASCLA candidates must still pass the Oregon examination covering contractor laws, rules, and business practices.

What is a Responsible Managing Individual?

The Responsible Managing Individual is an owner or employee who manages or supervises the licensed business’s construction activities and satisfies the applicable examination requirements.
