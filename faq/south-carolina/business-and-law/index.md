## The exam

### What is the South Carolina Commercial NASCLA 9th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, South Carolina Commercial Contractors, 9th Edition, a designated reference for the South Carolina Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors examination.

### How many questions are on the examination?

The South Carolina Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors examination contains 50 questions.

### What score is required to pass?

The passing requirement is 35 correct answers out of 50 questions, equivalent to 70%.

### Who administers South Carolina commercial contractor examinations?

PSI Services administers examinations associated with the South Carolina Contractors Licensing Board.

### What is the South Carolina Residential NASCLA 9th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, South Carolina Residential Builders, 9th Edition, designed for construction business and law preparation associated with South Carolina residential builder licensing.

### Is the NASCLA Residential Builders 9th Edition approved for the examination?

Yes. The NASCLA South Carolina Residential Builders 9th Edition is an accepted reference for the Business Management and Law for Residential Builders examination.

### When did South Carolina begin accepting the 9th Edition?

The 9th Edition became an accepted examination reference on October 9, 2026. The examination is scheduled to transition exclusively to the 9th Edition on April 9, 2027.

### How many questions are on the residential Business and Law examination?

The examination contains 50 scored multiple-choice questions.

### Who administers South Carolina residential builder examinations?

PSI Services administers the examinations associated with the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission.

### Can the NASCLA reference be highlighted or tabbed?

Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, or indexed with permanent tabs before testing. Temporary removable tabs and unauthorized handwritten notes are not permitted.

### Is the NASCLA Commercial 9th Edition an approved examination reference?

Yes. The South Carolina Commercial Contractors 9th Edition is the designated reference for the Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors examination.

### Are permanent reference tabs allowed during the examination?

Permanent tabs are permitted when they meet PSI requirements. The testing facility proctor makes the final determination concerning their acceptance.

### Is the South Carolina Residential NASCLA 9th Edition an approved examination reference?

Yes. The NASCLA South Carolina Residential Builders 9th Edition is an approved reference for the Business Management and Law for Residential Builders examination.

### Is the NASCLA Residential Builders 9th Edition an accepted examination reference?

Yes. The NASCLA South Carolina Residential Builders 9th Edition is recognized for the state's Business Management and Law for Residential Builders examination.

### Are highlighted and tabbed books allowed during testing?

Approved references may be highlighted and indexed when they meet the applicable PSI examination requirements. Final acceptance of tabs is determined by the examination proctor.

### How much time is allowed for the examination?

Candidates have 120 minutes to complete the examination.

### Who administers the South Carolina residential contractor examination?

PSI Services administers the examination associated with the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission.

### Are highlighted and tabbed references permitted during testing?

Approved reference materials may be highlighted and indexed when they comply with applicable PSI examination rules. Testing personnel determine whether reference tabs are acceptable.

### How many questions are on the South Carolina commercial Business and Law examination?

The examination contains 50 questions.

### Does the NASCLA reference come already highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. This product includes the professionally highlighted and tabbed South Carolina NASCLA Residential Builders 9th Edition.

## Licensing

### How long is the South Carolina commercial Business and Law exam?

Candidates have 125 minutes to complete the examination.

### Does South Carolina require contractor experience?

Applicants generally document at least two years of qualifying work experience within the preceding five years for the requested commercial classification or subclassification, subject to applicable qualification exceptions.

### How long is the Business Management and Law examination?

Candidates have 120 minutes to complete the examination.

### How long is the South Carolina residential Business and Law examination?

Candidates have 120 minutes to complete the examination.

## More questions

### What is a Primary Qualifying Party?

A Primary Qualifying Party is an individual designated in connection with the contractor business's licensing qualifications. South Carolina requires appropriate qualifying individuals for applicable commercial contractor classifications.

### Does South Carolina require a separate residential builder technical examination?

Yes. Residential builder applicants generally complete a separate technical examination unless they qualify for an approved technical examination waiver.

### Does this publication cover the technical residential builder examination?

The NASCLA manual focuses on construction business, law, and project management. The separate technical examination requires preparation for residential building practices and construction knowledge.

### How long is the examination?

Candidates have 125 minutes to complete the Business Management and Law examination.
