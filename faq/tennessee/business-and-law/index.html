Who administers Tennessee contractor licensing examinations?

Do Tennessee contractor applicants also need a trade examination?

Some Tennessee contractor classifications require a separate technical trade examination in addition to Business and Law. Requirements depend on the requested classification.

Are permanent tabs allowed during the Tennessee contractor exam?

Prov permits permanent tabs in authorized reference books. Movable tabs and Post-it notes are not permitted. All reference materials remain subject to testing-center requirements.
