The exam

Who Administers Tennessee Contractor Exams?

Beginning July 1, 2026, Prov became the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors' provider for new contractor examination scheduling.

How Many Questions Are on the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor Exam?

The NASCLA examination contains 115 questions, allows 330 minutes for completion, and requires at least 81 correct answers for a 70% passing score.

Does Passing NASCLA Automatically Give Me a Tennessee Contractor License?

No. Passing NASCLA satisfies an accepted commercial trade examination requirement for BC-B. Applicants must still complete the Tennessee Business and Law examination and the remaining applicable licensing requirements.

Why choose highlighted and tabbed books for the NASCLA commercial building exam?

Highlighted and tabbed books help candidates study with better organization. Highlighting draws attention to important content, while tabs help candidates move more quickly through large references. This can be useful for an open-book exam with many construction books.

Does passing the NASCLA commercial building exam automatically give me a Tennessee license?

No. Passing the NASCLA commercial building exam does not automatically issue a Tennessee contractor license. Applicants must still complete the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors application process and meet the requirements for the applicable classification.

Are highlighted and tabbed books a substitute for studying?

No. Highlighted and tabbed books are study tools. Candidates should still review the content, practice using the references, complete timed questions, and learn how to apply construction, safety, code, management, and trade concepts.

Does passing the NASCLA commercial exam give me a Tennessee contractor license?

No. Passing the NASCLA commercial exam does not automatically issue a Tennessee contractor license. Contractors must still apply through the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors and meet the requirements for the applicable license classification.

Why are there so many books for the NASCLA commercial building exam?

The exam covers a broad commercial construction scope. Candidates may be tested on codes, safety, sitework, structural systems, project management, concrete, masonry, steel, wood, roofing, accessibility, environmental controls, and contractor business responsibilities. Multiple references are needed because the work of a commercial building contractor touches many construction disciplines.

Why is reference navigation important for this exam?

Because the exam is open book and uses many references, candidates need to know which book to use and where to find information quickly. Strong reference navigation can help improve pacing and reduce confusion during timed testing.

Can this online exam prep guarantee that I will pass?

No. No exam prep course can guarantee a passing score, licensing approval, or exam outcome. This online prep is designed to help candidates study in a more organized way and build confidence with exam topics and references.

Licensing

Does Tennessee Accept the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor Exam?

Yes. Tennessee accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors for the BC-B Commercial Building classification.

Does Tennessee Require a Business and Law Exam?

Yes. New Tennessee contractor license applicants must satisfy the Tennessee Business and Law examination requirement.

How should I study for the Tennessee NASCLA Commercial Building Contractor exam?

Start by organizing your books by subject area. Learn the table of contents and index for each major reference, then practice locating answers under timed conditions. Focus on building code topics, OSHA safety, construction systems, project management, jobsite coordination, concrete, masonry, steel, roofing, excavation, and accessibility.

Do I still need business and law preparation for Tennessee?

Contractors should review Tennessee’s current licensing requirements to determine which business and law requirements apply to their license application. The NASCLA Contractors Guide included in this package supports business, law, and project management study, but state-specific requirements are separate from the commercial trade exam.
