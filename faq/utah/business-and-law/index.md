## The exam

### What is the Utah NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 4th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Utah, 4th Edition, a reference manual covering construction business operations, contractor licensing, law, and project management.

### Is the Utah NASCLA 4th Edition an authorized examination reference?

Yes. The Utah NASCLA 4th Edition is an authorized reference for the Utah Contractor Business and Law examination.

### Who administers the Utah Contractor Business and Law examination?

Prov, Inc. administers the examination for the Utah Division of Professional Licensing.

### What topics does the Utah NASCLA 4th Edition cover?

The guide covers business planning, licensing, insurance, estimating, bidding, construction contracts, scheduling, project management, workplace safety, financial management, taxation, and lien laws.

### What is the ISBN of the Utah NASCLA 4th Edition?

The ISBN-13 is 978-1-948558-15-0.

### Can the Utah NASCLA reference be highlighted or tabbed?

Approved reference books may be highlighted and indexed with permanent tabs according to the examination provider's requirements. Removable tabs are not permitted.

### Are permanent reference tabs allowed during the examination?

Permanent tabs may be used when they meet the testing provider's requirements. Reference materials remain subject to examination-center inspection.

### What is the Utah NASCLA Highlighted & Tabbed 4th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Utah, 4th Edition, professionally highlighted and tabbed for organized Business and Law examination preparation.

### Are highlighted and tabbed books permitted during testing?

Approved references may be highlighted and indexed with permanent tabs when they comply with the testing provider's requirements.

### Can the highlighted and tabbed reference be used during testing?

Authorized reference books may be highlighted and indexed according to the examination provider's requirements. Reference materials remain subject to inspection by testing personnel.

## Licensing

### Which Utah contractor classifications require Business and Law testing?

Utah identifies several classifications requiring the examination, including B100, E100, R100, H100, E200, E201, P200, and P201, subject to applicable qualification alternatives.

### Does Utah require contractor pre-licensure education?

Yes. Utah requires approved pre-licensure education for applicable contractor classifications, with recognized alternatives available for certain qualified applicants.

### Do general contractor applicants need construction experience?

General contractor applicants generally must demonstrate at least two years, or 4,000 hours, of qualifying construction industry experience, unless an accepted alternative applies.
