The exam

Does Passing NASCLA Automatically Give Me a Utah Contractor License?

No. NASCLA is an examination credential rather than a contractor license. Applicants must still satisfy the Utah Division of Professional Licensing requirements applicable to their classification and qualifier.

How many questions are on the Utah Business and Law exam?

The Utah Contractor Business and Law examination contains 60 questions, provides two hours for completion, and requires a 70% passing score.

Why are the books highlighted and tabbed?

Highlighting identifies useful information, while permanent tabs help candidates reach important chapters, definitions, code sections, tables, and technical subjects more efficiently.

How Many Questions Are on the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor Exam?

The examination contains 115 questions. Candidates have 330 minutes to complete the exam and need at least 81 correct answers to achieve the 70% passing score.

Does passing NASCLA automatically issue a Utah contractor license?

No. Applicants must complete the Utah contractor application and satisfy the applicable business, qualifying-individual, experience, classification, insurance, financial-responsibility, and documentation requirements.

Should I study with the prepared books before test day?

Yes. Repeated practice helps candidates learn which reference applies to each subject and become familiar with the highlighting and tabbing system.

Are the books provided at the testing center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all permitted references to the examination location.

Should I use the listed editions?

Yes. Page numbers, code sections, technical requirements, diagrams, tables, and publication organization may differ between editions.

Should I study directly from the listed references?

Yes. Candidates should combine online course review with regular reference practice so the examination library becomes familiar before test day.

Licensing

Does Utah Require the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor Exam?

No. Utah does not currently require a general contractor trade examination. Passing the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors is an accepted way for a general classification qualifier to satisfy Utah's two-year experience requirement.

Does Utah require a Business and Law exam for B100 applicants?

Yes. Initial B100 applicants must document completion of Utah's Business and Law examination requirement.

What experience is required for a Utah general-classification qualifier?

A general-classification qualifier generally needs at least two years, or 4,000 hours, of paid construction-industry experience unless another qualification method recognized by Utah applies.

Is Utah Business and Law still required?

Yes. Applicants must satisfy the applicable Utah Business and Law requirement as part of the contractor licensing process.

More questions

What Utah classification applies to general building work?

The B100 General Building Contractor classification applies to broad general building work within the scope established by Utah contractor licensing laws and rules.

What is the total initial payment?

The package price is $3,334. With the $1,000 refundable book deposit, the total initial payment is $4,334.

Does Utah Participate in the NASCLA Commercial Exam Program?

Yes. The Utah Division of Professional Licensing is a participating agency for the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors.

How Long Should I Allow for My Ultimate Rental Package Order?

Please allow an additional 15 business days for highlighted and tabbed book package orders.

What Is the Utah B-100 Classification?

B-100 General Building is one of Utah's general contractor classifications. Applicants seeking this classification must satisfy the licensing requirements established by the Utah Division of Professional Licensing.

Does Utah accept the NASCLA examination?

Yes. Utah accepts the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination toward the two-year experience requirement associated with applicable contractor licensing.

How may the NASCLA result be used in Utah?

A passing result may be applied toward Utah’s experience-related contractor licensing pathway, subject to the current requirements of the Utah Division of Professional Licensing.

Can a NASCLA transcript be sent electronically to Utah?

Yes. After passing, candidates can send an official transcript through the NASCLA National Examination Database.
