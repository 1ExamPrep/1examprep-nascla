## The exam

### What is the Virginia NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 11th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Virginia, 11th Edition, a reference covering construction business management, contractor licensing, law, and project administration.

### Is the Virginia NASCLA 11th Edition used for contractor examination preparation?

Yes. The Virginia NASCLA 11th Edition is a state-specific reference for preparing for Virginia contractor business examination subjects.

### Are Virginia Class A and Class B contractor business exams open book?

Yes. Virginia's Class A and Class B contractor business examinations use an open-book format with authorized references.

### Who administers Virginia contractor business examinations?

PSI Services administers the contractor business examinations for the Virginia Board for Contractors.

### What is the ISBN of the Virginia NASCLA 11th Edition?

The ISBN-13 is 978-1-948558-45-7.

### Can authorized Virginia contractor references be highlighted or tabbed?

Yes. PSI permits highlighting, underlining, and permanent reference tabs according to its examination rules. Removable tabs and unauthorized writing are prohibited.

### Is the Virginia NASCLA 11th Edition an examination reference?

Yes. The NASCLA Virginia 11th Edition is an authorized reference used for Virginia contractor business examination preparation.

### What is the Virginia NASCLA Highlighted & Tabbed 11th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Virginia, 11th Edition, professionally highlighted and tabbed to support organized examination preparation.

### Are highlighted and tabbed reference books allowed?

PSI permits highlighting, underlining, and permanent tabs in authorized references. Materials must satisfy examination-center requirements.

### Are highlighted and tabbed books permitted during testing?

Approved reference books may be highlighted and indexed with permanent tabs according to PSI examination rules. Materials are subject to testing-center inspection.

## Licensing

### What exams are required for a Virginia Class A contractor license?

The Designated Employee must pass the Virginia, General, and Advanced business examination portions. Applicable trade-related qualification requirements are separate.

### Does a Virginia Class C contractor need the business examination?

No. Virginia does not require the Designated Employee business examinations for Class C contractor licensing. Other applicable qualifications and pre-license education requirements still apply.

### Does Virginia require pre-license education?

Yes. New Virginia contractor license applicants must satisfy an eight-hour Board-approved pre-license education requirement.

### Are permanent tabs allowed during Virginia contractor examinations?

PSI permits permanent tabs on approved reference books. Removable tabs are not allowed, and materials must satisfy examination-center rules.

### What business examinations are required for Virginia Class A licensing?

The Designated Employee must complete the Virginia, General, and Advanced contractor business examination portions.

### Does Virginia Class C licensing require the business examinations?

No. Class C contractor licensing does not require the Designated Employee business examinations.

### Does Class C contractor licensing require the business examinations?

No. Class C contractor licensing does not require the Designated Employee business examinations, although other licensing requirements apply.

## More questions

### What is the difference between a Designated Employee and a Qualified Individual?

A Designated Employee satisfies the business examination requirements for Class A and Class B licensing. A Qualified Individual meets the qualifications associated with a requested contractor classification or specialty.
