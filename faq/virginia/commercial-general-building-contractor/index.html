The exam

Does Passing NASCLA Automatically Give Me a Virginia Contractor License?

No. Passing NASCLA can satisfy the applicable trade examination component, but the contractor business must still complete Virginia's licensing requirements, including the applicable Qualified Individual, Designated Employee, pre-license education, business examination, classification, and application requirements.

How Many Questions Are on the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor Exam?

The examination contains 115 questions. Candidates have 330 minutes to complete the exam and need at least 81 correct answers to achieve the 70% passing score.

Does passing NASCLA automatically issue a Virginia contractor license?

No. Applicants must complete the Virginia application process and satisfy the applicable class, classification, pre-license education, designated employee, qualified individual, experience, financial, and documentation requirements.

Licensing

Does Virginia Accept the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor Exam?

Yes. Virginia participates in the NASCLA Commercial Exam Program, and the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors is accepted for the comparable Virginia Commercial Building classification.

What are Virginia's contractor license classes?

Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses. The class determines the monetary level of projects the contractor may undertake.

What business exams are required for a Virginia Class A contractor?

The Designated Employee for a Class A contractor must complete the Advanced, General, and Virginia examination portions.

What business exams are required for Class B?

The Designated Employee for a Class B contractor must complete the General and Virginia examination portions.

Does Virginia require pre-license education?

Yes. New contractor applicants must complete the Board-approved pre-license education requirement through an eligible member of the applicant organization.

How Much Experience Does a Virginia Qualified Individual Need?

The minimum experience requirement depends on the license class. Current Virginia requirements generally call for one year for Class C, three years for Class B, and five years for Class A in the applicable classification or specialty.

Do Virginia Class A and Class B Contractors Need a Designated Employee?

Yes. Virginia Class A and Class B contractor applicants must designate an eligible individual who satisfies the applicable Designated Employee examination requirements.

What is the Virginia Commercial Building Contractor classification?

It is the classification associated with qualifying commercial, industrial, institutional, governmental, and related building construction work.

More questions

Who Is the Qualified Individual in Virginia?

The Qualified Individual is the person who meets the applicable experience and examination requirements for each classification or specialty listed on the contractor license. The QI must be a full-time employee or member of Responsible Management.

Does Virginia accept the NASCLA examination?

Yes. Virginia accepts the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination as comparable to the Virginia Commercial Building Contractor classification.

What Virginia classification is comparable to the NASCLA examination?

The NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination is comparable to Virginia’s Commercial Building Contractor classification.

What is a designated employee?

The designated employee is the individual responsible for satisfying the applicable Virginia contractor business-examination requirement for a Class A or Class B applicant.
