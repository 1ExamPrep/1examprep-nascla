## The exam

### Are all rental books highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. All books included in the rental package are highlighted and tabbed for organized study and reference navigation.

### Does passing the NASCLA exam automatically issue a Virginia contractor license?

No. A passing NASCLA examination can satisfy an accepted trade-examination component, but the contractor business must still satisfy Virginia's license-class, Qualified Individual, experience, education, examination, financial, and application requirements.

### Why use highlighted and tabbed rental books?

Highlighted and tabbed references provide a more organized way to study large technical resources. Tabs make major sections easier to locate, while highlighting helps important information stand out during repeated study and reference practice.

### Why choose highlighted and tabbed books for an open-book exam?

Highlighted and tabbed references provide a more organized way to navigate large technical books. Tabs can help candidates reach major sections faster, while highlighting makes important material easier to identify during study and reference practice.

### Does passing the NASCLA examination automatically issue a Virginia license?

No. Passing the examination can satisfy an accepted trade-examination component, but candidates must still complete the Virginia licensing process and meet all applicable state requirements.

The minimum passing score for the NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination is 70.

## Licensing

### Does an electrical contractor's qualified individual need a Virginia master electrician license?

Virginia contractor regulations identify a master electrician tradesman license issued by the Board for Contractors as the qualification associated with the Electrical Contractors specialty.

### What are the experience requirements for Virginia contractor Qualified Individuals?

The Qualified Individual must have at least one year of qualifying experience for Class C, at least three years for Class B, and at least five years for Class A.

### Does Virginia require contractor pre-license education?

Yes. Applicants for Virginia Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses must complete eight hours of Board-approved pre-license education according to the requirements associated with the applicable license class.

### Do Class A and Class B applicants have contractor-business exam requirements?

Yes. Class A and Class B businesses must designate an eligible employee who completes the applicable contractor-business examination requirements. Class B includes Virginia and General portions, while Class A also includes an Advanced portion.

### Is a Virginia master electrician license the same as a Virginia electrical contractor license?

No. A master electrician license is an individual tradesman credential. A Virginia contractor license applies to the contracting business. Electrical contractors must meet the contractor licensing requirements and identify the appropriately qualified individual for the electrical specialty.

### What qualification does Virginia require for the Electrical Contractors specialty?

Virginia's current contractor regulations require the qualified individual for the Electrical Contractors specialty to hold a Master Electrician tradesman license issued by the Virginia Board for Contractors.

### What qualification is associated with Virginia's Electrical Contractors specialty?

Virginia regulations identify a Master Electrician tradesman license issued by the Board for Contractors as the qualification associated with the Electrical Contractors specialty.

### Does Virginia accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination?

Yes. The Virginia Board for Contractors is a participating agency in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and accepts the Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination.

### What does a Virginia electrical contractor need for a qualified individual?

For the Virginia Electrical Contractors specialty, the contractor's qualified individual must hold a valid Virginia Master Electrician tradesman license issued through the Board for Contractors.

### How much experience does a Virginia contractor qualified individual need?

Virginia's current contractor application lists one year of qualifying experience for a Class C license, three years for Class B, and five years for Class A.

### Does exam preparation automatically qualify me for a Virginia contractor license?

No. Exam preparation and passing an applicable examination do not automatically issue a Virginia contractor or Master Electrician license. Applicants must complete the licensing process and satisfy all qualifications required by the Virginia Board for Contractors.

## More questions

### How long should I allow for my rental package order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for book & course rental package orders.

### Does Virginia participate in the NASCLA Electrical Examination Program?

Yes. The Virginia Board for Contractors and Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation are listed as participating agencies in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program.

### Can I rely only on the live classes to prepare?

The program is structured around both live virtual training and self-study. Independent review gives candidates additional opportunities to repeat calculations, practice NEC navigation, study safety references, and reinforce difficult technical subjects between live sessions.

### What subjects are most heavily represented on the exam?

General Code Requirements and Wiring and Protection each contain 17 items, Wiring Methods and Materials contains 16 items, and Equipment for General Use contains 13 items. Electrical Theory, Safety, Project Design and Management, Special Occupancies and Conditions, and Communication Systems make up the remaining content.

### How can I qualify for a Virginia master electrician examination?

Virginia's current regulations provide qualification paths that include holding a valid journeyman license in the trade for at least one year or documenting nine years of practical experience in the applicable trade. Applicants must provide the documentation required by the Board.

### Does Virginia require continuing education for master electricians?

Yes. Virginia requires licensed master electricians to complete approved electrical continuing education as a condition of renewal. The required electrical continuing education course is three hours and addresses the applicable National Electrical Code update.
