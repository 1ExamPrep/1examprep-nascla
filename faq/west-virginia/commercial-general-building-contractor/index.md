## The exam

### Does Passing NASCLA Automatically Give Me a West Virginia Contractor License?

No. Passing NASCLA satisfies an accepted trade examination component. Applicants must still pass the West Virginia Business and Law exam and satisfy the remaining licensing requirements for the applicable contractor classification.

### How many questions are on the West Virginia Business and Law exam?

The current West Virginia Business and Law examination contains 50 questions and provides three hours for completion.

### Are All the Books Highlighted and Tabbed?

Yes. The included references are prepared with highlighting and tabs to support organized review and open-book navigation.

### Who Administers West Virginia Contractor Licensing Exams?

ProV serves as the testing agent for West Virginia Contractor Licensing Board examinations.

### How Many Questions Are on the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor Exam?

The NASCLA examination contains 115 questions. Candidates have 330 minutes to complete the exam and must answer at least 81 questions correctly to achieve the 70% passing score.

### How Should I Organize the Books?

Group the references by subject, such as project management, code, concrete, steel, masonry, carpentry, roofing, excavation, safety, accessibility, environmental compliance, and building systems.

### Do I Need to Read Every Book From Cover to Cover?

No. Candidates should focus on understanding the major subjects, learning which book contains each topic, and practicing efficient reference navigation.

### Does Open Book Mean the Exam Is Easy?

No. Open-book testing still requires technical knowledge, careful reading, efficient reference selection, and strong navigation skills.

## Licensing

### Does West Virginia Accept the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor Exam?

Yes. The West Virginia Contractor Licensing Board participates in the NASCLA Commercial Exam Program and accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors through the participating-state process.

### Does West Virginia require a Business and Law exam?

Yes. West Virginia requires contractor applicants to pass the Business and Law examination in addition to applicable trade examinations.

### What does the West Virginia General Building Contractor classification cover?

The classification applies to qualifying building construction involving more than two contractor classifications and to supervision of qualifying construction. It is considered a commercial, business, or industrial contractor classification and applies to commercial and private properties.

### Can a General Building Contractor automatically perform electrical or plumbing work?

No. West Virginia requires a General Building Contractor to obtain the applicable additional classifications before performing regulated electrical, plumbing, HVAC, piping, or general engineering contracting work.
