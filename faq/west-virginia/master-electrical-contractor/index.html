The exam

Are all rental books highlighted and tabbed?

Is passing the NASCLA exam the same as receiving a West Virginia contractor license?

No. A passing NASCLA result can satisfy an accepted trade-examination requirement, but applicants must still complete the West Virginia contractor licensing process and receive approval from the appropriate licensing authority.

Does passing the NASCLA exam automatically give me a West Virginia contractor license?

No. Passing an accepted NASCLA examination can satisfy an applicable trade examination requirement, but applicants must still complete the West Virginia contractor licensing process and meet all additional state requirements.

Why use highlighted and tabbed books for an open-book examination?

Highlighted and tabbed books provide an organized way to study large technical references. Permanent tabs help candidates locate major subjects more efficiently, while highlighting makes important information easier to recognize during study and practice.

Can NASCLA exam books be highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. NASCLA examination rules permit approved references to be highlighted, underlined, and permanently indexed. Temporary tabs such as Post-it notes are not permitted.

The current NASCLA examination information lists a minimum passing score of 70.

Licensing

Does West Virginia accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician exam?

Yes. The West Virginia Contractors Licensing Board, Division of Labor, is a participating agency in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and accepts the Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination.

Does West Virginia require Business and Law?

Yes. West Virginia requires contractors to satisfy the applicable Business and Law examination requirement in addition to the trade examination when required.

Is the Business and Law exam separate from the NASCLA electrical exam?

Yes. The NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination covers the electrical trade component, while West Virginia's Business and Law examination addresses contractor business management and state licensing requirements.

Is the West Virginia contractor license the same as a West Virginia Master Electrician license?

No. Contractor licensing is administered through the West Virginia Division of Labor, while individual electrician licensing is administered by the West Virginia Office of the State Fire Marshal. Candidates should complete the requirements applicable to the credential they need.

Who licenses electrical contractors in West Virginia?

The West Virginia Contractors Licensing Board, Division of Labor, administers contractor licensing. Individual electrician licensing is administered separately through the West Virginia State Fire Marshal's Office.

Is a NASCLA exam result a West Virginia master electrician license?

No. A NASCLA examination result is not a license. Individual West Virginia master electrician licensing is administered separately by the West Virginia State Fire Marshal's Office.

Who licenses individual master electricians in West Virginia?

The West Virginia State Fire Marshal's Office, Regulatory and Licensing Division, administers individual electrician licensing, including master electrician licenses.

Does NASCLA provide a national electrical contractor license?

No. NASCLA provides an accredited examination that participating jurisdictions can accept for their trade-examination requirement. Candidates must still satisfy the licensing requirements of the jurisdiction where they are applying.

What experience is required for a West Virginia master electrician license?

West Virginia's electrician licensing rules require a master electrician applicant to have two years of qualifying journeyman-level electrical experience demonstrating the ability to perform and supervise electrical work. The current state examination application describes this as at least two years or 4,000 hours of qualifying hands-on electrical experience.

What should I study for the NASCLA electrical examination?

Preparation should include the National Electrical Code, electrical theory and calculations, wiring methods, grounding and bonding, circuit protection, electrical equipment, safety, project management, special systems, energy standards, and effective reference navigation.

How long should I allow for my rental package order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for book and courses rental package orders.

What does Live Virtual + Self Study Training mean?

The program combines scheduled live virtual instruction with independent study. Candidates can participate in structured training and continue reviewing references, concepts, calculations, and practice material outside the live sessions.

Can I study only during the live classes?

The training is designed to combine live instruction with self-study. Independent practice is useful for reinforcing calculations, reviewing technical concepts, and becoming more familiar with the electrical references between live sessions.

Can NASCLA examination results be used in other participating states?

NASCLA examination results may be transmitted to participating regulatory agencies through the NASCLA National Examination Database. Each state maintains its own licensing requirements, so a passing NASCLA result is not a national electrical license.
