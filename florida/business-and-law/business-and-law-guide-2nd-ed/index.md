## About this product

The Florida NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Florida Counties 2nd Edition, published under the authority of the Construction Industry Licensing Board (CILB), is an official reference for contractors seeking licensure in Florida counties. Distributed through Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, this NASCLA-accredited manual covers essential topics including contractor licensing requirements, lien laws, financial management, insurance and bonding, project scheduling, and safety compliance. It supports preparation for county-level business and law examinations and aligns with standards set by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR). The guide serves as the foundation for general, building, residential, and specialty contractor exams across Florida jurisdictions.
