About this product

Florida NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 1st Edition — Pre-Printed Tabs

The *Florida NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 1st Edition* provides the foundational reference for the business-and-law segment of various Florida county-level contractor licensing exams. This edition covers contractor business planning, licensing and insurance requirements, estimating and bidding, contract administration, scheduling and project management, job-site safety, environmental responsibilities, financial management, tax rules, and lien laws. The pre-printed tabs version includes ready-applied section markers to improve navigation during open-book examinations, aligning with One Exam Prep’s recommended format for efficient test performance.


Frequently asked questions

Is the NASCLA exam open book?

Yes. You bring the approved reference books, so knowing where things are in the books matters as much as knowing the material.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Real exam questions are confidential. Practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.

When will I get it?

Does buying make me eligible to test?
