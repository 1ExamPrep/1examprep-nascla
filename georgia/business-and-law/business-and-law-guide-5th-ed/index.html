Open Book Test

Georgia Construction Industry Licensing Board examinations have different reference-material rules depending on the trade classification.

Georgia Conditioned Air Contractor examinations are open-book examinations. The NASCLA Contractor's Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Georgia Construction Industry Licensing Board, 5th Edition, appears on the permitted reference list for applicable conditioned air examinations.

For these examinations, candidates may use authorized reference publications to locate relevant information while answering questions.

Open-book testing requires familiarity with the material. Candidates must recognize the topic being tested, locate the appropriate reference section, and apply the information correctly.

For example, a question involving project estimating may require understanding how costs are calculated and organized. Another question may involve contracts, insurance, financial administration, or construction business responsibilities.

Studying the NASCLA guide before examination day helps candidates understand the publication's organization and develop efficient reference navigation habits.

Other Georgia Construction Industry Licensing Board examinations may use different testing formats. Georgia plumbing examination reference materials have included restrictions prohibiting their use during testing, even when the publications are recommended for preparation.

The testing format and authorized references are determined by the specific examination and contractor classification.

For examinations that permit books, effective preparation includes reviewing chapter headings, understanding important business terminology, and practicing how to locate relevant information efficiently.

Licensing Steps

Georgia trade contractor licensing is administered through the Georgia Construction Industry Licensing Board and its respective trade divisions.

The process depends on the type of construction work and the credential the applicant is pursuing.

  1. Identify Your Trade: Determine whether your intended work involves plumbing, conditioned air, low voltage, electrical, or utility contracting.
  2. Select the Appropriate Classification: Identify the license classification associated with the work you plan to perform.
  3. Review Georgia Licensing Requirements: Understand the qualifications, experience, documentation, and examination requirements for your classification.
  4. Prepare Application Information: Organize the employment, experience, and applicant records required by the appropriate trade division.
  5. Complete Application Procedures: Submit the required information through the applicable Georgia licensing process.
  6. Obtain Examination Authorization: Complete the examination eligibility procedures associated with your classification.
  7. Prepare for Trade Testing: Review the technical subjects, codes, and reference materials designated for your examination.
  8. Study Business and Law: Use the Georgia NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 5th Edition, to review construction business and management responsibilities.
  9. Complete Required Examinations: Take the examinations applicable to your trade and license classification.
  10. Complete Licensing Requirements: Satisfy the remaining conditions established by the Georgia Construction Industry Licensing Board.
