Open Book Test

Open-book vs. reference-permitted testing depends on your specific Georgia exam. Georgia’s official reference lists may state whether the listed books can be used during the examination.

  • Conditioned Air Contractors: The official suggested reference list states that only the listed references may be used during the examination and includes a candidate notice about allowed highlighting and permanent tabs.
