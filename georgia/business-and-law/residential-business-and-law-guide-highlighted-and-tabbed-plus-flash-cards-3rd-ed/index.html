Exam Details

The Georgia Business and Law Examination evaluates knowledge of the administrative, financial, and legal responsibilities involved in operating a residential or general contracting business.

The examination is administered by PSI Services for the Georgia State Licensing Board for Residential and General Contractors.

  • Examination: Georgia Business and Law Examination
  • State: Georgia
  • Licensing Authority: Georgia State Licensing Board for Residential and General Contractors
  • Testing Provider: PSI Services
  • Number of Questions: 50
  • Question Format: Multiple choice
  • Time Allowed: 120 minutes
  • Passing Score: 70% (35 correct answers)
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Authorized Reference: NASCLA Contractor's Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Georgia State Licensing Board for Residential and General Contractors, 3rd Edition

Licensing Steps

Georgia residential and general contractor licensing is administered through the Georgia State Licensing Board for Residential and General Contractors.

The licensing process depends on the contractor classification and whether the applicant seeks an individual or qualifying agent credential.

  1. Identify Your Contractor Classification: Determine whether your intended work falls under Residential Basic, Residential Light Commercial, General Contractor, or General Contractor Limited Tier licensing.
  2. Review Georgia Licensing Requirements: Understand the experience, financial, documentation, and examination requirements associated with your classification.
  3. Prepare Your Application: Organize the applicant and business information required by the Board.
  4. Submit the Application: Follow the applicable Georgia contractor licensing procedures.
  5. Obtain Examination Approval: Complete the eligibility process established by the Georgia licensing board.
  6. Prepare for Business and Law: Study the Georgia NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 3rd Edition.
  7. Review With Flash Cards: Reinforce important business and law concepts through repeated study.
  8. Prepare for Trade Testing: Review the technical subjects associated with your contractor classification.
  9. Complete Required Examinations: Take the Georgia Business and Law Examination and applicable trade examination.
  10. Complete Licensing Requirements: Meet the remaining conditions established by the Georgia State Licensing Board.

Business and law preparation complements technical construction knowledge by addressing the responsibilities involved in managing a contracting company.
