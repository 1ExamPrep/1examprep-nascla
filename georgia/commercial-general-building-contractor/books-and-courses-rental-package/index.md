## Open Book Test

The Georgia NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination is an **open-book test**. Candidates may use approved references during the examination, subject to testing-center reference rules.

Open-book testing rewards preparation and efficient navigation. Having access to a reference does not eliminate the need to understand the subject. Searching through unfamiliar books for every question can consume valuable examination time. A stronger strategy is to study the concepts in advance, become familiar with the organization of each major reference, and use the books to confirm specific code provisions, tables, standards, procedures, or technical details when necessary.

The rental books in this package are highlighted and tabbed for exam preparation. Candidates should still spend time working directly with the books before the examination so the location and structure of important information becomes familiar.
