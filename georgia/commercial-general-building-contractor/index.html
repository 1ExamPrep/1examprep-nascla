Georgia Commercial GC questions

How long does book preparation take?

Please allow 15 business days for the highlighted and tabbed package to be prepared before shipment.

Which examinations are required for Georgia general contractors?

General Contractor and General Contractor Limited Tier applicants using the examination pathway must pass the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination and the Georgia Business and Law examination.

Does passing NASCLA automatically issue a Georgia license?

No. Candidates must complete the Georgia application process, pass the required Georgia Business and Law examination, and satisfy the applicable experience, financial, insurance, and licensing requirements.

Why are highlighting and tabs useful?

Highlighting identifies useful material, while permanent tabs help candidates reach major chapters, code sections, tables, and construction subjects more efficiently.

Should I study with the prepared books before test day?

Yes. Repeated practice helps candidates understand the tabbing system, learn which reference applies to each subject, and improve open-book navigation.

Are the books supplied at the examination center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all permitted references to the testing location.
