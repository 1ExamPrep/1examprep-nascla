Exam Details

The Hawaii Business and Law Contractor Examination evaluates knowledge of the legal, financial, and administrative responsibilities involved in operating a contracting business.

The examination is administered by PSI Services LLC for the Hawaii Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs, Professional and Vocational Licensing Division.

New contractor applicants who receive examination approval are required to complete the Business and Law Examination in addition to the applicable trade examination.

Licensing Steps

Hawaii contractor licensing is administered through the Hawaii Contractors License Board, under the Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs.

The licensing process depends on the contractor classification and whether the applicant is seeking an individual or business entity license.

  1. Identify Your Contractor Classification: Determine whether your intended work falls under General Engineering, General Building, or a specialty contractor classification.
  2. Review Hawaii Licensing Requirements: Understand the experience, qualification, documentation, and business requirements associated with your classification.
  3. Prepare Your Application: Gather the information and supporting documents required by the licensing board.
  4. Submit Your Application: Complete the applicable contractor licensing application process.
  5. Obtain Examination Approval: Receive authorization to schedule the required examinations.
  6. Prepare for Business and Law: Study the Hawaii NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 1st Edition.
  7. Prepare for Trade Testing: Review the technical subjects and applicable construction knowledge associated with your classification.
  8. Schedule Your Examination: Follow the PSI examination registration procedures after receiving approval.
  9. Complete Required Testing: Take the Business and Law Examination and the applicable trade examination.
  10. Complete Licensing Requirements: Fulfill the remaining conditions established by the Hawaii Contractors License Board.
