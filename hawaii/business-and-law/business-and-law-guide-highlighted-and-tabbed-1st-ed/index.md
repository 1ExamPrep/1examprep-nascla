## Test Information and Study Materials

### Part 1: Business Planning and Startup

The first major section of the Hawaii NASCLA guide introduces the decisions involved in establishing and organizing a construction company.

### Business Planning

Business planning helps contractors identify company objectives, evaluate available resources, understand operating expenses, and organize professional services. A structured plan supports informed business decisions.

### Business Organization

Business organization involves selecting a company structure and understanding administrative responsibilities. This subject introduces important concepts about managing construction business operations.

### Contractor Licensing

Licensing establishes qualifications for performing regulated construction work. Candidates benefit from understanding the classifications associated with their activities and the responsibilities of licensed contractors.

### Insurance and Risk Management

Construction businesses encounter financial, operational, and contractual risks. Insurance and risk management concepts help contractors recognize potential exposures and important business protection principles.

### Business Management

Effective business management requires planning, communication, accurate records, and responsible decision-making. These skills help contractors coordinate daily activities and maintain organized operations.

### Part 2: Construction Business Operations

The second major section focuses on managing construction projects and coordinating business operations.

### Construction Estimating

Estimating requires evaluating labor, materials, equipment, subcontractor expenses, and project costs. Understanding estimating principles supports accurate budgeting and construction proposals.

### Estimating and Bidding

Estimating and Bidding accounts for ten questions on the Hawaii Business and Law Examination. Contractors must understand how project costs and proposed work affect construction pricing decisions.

### Contract Management

Contract Management is the largest examination category, with twelve questions. Study construction agreements, contract terms, documentation, responsibilities, and communication between project participants.

### Project Scheduling

Scheduling coordinates employees, subcontractors, equipment, material deliveries, and construction activities. Understanding scheduling principles helps contractors organize work and monitor progress.

### Project Management

Project Management accounts for nine examination questions. It combines planning, scheduling, budgeting, supervision, and resource coordination.

### Construction Safety

Construction safety involves workplace hazard awareness, employee responsibilities, and organized jobsite practices. Safety and OSHA concepts represent an important examination subject.

### Environmental Responsibilities

Construction activities may involve environmental considerations involving materials, waste, and site conditions. Understanding these responsibilities supports informed project administration.

### Employee and Subcontractor Relationships

Construction projects require coordination among employees, subcontractors, and supervisors. Clear communication and organized documentation support professional working relationships.

### Part 3: Business Administration

The third major section focuses on financial and administrative responsibilities associated with operating a construction company.

### Financial Management

Financial Management accounts for nine examination questions. Contractors must understand business income, expenses, project costs, and available financial resources.

### Construction Accounting

Accounting provides organized records of financial transactions. Accurate documentation supports project cost management and responsible business administration.

### Cash Flow Management

Cash flow concerns the timing of money received and expenses paid. Understanding payments, payroll, materials, and operating expenses helps contractors manage financial responsibilities.

### Tax Laws

Tax Laws account for five examination questions. Review construction business tax concepts and the importance of maintaining organized financial records.

### Labor Laws

Labor Law accounts for five examination questions. Study employment responsibilities, payroll administration, workers' compensation, and unemployment compensation principles.

### Construction Lien Laws

Construction liens involve certain payment and property rights. Understanding lien principles helps contractors recognize the importance of agreements, payment administration, and documentation.
