## Hawaii Business & Law questions

### What is the Hawaii NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 1st Edition?

It is a Hawaii-specific contractor business, law, and project management reference published by NASCLA for construction professionals and licensing examination preparation.

### How many questions are on the examination?

The examination contains 80 scored questions .

### How much time is allowed?

Candidates have 180 minutes, or 3 hours , to complete the examination.

### What is the passing score?

The minimum passing score is 75% .

### Who administers the Hawaii contractor examinations?

PSI Services LLC administers the contractor examinations for the Hawaii Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs.

### Which agency oversees Hawaii contractor licensing?

The Hawaii Contractors License Board oversees contractor licensing under the Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs.
