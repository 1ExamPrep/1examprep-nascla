### Does Hawaii accept the NASCLA exam?

Hawaii is not on NASCLA's list of states that accept the commercial building exam in place of the state trade exam. The prep listed here is for the Hawaii exams named below; check the Hawaii licensing board for which exams it requires.

### How do I register for the NASCLA exam?
