Quick answers

Does Idaho accept the NASCLA exam?

Idaho is not on NASCLA's list of states that accept the commercial building exam in place of the state trade exam. The prep listed here is for the Idaho exams named below; check the Idaho licensing board for which exams it requires.

Which NASCLA exams can I prepare for in Idaho?

Master Electrical Contractor (7 products from $295.00); Journeyman Electrician (5 products from $295.00). Each has its own page with the books, tabbed sets, rentals and online courses for that exam.

How do I register for the NASCLA exam?

Apply through NASCLA at ned.nascla.org. You must be pre-approved before you can schedule, and then you apply to the Idaho board with your passing record.
