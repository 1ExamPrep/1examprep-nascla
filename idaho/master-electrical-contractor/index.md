## Idaho Master Electrical questions

### Are all rental books highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. All rental books included in this package are highlighted and tabbed for organized study and reference navigation.

### How long should I allow for my rental package order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for book & course rental package orders.

### Does Idaho accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination?

Yes. The Idaho Electrical Board, Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses, participates in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and accepts the Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination.

### Does Idaho also accept the NASCLA Journeyman Electrician examination?

Yes. Idaho accepts the NASCLA Journeyman Electrician examination as part of its participation in the NASCLA electrical program.

### Does an Idaho Master Electrician applicant need to pass the exam before applying?

Yes. Idaho's Master Electrician application requires initial applicants to pass the applicable examination before submitting the license application.

### What happens if I took the NASCLA exam outside Idaho?

Idaho's Master Electrician application requires applicants who took the applicable NASCLA examination outside Idaho to include a copy of their test scores.
