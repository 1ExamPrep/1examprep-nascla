Open Book Test

The Vanderburgh County NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination is open book. Approved references may be brought into the examination room according to Prov's current rules for examination materials.

Prov permits approved references to be highlighted and underlined in pen before testing. Permanent tabs may also be attached to help candidates organize their books. Temporary or movable tabs, including Post-it-style tabs, are not permitted. Candidates may not add handwritten notes or additional photocopied material to the references, and they may not mark the books during the examination.

The highlighted and tabbed format of this rental package is designed around organized open-book preparation. Candidates should still spend time learning how each book is structured. A tab is most useful when the candidate already understands what information is likely to be found in that section.

The 2023 National Electrical Code should receive extensive navigation practice. Candidates should become comfortable using definitions, articles, tables, exceptions, cross-references, and the index. Important study areas include branch circuits, feeders, services, conductor sizing, ampacity, overcurrent protection, grounding and bonding, wiring methods, motors, transformers, electrical equipment, special occupancies, and specialized systems.

Electrical theory should be studied through active problem solving. Candidates should practice voltage, current, resistance, power, circuit relationships, motors, transformers, and other calculations. A consistent approach to calculations can make it easier to identify the known values, determine what must be solved, select the correct formula, and check whether a Code requirement affects the result.

Safety preparation should include OSHA Part 1926 and NFPA 70E. OSHA Part 1926 addresses construction-safety requirements, while NFPA 70E addresses electrical safety in the workplace. Candidates should understand the purpose of both books and practice determining which reference applies to a particular safety question.
