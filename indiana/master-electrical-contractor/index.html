Indiana Master Electrical questions

Are all rental books highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. All books included in this rental package are highlighted and tabbed for organized study and reference navigation.

How long should I allow for my rental package order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for book & course rental package orders.

Does Vanderburgh County, Indiana accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician exam?

Yes. Vanderburgh County is an official participating agency in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and accepts the Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination.

Who administers the Vanderburgh County NASCLA electrical examination?

Prov administers the NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination for Vanderburgh County candidates.

How many questions are on the exam?

The current Vanderburgh County NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination contains 110 total questions, including 10 experimental questions.

How much time is allowed for the NASCLA electrical exam?

Candidates are allowed 270 minutes, or four hours and thirty minutes, to complete the examination.
