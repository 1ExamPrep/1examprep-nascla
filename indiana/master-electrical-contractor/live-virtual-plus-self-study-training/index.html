Exam Details

  • Examination: NASCLA Electrical Contractor (Master Electrician/Unlimited Electrician)
  • Testing Provider: Prov
  • Number of Questions: 110 total questions
  • Experimental Questions: 10
  • Time Allowed: 4.5 hours
  • Master-Level Passing Standard: 75%
  • Exam Type: Open book
  • Testing Location: Designated testing facility in Evansville, Indiana

The current examination outline divides the test among several technical areas. Project Design and Management accounts for 8 questions, Safety for 9 questions, Electrical Theory and Principles for 11 questions, General Code Requirements for 17 questions, Wiring and Protection for 17 questions, Wiring Methods and Materials for 16 questions, General Equipment Use for 13 questions, Special Occupancies, Special Equipment, and Special Conditions for 8 questions, and Communication Systems for 1 question. The examination also includes 10 experimental questions.

The distribution shows why preparation should extend beyond memorizing isolated Code provisions. The examination is administered locally for Vanderburgh County. Open Book Test

The Vanderburgh County NASCLA Electrical Contractor examination is open book. Candidates may use authorized references in accordance with the current Prov Candidate Information Bulletin and test-center requirements.

Prov specifically encourages candidates to become familiar with the examination subject areas and authorized reference materials before testing. Candidates may prepare approved references by highlighting text and placing permanent tabs on important pages. Current reference-material rules allow highlighting, underlining in pen, and permanent tabs.

Handwritten notes are not permitted in the reference books. Moveable tabs, Post-it notes, and additional photocopied materials are not allowed. Candidates may not write, highlight, or otherwise add material to their books during the examination.

The 2023 National Electrical Code should receive substantial navigation practice. Candidates should become familiar with the NEC index, definitions, chapters, articles, tables, exceptions, and cross-references. Important preparation areas include general Code requirements, branch circuits, feeders, services, conductor requirements, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, wiring methods, raceways, cables, motors, transformers, equipment, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, and communication systems.

Electrical theory should be studied through active problem solving. Practice voltage, current, resistance, power, circuits, motors, transformers, and related calculations. Build a consistent approach: determine what the question provides, identify what must be solved, select the correct formula or reference, complete the calculation, and then determine whether an NEC requirement affects the result.

Safety preparation should include both construction-safety material and electrical workplace-safety requirements. Candidates should learn which reference is likely to contain a particular safety topic rather than searching every safety resource for each question.

Live instruction can help candidates understand how these subject areas connect, while self-study provides the repetition necessary to strengthen navigation skills. Timed practice with the actual references can help candidates become more comfortable deciding which book to use and where to begin the search.

Reference Books

The Vanderburgh County Indiana NASCLA Master Electrical Contractor Exam Prep - Live Virtual + Self Study Training is organized around the following electrical and professional references:

  • National Electrical Code, NEC, 2023
    The principal electrical Code reference covering general requirements, wiring and protection, wiring methods, equipment, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, and communication systems.
  • Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 (OSHA)
    A federal construction-safety reference addressing workplace hazards, worker protection, jobsite practices, electrical safety considerations, and other occupational-safety requirements associated with construction work.
  • Delmar's Standard Textbook of Electricity, 6th
    A comprehensive electrical study resource covering electrical theory, circuits, formulas, calculations, motors, transformers, alternating and direct current, components, and practical electrical concepts.
  • A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge
    A project-management reference addressing planning, execution, communication, coordination, monitoring, risk, control, and professional project-management concepts.
  • ANSI/ASHRAE/IES 90.1-2022, Energy Standard for Sites and Buildings Except Low-Rise Residential Buildings - I-P edition
    An energy-efficiency standard covering applicable building and building-system requirements, including electrical and energy-management considerations.
  • NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Basic 14th
    A contractor-focused reference covering business practices, project management, contracts, planning, risk, financial concepts, and professional construction responsibilities.
  • NFPA 70E - Standard for Electrical Safety in the Workplace, 2024
    An electrical workplace-safety standard addressing practices intended to reduce exposure to electrical hazards while working on or around electrical equipment and systems.
  • Ugly's Electrical References
    A compact electrical field reference containing commonly used formulas, calculations, conversions, tables, and practical technical information.
  • Understanding Electrical Theory for NEC Applications
    An electrical-theory reference connecting calculations and fundamental electrical principles with practical National Electrical Code applications.
