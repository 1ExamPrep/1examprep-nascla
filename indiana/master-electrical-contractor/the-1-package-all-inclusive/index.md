The **2023 National Electrical Code** should be a central part of your study routine. Practice locating articles, working with the index, reading tables, following cross-references, and determining where common code subjects are organized. Familiarity with the structure of the NEC can reduce unnecessary searching during an open-book exam.

Electrical theory also deserves focused study. Use Delmar's Standard Textbook of Electricity and Understanding Electrical Theory for NEC Applications to strengthen your understanding of voltage, current, resistance, power, circuits, conductors, loads, and calculations.

Safety is a dedicated exam content area. OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926 supports construction jobsite safety preparation, while NFPA 70E supports electrical workplace safety review. Candidates should understand the distinction between general jobsite safety requirements and electrical hazard-related practices.

Project Design and Management is also specifically tested. Contractors need to understand more than field installation techniques. Planning, coordination, project organization, and management responsibilities are part of the broader knowledge expected of an electrical contractor.

The current exam includes 110 questions in 4.5 hours, so pacing should be part of your preparation. Some questions may be answered from knowledge, while others may require reference searches or calculations. Practicing with your highlighted and tabbed books can help you develop a more consistent method for deciding when to search and where to look.
