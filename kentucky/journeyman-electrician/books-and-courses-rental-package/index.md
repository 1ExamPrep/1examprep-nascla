## Exam Details

The NASCLA Accredited Examination for Journeyman Electricians is part of the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program. NASCLA developed the examination using a national job analysis and input from electrical subject matter experts to represent the technical knowledge and practical skills used by journeyman electricians.

The examination is designed around broad areas of journeyman electrical competency. Candidates should prepare for subjects including:

## State Requirements

Electrician licensing in Kentucky is administered by the **Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction**. Kentucky maintains separate licensing categories for electricians, master electricians, and electrical contractors.

Under Kentucky law, an applicant for an electrician license must generally complete **8,000 hours of verifiable experience in the electrical trade**. The qualifying experience must meet the requirements established by Kentucky law and the Department.

Kentucky permits certain education and training to substitute for a portion of the experience requirement. Qualifying electrical training, education, teaching experience, and applicable supervised military occupational specialty experience can be applied according to the specific provisions established under Kentucky law.

Completion of qualifying electrical training can substitute for part of the required field experience. Kentucky law also recognizes qualifying associate degree or diploma programs in electrical technology or related fields from the Kentucky Community and Technical College System or another accredited college or university.

Applicable supervised military occupational specialty experience can also be used when it meets Kentucky's requirements. Documentation requirements apply to candidates using military experience as part of the licensing pathway.

Applicants should maintain accurate records showing employers, dates of employment, electrical duties, training programs, educational credentials, and other information needed to demonstrate qualifying experience. Kentucky may require verification documents before determining that an applicant meets the licensing standard.

Kentucky law requires an electrician applicant to achieve a passing score on the examination required or accepted by the Department. The NASCLA Journeyman Electrician examination is now one accepted option.

Kentucky electrician licenses are distinct from master electrician and electrical contractor licenses. An individual seeking to perform work or operate a contracting business that requires another license classification must satisfy the separate requirements for that credential.

Kentucky electrician licensing rules also provide for license renewal. Current Kentucky law provides for electrician licenses to renew on a two-year cycle.

The examination pathway and the license application should be treated as connected but separate parts of the process. The examination demonstrates trade knowledge, while Kentucky evaluates the applicant's experience and other qualifications before issuing the license.
