## State Requirements

Electrician licensing in Kentucky is administered by the **Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction**. Kentucky regulates electricians, master electricians, and electrical contractors as separate licensing categories.

An applicant for a Kentucky electrician license generally must provide proof of **8,000 hours of verifiable experience in the electrical trade**. Kentucky recognizes specific education, apprenticeship, teaching, and military pathways that may substitute for portions of the required practical experience.

An applicant may use qualifying apprenticeship training toward the experience requirement. Kentucky recognizes approved electrical training programs and requires documentation showing participation and completion of the applicable instructional hours.

Qualifying electrical education can also reduce the practical-experience requirement. Kentucky's current application guidance recognizes qualifying electrical technology education from the Kentucky Community and Technical College System or another accredited college or university.

Kentucky also recognizes qualifying teaching experience in electrical technology or a related field as a substitute for a portion of the required work experience. Applicable supervised military occupational specialty experience may also be used according to Kentucky law.

Experience submitted for an electrician license must be verifiable. Kentucky may require employer documentation or other approved records demonstrating the applicant's electrical work. The Department may request additional proof when submitted experience appears incomplete or insufficient.

Kentucky currently allows candidates to pass the electrician examination through an approved provider, including the accepted NASCLA option. Candidates using NASCLA must provide the required examination documentation to the Department when completing the licensing process.

The 2023 National Electrical Code is currently Kentucky's adopted NEC edition. Kentucky began mandatory enforcement of the 2023 NEC on January 1, 2025, and all Kentucky electrical examinations transitioned to the 2023 NEC beginning January 1, 2026.

Kentucky electrician licenses are separate from electrical contractor and master electrician licenses. Candidates planning to operate an electrical contracting business should review the additional licensing requirements associated with the appropriate contractor and master electrician credentials.
