## Exam Details

The NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors is part of the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program. The examination is designed for Electrical Contractors, Master Electricians, and Unlimited Electricians and is recognized by participating licensing agencies, including the Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction.

The exam evaluates professional electrical knowledge across multiple technical areas rather than focusing on the National Electrical Code alone. Preparation should include electrical theory, calculations, grounding and bonding, wiring methods, overcurrent protection, electrical equipment, personal and jobsite safety, project design and management, special systems, and other subjects associated with commercial and professional electrical work.

Kentucky requires electrical contractor, master electrician, and electrician applicants to pass an examination administered by an approved examination provider. Kentucky also recognizes equivalent examinations when they meet the state's requirements, and the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination is accepted through Kentucky's participation in the NASCLA electrical program.

A passing electrical examination result is only one part of the licensing process. Kentucky separately evaluates the applicant's experience, license application, business relationship, and other required documentation before issuing an electrical license.

Candidates pursuing a Kentucky Master Electrician license should pay particular attention to both technical knowledge and documentation of their electrical experience. Candidates pursuing an Electrical Contractor license should also understand Kentucky's requirement that the contractor be affiliated with a Kentucky licensed Master Electrician.

## State Requirements

Kentucky electrical licensing is administered by the Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction. The state licenses Electrical Contractors, Master Electricians, and Electricians as separate credentials, each with its own application requirements.

For a Kentucky Electrical Contractor license, the applicant must submit the required contractor application and identify the Master Electrician affiliated with the business. Kentucky law also requires Electrical Contractor applicants to provide proof of compliance with applicable workers' compensation and unemployment insurance laws and maintain the required general liability insurance.

A business entity applying for an Electrical Contractor license must designate an eligible person to take any examination required on behalf of the company. Kentucky regulations allow that designee to be an owner, officer, director, or full-time employee of the applicant business.

For a Kentucky Master Electrician license, applicants must satisfy the experience and training standards established by state law. One recognized pathway involves six years of verifiable electrical experience together with an acceptable electrical training course, or additional qualifying experience. Other experience and education combinations may also qualify under Kentucky law.

Kentucky licensing guidance also recognizes an experience pathway based on eight years of qualifying full-time electrical work. Candidates may qualify through approved combinations of electrical experience, apprenticeship training, and qualifying electrical education depending on their background.

Electrical experience used toward Kentucky licensure must involve work within the scope of the National Electrical Code. Applicants should maintain clear documentation of their employment, apprenticeship, education, or other qualifying experience because the Department may request supporting evidence as part of the licensing process.

Kentucky participates in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and accepts the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination. The NASCLA examination is an accepted testing pathway, but Kentucky remains responsible for determining whether an applicant satisfies the complete licensing requirements.

Electrical contractor licensing and individual Master Electrician licensing are related but distinct. A Kentucky Electrical Contractor applicant must be affiliated with a Kentucky licensed Master Electrician. Candidates planning to operate an electrical contracting business should understand both the individual credential and the contractor-business licensing requirements.
