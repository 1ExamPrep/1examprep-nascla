Kentucky Master Electrical questions

Are all rental books highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. All books included in this rental package are highlighted and tabbed for organized study and reference navigation.

How long should I allow for my Ultimate package order?

Please allow up to 15 business days for ultimate book package orders.

Does Kentucky accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam for master electrician licensing?

Yes. Kentucky's Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction accepts the NASCLA examination as an examination option for master electrician applicants. Applicants must still satisfy Kentucky's separate experience, application, and licensing requirements.

What National Electrical Code edition is Kentucky currently using?

Kentucky currently uses the 2023 National Electrical Code. Beginning January 1, 2026, Kentucky electrical licensing examinations are based on the 2023 NEC.

How much experience is required for a Kentucky Master Electrician license?

Kentucky provides multiple qualifying pathways. One pathway requires eight years of verifiable electrical-trade experience. Other pathways allow qualifying training or education to reduce the amount of experience required when the applicant meets the applicable statutory conditions.

Does Kentucky require my NASCLA transcript?

Yes. Applicants using the NASCLA examination for Kentucky licensure must provide the required passing score information and have their electronic transcript transmitted through the NASCLA National Examination Database.
