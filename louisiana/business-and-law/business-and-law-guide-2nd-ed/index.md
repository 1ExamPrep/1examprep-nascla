## Exam Details

The **State Plumbing Board of Louisiana** administers the Master Plumber and Master Natural Gas Fitter licensing examinations. Unlike the general contractor Business and Law requirement administered through the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors, these examinations are administered under the State Plumbing Board's licensing program.

Both master-level examinations include a business and law section based on the Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters NASCLA Guide, 2nd Edition.

### Louisiana Master Plumber Examination

The Master Plumber examination consists of three written parts:

- **Part I — Plumbing Knowledge:** 80 questions with a 3-hour time limit. This portion is based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code and 2021 International Residential Code.
- **Part II — Sewage Disposal:** 25 questions with a 2-hour time limit. This portion uses Title 51, Public Health – Sanitary Code, Part XIII, Sewage Disposal.
- **Part III — Business and Law:** 40 questions with a 2-hour time limit. This portion is based on the NASCLA Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 2nd Edition.

The NASCLA reference sold on this page is especially relevant to **Part III of the Master Plumber examination**, which addresses business management and legal responsibilities.

### Louisiana Master Natural Gas Fitter Examination

The Master Natural Gas Fitter examination consists of two written parts:

- **Part I — Natural Gas Trade Knowledge:** 80 questions with a 3-hour time limit. This portion uses the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code.
- **Part II — Business and Law:** 40 questions with a 2-hour time limit. This portion is based on the NASCLA Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters Guide, 2nd Edition.

This NASCLA publication serves as the designated business and law reference for **Part II of the Master Natural Gas Fitter examination**.

## Open Book Test

The **Louisiana Master Plumber and Master Natural Gas Fitter examinations are open-book written examinations**.

The State Plumbing Board of Louisiana permits designated reference publications during these examinations, including the **NASCLA Contractor's Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters, 2nd Edition**.

Open-book testing requires both subject knowledge and efficient reference navigation. Candidates must recognize what an examination question is asking, determine the relevant topic, and locate the appropriate information.

For example, a business and law question may involve construction contracts, estimating, licensing responsibilities, project management, financial administration, or workplace safety.

Understanding how these subjects are arranged within the NASCLA guide can make preparation more effective.

The State Plumbing Board permits approved reference books in hardback or softback form, or bound loose-leaf materials. The Board's examination instructions prohibit handwritten notes in reference materials. Highlighting and tabs are permitted, subject to the applicable examination rules.

## Licensing Steps

The Louisiana Master Plumber and Master Natural Gas Fitter licensing processes are administered by the **State Plumbing Board of Louisiana**.

Each credential has its own qualification requirements and examination structure.

### Master Plumber Licensing

1. **Review Qualifications:** Applicants must hold a current Louisiana Journeyman Plumbing License or qualify through the professional engineering license pathway recognized by the Board.
2. **Prepare the Application:** Complete the Master Plumber examination application and organize required supporting documentation.
3. **Submit the Application:** Follow the State Plumbing Board's application procedures.
4. **Receive Examination Approval:** Applications are reviewed before examination scheduling.
5. **Study Technical References:** Prepare using the plumbing code and sewage disposal publications identified for the examination.
6. **Study Business and Law:** Use the Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters NASCLA Guide, 2nd Edition, for the business and law section.
7. **Complete the Examination:** Take the required examination parts administered by the State Plumbing Board.
8. **Complete Licensing Requirements:** Follow the Board's instructions for satisfying remaining licensing conditions.

### Master Natural Gas Fitter Licensing

Applicants for the Master Natural Gas Fitter examination may qualify through a current Louisiana Natural Gas Fitter License, a qualifying Mechanical (Statewide) Work License, or a recognized professional engineering license.

The licensing process includes application review, examination approval, preparation for the natural gas trade section, and study of the NASCLA business and law reference.

Candidates complete the applicable written examination parts and then follow the Board's licensing procedures.

## State Requirements

Louisiana plumbing and natural gas fitting are regulated through the **State Plumbing Board of Louisiana**.

The Board administers licensing classifications for plumbing and natural gas professionals, including journeyman and master-level credentials.

A Louisiana Master Plumber is authorized to perform plumbing work within the scope of the credential and employ registered apprentices and licensed journeyman plumbers.

A Louisiana Master Natural Gas Fitter is qualified to perform regulated natural gas fitting work and employ registered apprentices and licensed natural gas fitters.

Master-level licensing reflects responsibilities that extend beyond technical installation. These professionals must understand how to manage a contracting operation and supervise work appropriately.

## Test Information and Study Materials

### Business Planning and Startup

The first major section of the NASCLA publication introduces the decisions involved in establishing and organizing a plumbing or natural gas contracting business.

### Business Planning

Business planning helps contractors identify company objectives, evaluate resources, anticipate expenses, and organize services. Understanding these principles supports informed decisions about operating a professional contracting company.

### Business Structures

Business structure affects ownership, management, and administrative responsibilities. Studying organizational structures helps candidates recognize important considerations in company operations.

### Contractor Licensing

Licensing establishes requirements for performing regulated plumbing and natural gas fitting work. Candidates benefit from understanding the responsibilities of licensed contractors and the importance of proper credentials.

### Insurance and Risk Management

Contracting businesses encounter financial, operational, and contractual risks. Insurance and risk management concepts help contractors understand potential exposures and business protection responsibilities.

### Construction Business Operations

The second major section covers responsibilities involved in planning and managing construction work.

### Construction Estimating

Estimating involves evaluating labor, materials, equipment, subcontractor costs, and other project expenses. Understanding estimating principles supports organized budgeting and professional construction proposals.

### Contracts and Estimating

Contracts and estimating are especially important for the Louisiana Master Plumber business and law examination. This subject area accounts for 32.5% of the Master Plumber Part III content outline.

Candidates should study how agreements define responsibilities, how estimates are developed, and how proper documentation supports construction business operations.

### Contract Management

Contract management involves understanding agreement terms, maintaining records, communicating expectations, and administering project obligations. These principles are relevant to both plumbing and natural gas contracting businesses.

### Project Scheduling

Scheduling coordinates workers, materials, equipment, subcontractors, and project activities. Understanding scheduling principles helps contractors organize construction operations and manage project progress.

### Project Management

Project management combines planning, budgeting, scheduling, supervision, and communication. These responsibilities help contractors coordinate available resources and maintain organized operations.

### Construction Safety

Construction safety involves recognizing workplace hazards and understanding important jobsite responsibilities. Safety knowledge is relevant to professional plumbing and natural gas fitting operations.

### Environmental Responsibilities

Construction activities can involve environmental considerations associated with materials, waste, and jobsite conditions. Understanding these responsibilities supports informed project administration.

### Employee and Subcontractor Management

Managing workers involves communication, scheduling, supervision, and documentation. Effective coordination supports professional relationships and organized project delivery.

### Customer Relations

Customer relationships depend on clear expectations, accurate project records, and consistent communication. These principles help contractors manage agreements and maintain professional service standards.

### Business Administration

The third major section focuses on financial and administrative responsibilities involved in operating a contracting company.

### Financial Management

Financial management helps contractors understand business income, expenses, project costs, and available resources. Organized financial practices support informed business decisions.

### Construction Accounting

Accounting provides a framework for documenting transactions and monitoring business activities. Accurate records help contractors evaluate expenses, project costs, and financial performance.

### Cash Flow Management

Cash flow concerns the timing of money received and expenses paid. Understanding payroll, materials, customer payments, and operating expenses supports responsible construction business administration.

### Tax Fundamentals

Taxes are an important part of business administration. Understanding general tax concepts and maintaining organized financial records helps contractors manage administrative responsibilities.

### Construction Lien Laws

Construction liens involve certain payment and property rights. Understanding lien principles helps contractors recognize the importance of contracts, payment procedures, and accurate project documentation.
