Licensing Steps

Louisiana contractor licensing is administered through the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors.

The licensing process depends on the type of construction work, the selected classification, and the applicant's business circumstances.

  1. Identify Your Contractor Classification: Determine the type of work your business intends to perform.
  2. Review Louisiana Licensing Requirements: Understand the qualifications and documentation associated with your selected classification.
  3. Prepare Application Information: Organize business, ownership, and qualifying party records.
  4. Submit Your Application: Follow the Board's applicable contractor licensing process.
  5. Complete Qualifying Party Verification: Provide the information required for qualifying party review.
  6. Receive Examination Approval: Obtain instructions for completing applicable education and examination requirements.
  7. Complete Business and Law: Access the LSLBC Contractor Portal and complete the online course and assessment.
  8. Prepare for Trade Testing: Study technical construction subjects associated with your classification when required.
  9. Complete Applicable Trade Examinations: Follow PSI registration and examination procedures.
  10. Fulfill Remaining Licensing Requirements: Complete the conditions associated with your Louisiana contractor application.
