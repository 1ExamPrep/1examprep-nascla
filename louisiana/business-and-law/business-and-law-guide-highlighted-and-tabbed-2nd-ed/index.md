## Exam Details

The State Plumbing Board of Louisiana administers the Louisiana Master Plumber and Master Natural Gas Fitter licensing examinations. Both examinations contain business and law components based on the NASCLA Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters, 2nd Edition.

**Louisiana Master Plumber Examination**

- **Part I:** 80 questions with a three-hour time limit, covering plumbing code knowledge.
- **Part II:** A two-hour examination section covering Title 51, Public Health – Sanitary Code, Part XIII, Sewage Disposal.
- **Part III:** 40 questions with a two-hour time limit, based on the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters, 2nd Edition.

**Louisiana Master Natural Gas Fitter Examination**

- **Part I:** 80 questions with a three-hour time limit, based on the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code.
- **Part II:** 40 questions with a two-hour time limit, based on the NASCLA Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters, 2nd Edition.

**Examination Provider:** State Plumbing Board of Louisiana.

**Examination Locations:** Baton Rouge and Shreveport.

## State Requirements

The State Plumbing Board of Louisiana regulates individuals performing plumbing and natural gas work within the state. Master-level licensing reflects professional qualifications and responsibilities associated with supervising work and managing regulated trade operations.

**Louisiana Master Plumber:** A licensed Master Plumber is qualified to perform installation, repair, and maintenance of new and existing plumbing systems and may employ registered apprentices and licensed journeyman plumbers within the applicable regulatory framework.

**Louisiana Master Natural Gas Fitter:** A licensed Master Natural Gas Fitter is qualified to install, repair, and alter natural gas systems and may employ registered apprentices and licensed natural gas fitters.

Master-level professionals must understand not only trade-specific installation requirements but also the administrative and business obligations associated with professional contracting operations.

The NASCLA guide addresses important subjects such as business organization, licensing, insurance, contracts, estimating, project management, safety, financial responsibilities, and professional relationships.
