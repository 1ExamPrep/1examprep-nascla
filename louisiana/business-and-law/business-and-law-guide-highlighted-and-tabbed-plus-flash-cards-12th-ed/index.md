## Exam Details

The State Plumbing Board of Louisiana administers the licensing examinations for Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters. Both examinations include a business and law component that uses the NASCLA Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 2nd Edition.

Understanding the examination structure allows candidates to organize their study schedule and concentrate on the sections directly supported by this reference book.

**Louisiana Master Plumber Examination:**

- **Part I:** 80 questions with a three-hour time limit, covering plumbing code knowledge.
- **Part II:** 25 questions with a two-hour time limit, covering the applicable sanitary code and sewage disposal material.
- **Part III:** 40 questions with a two-hour time limit, covering business, law, and project management knowledge using the NASCLA Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters, 2nd Edition.

**Louisiana Master Natural Gas Fitter Examination:**

- **Part I:** 80 questions with a three-hour time limit, based on the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code.

**Licensing Agency:** State Plumbing Board of Louisiana.

**Testing Provider:** State Plumbing Board of Louisiana.

## State Requirements

Louisiana's plumbing and natural gas licensing framework helps establish professional qualifications and responsibilities for individuals performing regulated trade work.

A Louisiana Master Plumber is qualified to perform installation, repair, and maintenance of new and existing plumbing systems and may employ registered apprentices and licensed journeyman plumbers within the applicable regulatory framework.

A Louisiana Master Natural Gas Fitter is qualified to install, repair, and alter natural gas systems and employ registered apprentices and licensed natural gas fitters.

The licensing process involves both trade knowledge and an understanding of professional business obligations. Master-level professionals must be familiar with the responsibilities associated with contracting, supervising work, managing projects, and operating within Louisiana's regulatory structure.

The NASCLA Contractors Guide addresses the business knowledge that complements technical plumbing and natural gas expertise.
