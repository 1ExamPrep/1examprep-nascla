## Exam Details

The Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors (LSLBC) oversees contractor licensing and associated examination requirements in Louisiana. Contractors pursuing applicable commercial, residential, or specialty classifications must satisfy the Board's requirements for their particular license categories.

Louisiana's Business and Law requirement is completed through an online course and assessment using the LSLBC Contractor Portal. This process is separate from trade examinations administered through PSI.

The Board identifies the following Business and Law assessment information:

- **Licensing Authority:** Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors

- **Testing Method:** Online through the LSLBC Contractor Portal

- **Estimated Completion Time:** Approximately 30–45 minutes

## Licensing Steps

Louisiana contractor licensing involves several stages, depending on the license classification and business activities involved. Understanding the general process can help construction professionals organize their responsibilities before pursuing licensure.

1. **Determine Your Contractor Classification:** Identify the commercial, residential, specialty, or other applicable contractor category associated with your intended construction work.
2. **Review LSLBC Requirements:** Examine the licensing criteria and documentation requirements associated with the classification you intend to pursue.
3. **Submit the Appropriate Application:** Complete the applicable licensing application through the Board's established procedures and provide required supporting information.
4. **Identify a Qualifying Party:** Where required, designate an eligible qualifying party responsible for satisfying applicable examination or credential requirements.
5. **Complete Business and Law:** Following approval, the qualifying party completes the applicable online Business and Law course and assessment through the Contractor Portal.
6. **Complete Required Trade Examinations:** Qualifying parties pursuing classifications with trade testing requirements complete the applicable examinations through the designated testing process.
7. **Complete Remaining Licensing Requirements:** Satisfy outstanding Board requirements before the contractor license is issued.

The Louisiana NASCLA reference guide supports business education throughout this process. Its coverage of planning, licensing, insurance, contracts, project management, and financial practices can also remain useful after the initial licensing process is complete.
