Exam Details

The State Plumbing Board of Louisiana administers the Master Plumber and Master Natural Gas Fitter examinations. Both licensing examinations include a business and law component based on the NASCLA Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters, 2nd Edition.

Louisiana Master Plumber Examination

  • Part I: 80 questions with a three-hour time limit, addressing plumbing code knowledge.
  • Part II: 25 questions with a two-hour time limit, addressing applicable sanitary code and sewage disposal requirements.
  • Part III: 40 questions with a two-hour time limit, based on the NASCLA Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 2nd Edition.

Louisiana Master Natural Gas Fitter Examination

  • Part I: 80 questions with a three-hour time limit, based on the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code.
  • Part II: 40 questions with a two-hour time limit, based on the NASCLA Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters, 2nd Edition.

Examination Provider: State Plumbing Board of Louisiana.

Examination Locations: Baton Rouge and Shreveport.

The included NASCLA book supports preparation for the business and law examination components. The technical plumbing and natural gas portions use additional designated code references.

Open Book Test

The Louisiana Master Plumber and Master Natural Gas Fitter written examinations are open-book examinations. Candidates may bring approved reference publications into the examination room, subject to the State Plumbing Board of Louisiana's examination requirements.

The NASCLA Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters, 2nd Edition, is an approved reference for the applicable business and law portions.

Open-book testing requires candidates to understand the reference's organization and efficiently locate relevant information during timed examination sessions.

Important Examination Reference Rules:

  • Approved reference books may be highlighted and tabbed.
  • Written notes in reference materials are prohibited.
  • Candidates may not write in reference books during the examination.
  • Loose pages, sticky notes, and notepads are prohibited.
  • Permitted reference books must meet the Board's binding requirements.
  • Electronic devices capable of recording data are prohibited during the examination.

The included flash cards are intended for studying and are not examination-room reference materials. Candidates should use them during preparation and bring only materials permitted by the State Plumbing Board of Louisiana to the examination.

Highlighting and tabs can make navigation easier, but candidates should also understand the underlying material. Regular practice locating information helps develop useful habits for timed open-book testing.

State Requirements

The State Plumbing Board of Louisiana establishes licensing requirements for professionals performing regulated plumbing and natural gas work.

A Louisiana Master Plumber is qualified to perform plumbing installation, repair, and maintenance and may employ licensed journeyman plumbers and registered apprentices within the applicable regulatory framework.

A Louisiana Master Natural Gas Fitter is qualified to install, repair, and alter natural gas systems and employ licensed natural gas fitters and registered apprentices.

Master-level professionals must understand more than technical installation procedures. Business responsibilities, financial management, customer relationships, contracts, safety, and applicable laws are also important parts of operating professionally.

The NASCLA guide addresses these business-related subjects and supports candidates preparing for the designated examination components.

Louisiana license holders must also satisfy applicable continuing education and licensing renewal responsibilities established by the State Plumbing Board.
