## Exam Details

The **State Plumbing Board of Louisiana** administers the Master Plumber and Master Natural Gas Fitter examinations.

Both examinations evaluate technical knowledge associated with the trade and business responsibilities involved in operating a professional contracting company.

The Louisiana NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 2nd Edition, is the designated reference for the business and law sections of both master-level examinations.

### Louisiana Master Plumber Examination

The Louisiana Master Plumber examination consists of three written parts:

- **Part I — Plumbing Knowledge:** 80 questions with a 3-hour time limit. This portion uses the 2021 International Plumbing Code and 2021 International Residential Code.
- **Part II — Sewage Disposal:** 25 questions with a 2-hour time limit. This portion uses Louisiana Title 51, Public Health – Sanitary Code, Part XIII, Sewage Disposal.
- **Part III — Business and Law:** 40 questions with a 2-hour time limit. This portion uses the NASCLA Contractor's Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters, 2nd Edition.

This book and tabs bundle is particularly relevant to **Part III of the Louisiana Master Plumber examination**.

The Business and Law section addresses construction business responsibilities, including estimating, contracts, business organization, financial management, project administration, and safety.

### Louisiana Master Natural Gas Fitter Examination

The Master Natural Gas Fitter examination consists of two written parts:

- **Part I — Natural Gas Trade Knowledge:** 80 questions with a 3-hour time limit, based on the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code.
- **Part II — Business and Law:** 40 questions with a 2-hour time limit, based on the Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters NASCLA Guide, 2nd Edition.

This NASCLA publication serves as the business and law reference for **Part II of the Master Natural Gas Fitter examination**.

## Open Book Test

The **Louisiana Master Plumber and Master Natural Gas Fitter written examinations are open book**.

The State Plumbing Board of Louisiana identifies specific reference publications that candidates may use during testing, including the **NASCLA Contractor's Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Louisiana Master Plumbers and Master Natural Gas Fitters, 2nd Edition**.
