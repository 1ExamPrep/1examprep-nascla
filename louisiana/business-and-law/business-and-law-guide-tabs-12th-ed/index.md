The NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Louisiana General Contractors 12th Edition, is published under the authority of the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors (LSLBC) and is used for the Louisiana General Contractor licensing examination. This NASCLA-accredited reference, distributed by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, covers essential topics including contract administration, lien laws, project management, financial management, and labor laws relevant to Louisiana contractors.
