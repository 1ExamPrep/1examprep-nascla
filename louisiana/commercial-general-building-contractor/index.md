## Louisiana Commercial GC questions

### How long does book preparation take?

Please allow an additional 15 business days for highlighted and tabbed book package orders.

### Does Louisiana accept the NASCLA examination?

Yes. The Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors for the applicable trade-examination requirement.

### Does passing NASCLA automatically issue a Louisiana license?

No. Applicants must complete the Louisiana licensing process and satisfy all applicable business, qualifying-party, financial, insurance, classification, and documentation requirements.

### Why are highlighting and tabs useful?

Highlighting identifies useful material, while permanent tabs help candidates reach important chapters, code sections, tables, and technical subjects more efficiently.

### Should I study with the prepared books before test day?

Yes. Repeated practice helps candidates learn which reference applies to each subject and become familiar with the highlighting and tabbing system.

### Are the books supplied at the examination center?

No. Candidates are responsible for obtaining and bringing all permitted references to the testing location.
