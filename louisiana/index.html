Quick answers

Does Louisiana accept the NASCLA exam?

Yes. Louisiana accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors in place of its state trade exam. You still apply to the Louisiana board and meet its other requirements, which usually include a business and law exam.

Which NASCLA exams can I prepare for in Louisiana?

Commercial General Building Contractor (9 products from $124.99); Master Electrical Contractor (7 products from $295.00); Business and Law (9 products from $19.95). Each has its own page with the books, tabbed sets, rentals and online courses for that exam.

How do I register for the NASCLA exam?

Apply through NASCLA at ned.nascla.org. You must be pre-approved before you can schedule, and then you apply to the Louisiana board with your passing record.
