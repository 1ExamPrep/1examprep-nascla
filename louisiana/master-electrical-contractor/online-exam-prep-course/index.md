## Exam Details

The NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors evaluates the technical and management knowledge associated with electrical contracting. The examination addresses electrical installation practices, electrical codes, workplace safety, project responsibilities, and the application of electrical theory.

- **Exam Name:** NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors
- **Classification:** Electrical Contractor (Master Electrician/Unlimited Electrician)
- **Exam Provider:** PSI
- **Exam Format:** Computer-based examination
- **Number of Scored Questions:** 100
- **Passing Score:** 75 correct answers
- **Time Allowed:** 270 minutes (4 hours and 30 minutes)
- **Exam Type:** Open book
- **Louisiana Licensing Authority:** Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors (LSLBC)

The examination covers nine major technical and management categories. Understanding the distribution of questions helps candidates develop an effective study plan and dedicate appropriate attention to heavily tested subjects.

- **Wiring and Protection:** 17 questions
- **Wiring Methods and Materials:** 16 questions
- **Equipment for General Use:** 13 questions

General code requirements, wiring and protection, and wiring methods and materials represent a significant portion of the examination. Candidates should also review electrical theory, equipment requirements, electrical safety practices, and project management concepts.

## Licensing Steps

Electrical contractors pursuing Louisiana licensing through the NASCLA examination pathway must complete the appropriate examination and licensing processes. The NASCLA examination provides an alternative route for meeting applicable trade examination requirements, but it does not independently grant a contractor license.

1. **Identify the Appropriate Classification:** Review Louisiana's Electrical contractor classification and determine the licensing category associated with your intended contracting activities.
2. **Prepare for the NASCLA Examination:** Study electrical code requirements, wiring systems, calculations, electrical theory, construction safety, and project management.
3. **Register for the Examination:** Complete the applicable NASCLA examination registration process and obtain the necessary examination authorization.
4. **Schedule Through PSI:** Arrange the examination appointment through the designated testing provider.
5. **Complete the Electrical Contractor Examination:** Take the examination and achieve the required passing score.
6. **Submit Your NASCLA Transcript:** Use NASCLA's National Examination Database to send the passing examination transcript directly to the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors.
7. **Complete Louisiana Licensing Requirements:** Submit the appropriate state contractor application and satisfy the remaining licensing and qualification requirements.

Louisiana licensing requirements are separate from examination registration. Applicants must satisfy the board's requirements for their requested contractor classification, including applicable business, financial, administrative, and qualifying-party requirements.

## State Requirements

The Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors regulates contractor licensing for the state's Electrical classification. This classification includes qualifying activities involving electrical wiring, equipment, fixtures, and related electrical systems.

Louisiana's Electrical major classification applies to covered electrical work exceeding $10,000 in value. Contractors performing work within this classification must comply with the applicable state licensing requirements.

Louisiana recognizes passing results from the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors toward the applicable Electrical trade examination requirement. Applicants requesting this credit must arrange for their NASCLA transcript to be transmitted directly to the licensing board through the NASCLA National Examination Database.

In addition to trade examination credit, the licensing process involves the appropriate contractor application, qualifying-party information, and other documentation required by the board.

Electrical professionals pursuing licensing should understand the distinction between passing a trade examination and obtaining a contractor license. The NASCLA examination evaluates technical competency, while Louisiana determines whether the applicant satisfies the requirements to conduct licensed contracting activities.

The NASCLA examination may also be useful for professionals interested in contractor licensing in participating jurisdictions beyond Louisiana. However, each jurisdiction maintains its own licensing application and approval requirements.
