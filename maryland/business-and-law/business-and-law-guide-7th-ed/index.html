Exam Details

The Maryland Home Improvement Contractor examination evaluates knowledge of the laws and regulations associated with operating a home improvement business. The examination is administered by PSI Services on behalf of the Maryland Home Improvement Commission.

Maryland Home Improvement Contractor Exam:

  • Exam Name: Maryland Home Improvement Contractor Examination
  • Licensing Authority: Maryland Home Improvement Commission (MHIC)
  • Testing Provider: PSI Services
  • Exam Format: Computer-based examination
  • Number of Questions: 55
  • Time Allowed: 150 minutes
  • Passing Score: 39 correct answers out of 55
  • Exam Type: Open book
  • Approved Contractor Reference: NASCLA Contractor's Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Maryland Home Improvement Commission, 7th Edition

The contractor examination includes the following subject areas:

  • Home Improvement Law: 41 questions
  • Door-to-Door Sales Act: 4 questions
  • Labor Laws: 3 questions
  • Safety Regulations: 4 questions
  • Estimating: 3 questions

Home Improvement Law represents the largest portion of the examination. Candidates should give particular attention to the regulatory requirements governing home improvement work, contractual responsibilities, and interactions with homeowners.

The exam also evaluates knowledge of other important business responsibilities, including employment-related laws, workplace safety, estimating practices, and applicable consumer sales protections.

Open Book Test

The Maryland Home Improvement Contractor licensing examination is an open-book test. Candidates are permitted to bring designated reference materials into the PSI examination center, including the NASCLA Contractor's Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Maryland Home Improvement Commission, 7th Edition.

Open-book testing does not eliminate the need to study. Candidates must understand the material well enough to identify the subject of a question, determine where the relevant information appears, and locate answers efficiently within the examination time limit.

The following examination reference rules apply:

  • Reference books may be highlighted and underlined.
  • Books may contain acceptable permanent indexing tabs.
  • Reference books must otherwise remain unmarked and may not contain handwritten notes.
  • Loose papers and additional attached papers are prohibited.
  • Removable tabs that can be detached without damaging the page are not permitted.
  • Post-it flags and removable index markers are not acceptable examination tabs.
  • Books published by other testing companies are not permitted as examination references.

PSI examination personnel determine whether a candidate's reference materials and tabs meet the applicable testing rules.

For effective preparation, review the manual's table of contents, become familiar with its four sections, and practice locating subjects without repeatedly reading entire chapters. An organized approach helps candidates make better use of the available examination time.

Licensing Steps

Individuals planning to obtain a Maryland Home Improvement Contractor license must complete the examination and licensing procedures established by the Maryland Home Improvement Commission.

Step 1: Review Maryland Licensing Requirements

Begin by reviewing the eligibility requirements for a Maryland Home Improvement Contractor license. Applicants must demonstrate qualifying trade experience or applicable educational training and satisfy the Commission's other licensing requirements.

Step 2: Prepare for the Contractor Examination

Study the NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 7th Edition, and become familiar with Maryland home improvement laws, business responsibilities, safety regulations, estimating, and related subjects.

Step 3: Apply for Examination Approval

Complete the PSI examination application process and provide the documentation required for examination eligibility. PSI approval is required before scheduling the licensing examination.

Step 4: Schedule and Take the Examination

After approval, schedule your examination through PSI. Prepare your approved reference materials, review testing center requirements, and arrive with the identification required by the testing provider.

Step 5: Submit Your License Application

After passing the examination, complete the Maryland Home Improvement Commission licensing application. Provide the required business, financial, experience, insurance, and supporting documentation applicable to your circumstances.

Step 6: Complete the Licensing Process

Follow the Commission's instructions until your application has been processed and the appropriate home improvement contractor license has been issued.

Passing examination results remain valid for two years. Applicants must complete their licensing application within that period to avoid having to retake the examination.

State Requirements

The Maryland Home Improvement Commission regulates home improvement contractors performing covered work on residential properties in Maryland.

Home improvement work generally includes the alteration, remodeling, repair, or replacement of a building or part of a building used as a residence. The law also covers specified work involving individual condominium units.

Applicants for a Maryland Home Improvement Contractor license must satisfy several established requirements.

  • Experience: Applicants generally need at least two years of qualifying experience in home improvement, construction, or related work, or qualifying educational training.
  • Examination: Applicants must successfully complete the applicable licensing examination.
  • Financial Solvency: Contractor applicants must demonstrate financial responsibility according to the Commission's requirements. Appropriate surety bond or indemnitor arrangements may apply when financial solvency guidelines are not met.
  • Liability Insurance: Contractors must maintain the general liability insurance required under Maryland law and provide appropriate proof of coverage.
  • Business Documentation: Applicable corporation records, trade name registrations, and other organizational documents must be supplied when required.
  • Application Approval: The Maryland Home Improvement Commission reviews licensing applications and supporting documentation before issuing licenses.

A Maryland Home Improvement Contractor license is issued for a two-year licensing period. License holders must follow applicable requirements for maintaining their license and conducting regulated home improvement work.

Understanding these requirements is important not only for examination preparation but also for managing a professional and compliant home improvement business.
