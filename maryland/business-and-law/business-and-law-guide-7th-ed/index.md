## About this product

The Maryland NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 7th Edition, approved by the Maryland Home Improvement Commission (MHIC), serves as the official reference for the Maryland Home Improvement Contractor licensing exam. Published by NASCLA and used by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, this manual provides essential content covering Maryland state laws, business organization, contract regulations, lien laws, tax requirements, and project management practices specific to home improvement work. It aligns with NASCLA’s standardized contractor exam structure and emphasizes compliance, safety, and financial management principles crucial for passing the Maryland contractor licensing examination.

NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Maryland Home Improvement Commission 6th Edition is organized into 4 sections.

Part 1 focuses on preparing you for the licensing exam. This section will help you understand licensing requirements, estimating, employee management, lien law and safety and environmental responsibilities.

Part 2 provides completion of the home improvement licensing application including fees, instructions, and sample forms.

Part 3 provides valuable information on choosing a business structure, basic management skills, insurance, and business marketing.

Part 4 presents fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers business planning, scheduling, project management, and building good relationships with subcontractors, financial management, and tax basics.
