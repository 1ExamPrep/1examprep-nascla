## About this product

The Maryland NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, MD Home Improvement Commission 7th Edition; Highlighted & Tabbed, available through Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, is the officially approved reference manual for contractor licensing examinations administered by PSI for the Maryland Home Improvement Commission (MHIC). Developed by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA), this comprehensive guide covers Maryland-specific requirements for business organization, contract law, lien regulations, insurance, financial management, and project supervision within the home improvement industry. The Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep edition is pre-highlighted and tabbed in alignment with PSI’s open-book testing format, enabling efficient reference during the exam. This resource provides candidates with the foundational business and legal knowledge required to meet MHIC standards and obtain licensure as a Maryland Home Improvement Contractor while ensuring compliance with state construction and consumer protection laws.

NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Maryland Home Improvement Commission 7th Edition is organized into 4 sections.

Part 1 focuses on preparing you for the licensing exam. This section will help you understand licensing requirements, estimating, employee management, lien law and safety and environmental responsibilities.

Part 2 provides completion of the home improvement licensing application including fees, instructions, and sample forms.

Part 3 provides valuable information on choosing a business structure, basic management skills, insurance, and business marketing.

Part 4 presents fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers business planning, scheduling, project management, and building good relationships with subcontractors, financial management, and tax basis.
