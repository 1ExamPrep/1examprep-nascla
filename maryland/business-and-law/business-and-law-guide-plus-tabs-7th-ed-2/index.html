Exam Details

The Maryland Home Improvement Contractor examination measures knowledge of laws and regulations associated with operating a home improvement business. The examination is administered by PSI Services for the Maryland Home Improvement Commission.

  • Examination: Maryland Home Improvement Contractor (MHIC)
  • Licensing Agency: Maryland Home Improvement Commission
  • Testing Provider: PSI Services
  • Examination Format: Computer-based
  • Number of Questions: 55
  • Passing Requirement: At least 70% correct responses
  • Examination Type: Open book
  • Designated Reference: NASCLA Contractor's Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Maryland Home Improvement Commission, 7th Edition

The contractor examination covers five major subject areas:

  • Home Improvement Law: 41 questions
  • Door-to-Door Sales Act: 4 questions
  • Labor Laws: 3 questions
  • Safety Regulations: 4 questions
  • Estimating: 3 questions

Home Improvement Law accounts for the largest portion of the examination. Candidates should dedicate substantial study time to understanding licensing responsibilities, consumer protections, home improvement contracts, and other Maryland regulatory subjects.

Knowledge of estimating, employment responsibilities, workplace safety, and consumer sales protections is also important for the examination and professional contractor operations.

Licensing Steps

Becoming a licensed Maryland Home Improvement Contractor involves satisfying the examination and application requirements established by the Maryland Home Improvement Commission.

Step 1: Review Contractor Licensing Qualifications

Review the Commission's experience, education, business, insurance, and financial requirements. Applicants generally need at least two years of qualifying trade experience or comparable educational training accepted by the Commission.

Step 2: Prepare for the MHIC Examination

Study the NASCLA Maryland Home Improvement Commission, 7th Edition. Use the matching tabs to learn the book's organization and practice locating information relating to licensing, law, estimating, contracts, safety, and business administration.

Step 3: Register for the Examination

Follow the PSI examination registration process and complete the required application information. Schedule your examination according to the testing provider's instructions.

Step 4: Complete the Examination

Attend your scheduled testing appointment with the required identification and permitted reference materials. Follow all PSI testing center procedures and instructions.

Step 5: Apply for the Contractor License

After successfully passing the examination, complete the Maryland Home Improvement Commission license application and provide the required supporting documentation.

Step 6: Complete the Commission's Review Process

Submit applicable financial records, proof of insurance, business documentation, and other required materials. The Commission evaluates applications before issuing licenses.

Passing examination results remain valid for two years. Applicants must apply for licensure within that period.

State Requirements

The Maryland Home Improvement Commission regulates contractors performing covered residential home improvement work in Maryland.

Home improvement work generally involves alteration, remodeling, repair, or replacement of residential buildings or designated portions of residential property.

Applicants for a Maryland Home Improvement Contractor license must meet established professional and administrative requirements.

  • Trade Experience: Generally at least two years of qualifying construction or home improvement experience, or accepted comparable educational training.
  • Licensing Examination: Successful completion of the required contractor examination.
  • Financial Responsibility: Documentation demonstrating financial solvency under the Commission's standards.
  • Liability Insurance: Proof of the required general liability insurance coverage.
  • Business Registration: Appropriate business formation or trade name documentation when applicable.
  • Application Documentation: Submission of financial information, supporting records, and required application materials.
  • Licensing Approval: Completion of the Commission's review and approval process.

Maryland Home Improvement Contractor licenses are issued for two-year licensing periods. Contractors must also comply with applicable renewal and professional conduct requirements.
