About this product

The Maryland NASCLA Home Improvement Salesperson Reference Guide, 6th Edition, published under the Maryland Home Improvement Commission (MHIC) and distributed through Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, serves as the official study reference for individuals preparing for the Maryland Home Improvement Salesperson licensing examination. This edition aligns with NASCLA standards and includes regulatory content covering state laws, contract requirements, advertising rules, sales practices, and homeowner protection guidelines essential for compliance with MHIC regulations. The manual functions as a foundational text for exam candidates, providing the statutes, administrative codes, and trade practices necessary to qualify for and maintain a Maryland Home Improvement Salesperson license.


NASCLA Home Improvement Salesperson Reference Guide, Maryland Home Improvement Commission 5th Edition is organized into two sections.

Part 1 focuses on preparing you for the licensing exam. This section will help you understand licensing requirements, estimating, and contract management.

Part 2 covers completion of the home improvement licensing application including fees, instructions, and sample forms.

Frequently asked questions

Is the NASCLA exam open book?

Yes. You bring the approved reference books, so knowing where things are in the books matters as much as knowing the material.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Real exam questions are confidential. Practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.
