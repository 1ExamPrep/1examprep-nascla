### Does Maryland accept the NASCLA exam?

Maryland is not on NASCLA's list of states that accept the commercial building exam in place of the state trade exam. The prep listed here is for the Maryland exams named below; check the Maryland licensing board for which exams it requires.

### How do I register for the NASCLA exam?
