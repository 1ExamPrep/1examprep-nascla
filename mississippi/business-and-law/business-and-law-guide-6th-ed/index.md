## About this product

The *Mississippi NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 6th Edition* available from Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep is the official reference manual for the Mississippi State Board of Contractors (MSBOC) licensing examinations. Published by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA), this guide provides comprehensive coverage of Mississippi-specific business laws, licensing requirements, lien laws, financial management, project organization, safety, and labor regulations. Required for both commercial and residential contractor exams administered by PSI, the 6th Edition aligns with MSBOC’s current testing standards and serves as an essential resource for contractors preparing to obtain or renew a Mississippi contractor’s license while maintaining compliance with state construction laws and administrative codes.

The Mississippi - NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 6th Edition is organized into three sections.

Part 1 Focuses on planning and starting your business. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

Part 2 Covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors and customers.

Part 3 Provides valuable information to assist you in running the administrative function of your business. Financial management, tax basics, and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and failure proper attention. can cause serious problems.
