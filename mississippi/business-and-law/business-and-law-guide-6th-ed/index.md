## Licensing Steps

Individuals seeking a Mississippi contractor license must follow the examination and licensing process established by the Mississippi State Board of Contractors.

**Step 1: Determine Your Contractor Classification**

Identify the appropriate residential, commercial, or specialty classification for the type of construction work you intend to perform. Licensing classifications determine which additional trade examination requirements apply.

**Step 2: Prepare Your Licensing Application**

Complete the applicable Mississippi State Board of Contractors application and gather required business, qualifying-party, experience, insurance, and financial documentation.

**Step 3: Submit Your Application to MSBOC**

Submit your completed licensing application to the Board. MSBOC reviews the application, identifies any missing requirements, and determines examination eligibility.

**Step 4: Receive PSI Examination Authorization**

After the Board confirms examination eligibility and provides the necessary information to PSI, the testing provider sends scheduling instructions to the applicant.

**Step 5: Prepare for the Required Examinations**

Study the Mississippi NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 6th Edition, for the Law and Business Management examination. Prepare separately for any required trade examination using its designated reference materials.

**Step 6: Complete Your Examinations**

Attend your scheduled PSI examination appointment with the required identification and permitted materials. Complete all examinations required for your requested classification.

**Step 7: Complete the Licensing Review**

After examination results and all required documentation are received, the completed application is submitted for Board review. Approved applicants receive their contractor license according to the Board's issuance procedures.

Following the correct application sequence is important because Mississippi applicants must receive examination authorization before scheduling with PSI.

## State Requirements

The Mississippi State Board of Contractors regulates commercial and residential contractors who bid on or perform covered construction work within the state.

Licensing requirements depend on the contractor's classification, business structure, and type of construction activity.

**Important Mississippi Contractor Licensing Requirements:**

- **Licensing Application:** Applicants must complete the appropriate commercial or residential contractor application.
- **Qualifying Party:** The applicant must identify the individual responsible for meeting applicable examination and qualification requirements.
- **Law and Business Examination:** Contractor license applicants are required to complete the Mississippi Law and Business Management examination.
- **Trade Examination:** An additional examination may be required depending on the requested classification.
- **Financial Documentation:** Commercial applicants must satisfy the Board's applicable financial statement and net-worth requirements.
- **Business Information:** Applicable business registration, tax identification, and organizational documentation must be provided.
- **Insurance:** Active license holders must maintain the general liability insurance required by the Board.
- **Application Approval:** Licenses are issued following completion of examination, documentation, and Board approval requirements.

Mississippi contractor licenses are valid for one year from issuance and must be renewed annually.
