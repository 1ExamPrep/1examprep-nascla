Licensing Steps

Obtaining a Mississippi contractor license involves satisfying the applicable application, examination, and approval requirements established by the Mississippi State Board of Contractors.

Step 1: Identify Your Contractor Classification

Determine whether you are pursuing residential, commercial, or specialty contractor licensing. Different classifications may involve different technical examination and licensing requirements.

Step 2: Review Licensing Qualifications

Review the applicable requirements for your selected classification, including qualifying-party information, business documentation, financial responsibilities, and insurance.

Step 3: Complete the Contractor License Application

Prepare the appropriate application and gather the supporting documentation required by the Mississippi State Board of Contractors.

Step 4: Obtain Examination Authorization

Submit the completed licensing application for review. After determining eligibility, the Board provides the necessary examination authorization information to PSI.

Step 5: Prepare for the Examination

Study the Mississippi NASCLA Contractors Guide, 6th Edition, for the business and law examination. Use the highlighted information and installed chapter tabs to practice locating important material.

Step 6: Complete Required Examinations

Schedule and attend the required PSI examinations. Bring the appropriate identification and approved reference materials, and follow the testing center's instructions.

Step 7: Complete the Licensing Process

After satisfying the examination requirements, complete any remaining documentation and Board review procedures before the applicable contractor license is issued.

Understanding the licensing sequence helps applicants organize their preparation and complete the required administrative steps.
