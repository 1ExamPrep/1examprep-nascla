Licensing Steps

Obtaining a Mississippi contractor license involves completing the applicable application, examination, and approval requirements established by the Mississippi State Board of Contractors.

Step 1: Determine Your Contractor Classification

Identify the appropriate residential, commercial, or specialty classification for the construction work you intend to perform. Different classifications may involve different technical examination requirements.

Step 2: Review Licensing Qualifications

Review the Board's requirements for your selected classification, including qualifying-party information, business documentation, financial responsibilities, and insurance requirements.

Step 3: Complete the Contractor License Application

Prepare the appropriate licensing application and gather the supporting documentation required by the Mississippi State Board of Contractors.

Step 4: Obtain Examination Authorization

Submit the completed application for review. After determining examination eligibility, the Board provides the necessary authorization information to PSI.

Step 5: Prepare for the Required Examinations

Study the highlighted and tabbed Mississippi NASCLA 6th Edition for the Law and Business Management examination. Use the flash cards for additional concept review and prepare separately for any required technical trade examination.

Step 6: Complete Your Examinations

Schedule and attend the PSI examinations. Bring the required identification and permitted reference materials, and follow testing center procedures.

Step 7: Complete the Licensing Process

After satisfying examination requirements, complete any remaining documentation and Board review procedures before the applicable contractor license is issued.
