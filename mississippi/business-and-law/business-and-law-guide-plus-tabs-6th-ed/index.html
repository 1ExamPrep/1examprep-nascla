Licensing Steps

Obtaining a Mississippi contractor license involves completing the applicable application, examination, and approval requirements established by the Mississippi State Board of Contractors.

Identify the residential, commercial, or specialty contractor classification corresponding to the work you intend to perform. Different classifications may involve different technical examination requirements.

Step 2: Review Licensing Qualifications

Review the Mississippi State Board of Contractors requirements for your selected classification, including qualifying-party information, business documentation, financial responsibilities, and applicable insurance requirements.

Step 4: Obtain Examination Authorization

Step 7: Complete the Licensing Process

After satisfying the examination requirements, complete any remaining documentation and Board review requirements before the applicable contractor license is issued.
