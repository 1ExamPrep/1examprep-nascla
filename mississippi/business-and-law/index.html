Mississippi Business & Law questions

Is the Mississippi NASCLA 6th Edition approved for the contractor licensing exam?

Yes. The NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Mississippi, 6th Edition, is an approved reference for the Mississippi Law and Business Management examination administered by PSI Services.

How many questions are on the Mississippi Law and Business Management examination?

The Mississippi Law and Business Management examination contains 50 questions and has a two-hour time limit.

Who administers the Mississippi contractor examination?

PSI Services administers the contractor examinations for the Mississippi State Board of Contractors. Applicants must receive the required examination authorization before scheduling.

Do all Mississippi contractor applicants need to take the business and law exam?

The Mississippi State Board of Contractors requires license applicants to complete the Law and Business Management examination. Additional trade examinations may apply depending on the requested classification.

How long is a Mississippi contractor license valid?

Mississippi contractor licenses are valid for one year from the date of issuance and must be renewed annually.

Is the Mississippi NASCLA 6th Edition an approved examination reference?

Yes. The Mississippi NASCLA 6th Edition is a designated reference for the Mississippi Law and Business Management examination administered by PSI Services.
