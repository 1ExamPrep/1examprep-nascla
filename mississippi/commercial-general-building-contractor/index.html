Mississippi Commercial GC questions

How long does book preparation take?

Please allow an additional 15 business days for highlighted and tabbed book package orders.

Does Mississippi accept the NASCLA examination?

Yes. Applicants seeking the Mississippi Building Construction major classification may choose the NASCLA accredited examination instead of the Mississippi Building Construction trade examination.

Is Mississippi Law and Business Management still required?

Yes. Mississippi contractor applicants must satisfy the state Law and Business Management examination requirement even when using the NASCLA examination for the trade portion.

Does passing NASCLA automatically issue a Mississippi license?

No. Applicants must complete the Mississippi licensing process and satisfy all applicable business, qualifying-party, financial, insurance, classification, and application requirements.

Why are highlighting and tabs helpful?

Highlighting identifies useful material, while permanent tabs help candidates reach important chapters, code sections, tables, and technical subjects more efficiently.

Should I study with the prepared books before the examination?

Yes. Repeated practice helps candidates learn which reference applies to each subject and become familiar with the highlighting and tabbing system.
