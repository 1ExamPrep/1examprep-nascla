## Exam Details

This Ultimate package supports preparation for two separate contractor examination areas. The Mississippi Law and Business Management examination focuses on the business, legal, financial, and administrative responsibilities involved in operating a contracting company in Mississippi. The NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors measures knowledge across the major technical and management areas of commercial building construction.

All Mississippi contractor applicants are required to complete the Mississippi Law and Business Management examination. Depending on the classification requested, the applicant may also need a trade examination. Candidates pursuing Mississippi’s major Building Construction classification may use the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors instead of the Mississippi Building Construction trade examination.

The NASCLA commercial examination is a standardized trade test accepted by Mississippi and other participating contractor licensing agencies. Passing candidates can use the NASCLA National Examination Database to send their examination transcript to participating agencies. Each state continues to apply its own licensing application, business, financial, experience, qualifying-party, insurance, and classification requirements.

The NASCLA examination addresses a broad range of commercial construction topics. Passing the NASCLA examination does not automatically create a Mississippi contractor license. The applicant must still complete the Mississippi State Board of Contractors application process, pass the required Mississippi Law and Business Management examination, and satisfy the requirements associated with the requested classification.

## Open Book Test

The Mississippi contractor examinations and the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors are open-book tests. Candidates are responsible for bringing the permitted reference materials and following the current examination rules for books, highlighting, tabs, underlining, bindings, notes, and supplemental materials.

The Mississippi Law and Business Management examination uses designated business and law reference material. ## Licensing Steps

1. **Select the Mississippi contractor classification.** Determine whether the major Building Construction classification matches the work the applicant intends to contract for, supervise, and perform.
2. **Establish or register the applying business.** Corporations, limited liability companies, partnerships, and other business entities must be properly registered and in good standing.
3. **Identify the qualifying party.** The applicant must designate the individual who will satisfy the examination and experience requirements for the requested classification.
4. **Prepare the required financial information.** Mississippi commercial contractor applications require financial documentation supporting the applicant’s qualifications for the requested license.
5. **Obtain the required insurance.** Prepare proof of the liability insurance required for the commercial contractor application.
6. **Complete tax-registration requirements.** Obtain the Mississippi tax identification information required for the applying business.
7. **Submit the contractor application.** Provide the Mississippi State Board of Contractors with the application, qualifying-party information, supporting documentation, and applicable fees.
8. **Receive examination eligibility.** The Board reviews the submitted application information before the qualifying party completes the required state examinations.
9. **Prepare for the Mississippi business examination.** Review contractor law, business management, contracts, financial responsibilities, project administration, employment concerns, insurance, taxes, safety, and licensing requirements.
10. **Apply for the NASCLA examination.** Complete the NASCLA application process and receive approval before scheduling the commercial general building examination.
11. **Pass the required examinations.** Complete the Mississippi Law and Business Management examination and the NASCLA commercial general building trade examination.
12. **Send the NASCLA transcript.** Use the NASCLA National Examination Database to send the passing trade-examination result to the Mississippi licensing authority.
13. **Complete final application review.** Respond to requests for missing documentation or additional information before the license application is approved.
14. **Receive the license before contracting.** Do not offer, bid, or perform work requiring a Mississippi contractor license until the license has been issued.

## State Requirements

Commercial contractor licensing in Mississippi is administered by the Mississippi State Board of Contractors. Commercial and residential contractors performing work within regulated classifications must obtain the appropriate license before contracting.

The major Building Construction classification includes commercial building work and residential structures within the scope established by the Board. Mississippi accepts either its state Building Construction trade examination or the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors for this classification.

All applicants must complete the Mississippi Law and Business Management examination. Passing the NASCLA examination may satisfy the accepted trade-exam component, but it does not replace the Mississippi business examination or the remaining licensing requirements.

The applicant must identify a qualifying party who satisfies the experience and examination requirements for the classification. Mississippi allows multiple qualifying parties to be associated with a license, allowing one individual to qualify the trade classification while another completes the law and business examination when permitted.

Corporations, limited liability companies, partnerships, and other applying entities must be properly registered. The legal name used on the contractor application should match the official business registration and supporting documents.

Commercial applicants must provide the required tax identification information, financial documentation, and proof of general liability insurance. A completed application is reviewed by the Mississippi State Board of Contractors before a certificate of responsibility or contractor license is issued.

Local counties and municipalities may impose additional business-license, permit, zoning, inspection, or registration requirements. A Mississippi contractor license does not replace local approvals or project-specific construction permits.
