## Quick answers

### Does Mississippi accept the NASCLA exam?

Yes. Mississippi accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors in place of its state trade exam. You still apply to the Mississippi board and meet its other requirements, which usually include a business and law exam.

### How do I register for the NASCLA exam?

Apply through NASCLA at ned.nascla.org. You must be pre-approved before you can schedule, and then you apply to the Mississippi board with your passing record.
