## State Requirements

The **Mississippi State Board of Contractors** regulates commercial and residential contractors in Mississippi. Residential electrical contractors performing regulated residential electrical work must hold the applicable Mississippi license.

The Residential Electrical classification involves the **installation, maintenance, and repair of residential electrical systems**. The holder of this classification is limited to residential electrical work only.

Mississippi requires applicants to complete an application before testing. After the Board receives and reviews the application, the qualifying party's information is forwarded to PSI so the individual can schedule the required examination.

All applicants must take the **Mississippi Law and Business Management examination**. Depending on the classification and examination pathway, a trade exam may also be required. Mississippi's acceptance of the NASCLA Residential Electrical Contractor examination allows the accepted NASCLA result to address the applicable residential electrical trade-examination component.

The Board's examinations are open book. Candidates receive a Candidate Information Bulletin containing examination procedures, scheduling information, reference-book rules, and other testing instructions. Candidates should follow the current bulletin when determining exactly which materials can be used at a specific Mississippi examination appointment.

Applicants must satisfy the Board's full application requirements before a license is issued. The Board reviews the application, documentation, and passing examination results before approving the license.

Mississippi also requires appropriate insurance documentation for active residential licenses. General liability insurance is part of the residential contractor licensing process, and workers' compensation documentation applies when required by the applicant's business circumstances.

Contractor licenses are issued to the approved entity and must be used in the name shown on the license. Mississippi instructs contractors to conduct business, sign bids, contracts, permits, and other documents using the licensed business name.

Mississippi contractor licenses are renewed annually. Beginning in **2026**, the Mississippi State Board of Contractors sends annual renewal notices by email only. License holders remain responsible for renewing on time and maintaining current contact information with the Board.
