## Licensing Steps

1. **Request the application:** Request an application and instructions from the Missouri Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors (OSEC).
2. **Submit your application to OSEC:** Missouri determines eligibility for licensure based on your application.
3. **Receive approval to test:** After approval, you’ll receive instructions to pay and schedule your exam with PSI.
4. **Pay PSI and schedule:** The exam fee is paid to PSI, and the exam is scheduled at a PSI testing center.
5. **Take the NASCLA exam:** Sit for the computer-based examination with your approved reference books.
