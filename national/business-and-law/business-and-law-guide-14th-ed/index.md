## About this product

The *Basic NASCLA Contractors’ Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Basic 14th Edition* is an officially recognized reference prepared under the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA). Used by multiple state contractor licensing boards, including the CILB, it serves as a comprehensive text for the Business and Law portions of general, building, and specialty contractor exams. The guide outlines regulatory compliance, financial management, labor laws, lien processes, and project documentation requirements applicable to contractor licensing in states such as Florida, Alabama, Georgia, and the Carolinas. Integrated into Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep’s contractor exam preparation programs, it aligns with NASCLA’s standardized exam framework and provides essential content for passing state-recognized trade and business law examinations.

Basic NASCLA Contractors' Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Basic 14th Edition. NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Basic 14th Edition is organized into 3 sections.

Part 1 focuses on business planning and startup. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

Part 2 covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors and customers.

Part 3 provides valuable information to assist you in managing the administrative functions of your business. Financial management, tax basics and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and can create serious problems if you do not give them the proper attention.
