The *Basic NASCLA Contractors’ Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Basic 14th Edition; Highlighted & Tabbed* is published under the authority of the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA) and serves as a core reference for the Business and Law portion of the NASCLA-accredited contractor licensing examination. Distributed through Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, this edition includes organized highlighting and pre-tabbed sections covering licensing laws, contract administration, project management, financial management, lien laws, and safety regulations applicable to general contractors and construction professionals. It aligns with the requirements set by state licensing boards such as the CILB and supports preparation for state-recognized contractor exams, including business and finance segments. Designed for use in test centers approved by NASCLA, the guide ensures uniform coverage of essential contractor knowledge areas including bidding, employment law, and environmental compliance as tested across multiple state contractor boards.

The NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management is organized into three sections.

Part 2 Covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business.
