About this product

The Basic NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Basic 13th Edition is a reference manual recognized by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA) and used by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep for contractor exam preparation. This publication provides fundamental information for state contractor licensing exams covering business organization, project management, contract law, and financial management. The 13th Edition aligns with the standardized NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors and serves as an essential reference for candidates preparing for state contractor licensing exams in multiple jurisdictions. The pre-printed tabs offered by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep correspond to major book sections to improve study efficiency and navigation during open-book test sessions. This guide supports contractor license exam preparation for general, residential, and building contractors across NASCLA-participating states, ensuring compliance with legal and business standards within the construction industry.

Frequently asked questions

Is the NASCLA exam open book?

Yes. You bring the approved reference books, so knowing where things are in the books matters as much as knowing the material.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Real exam questions are confidential. Practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.

When will I get it?

Online courses are delivered by email after purchase. Books ship from our fulfillment center, 1 Exam Prep; shipping options are shown at checkout.

Does buying make me eligible to test?

No. NASCLA approves you to sit the exam and your state board decides on your license. This package gets you ready for the test itself.
