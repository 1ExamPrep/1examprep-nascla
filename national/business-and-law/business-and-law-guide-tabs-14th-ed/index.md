## About this product

The Basic NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Basic 14th Edition; Pre-Printed Tabs, published by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA), is a key reference used for contractor licensing exams and administered by multiple state boards and testing vendors such as PSI and Prov. This guide covers essential business practices, state and federal contracting laws, lien laws, project management, and financial management principles necessary for general and specialty contractor licensing. The 14th Edition provides updated regulatory standards, safety compliance, and legal guidelines applicable to contractors pursuing licensure across various NASCLA-participating states.
