Nationwide Master Electrical questions

What NASCLA exam do these flash cards prepare me for?

They are designed to support preparation for the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Contractor examination for Master Electricians and Unlimited Electricians.

How many questions are on the NASCLA Master Electrician exam?

The examination contains 100 scored questions plus 10 additional non-scored experimental questions.

What score do I need to pass the NASCLA Master Electrician exam?

The current examination bulletin identifies a minimum passing score of 75 .

How long is the NASCLA Master Electrical exam?

Candidates receive 270 minutes , or 4½ hours, to complete the examination.

Is the NASCLA Master Electrician exam computer based?

Yes. The examination is administered by computer through PSI.

What subjects are covered on the NASCLA Master Electrical exam?

The content outline includes project design and management, safety, electrical theory and principles, general code requirements, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, equipment for general use, special occupancies and equipment, special conditions, and communication systems.
