About this package

The NASCLA Residential Electrical Contractor Book Package by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep includes all officially approved reference materials required for the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Residential Electrical Contractors. This comprehensive set features the NFPA 70: National Electrical Code (NEC), OSHA Construction Safety and Health Regulations, and the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management. Designed for use in PSI-administered licensing exams, the package covers residential wiring methods, service calculations, grounding, overcurrent protection, and low-voltage systems. Aligned with NASCLA’s multi-state reciprocity program, this book package ensures candidates meet the technical and regulatory knowledge standards for residential electrical contractor licensure recognized across participating jurisdictions.

This package includes the following books effective for testing 8/1/25:

1. National Electrical Code, 2023

2. Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR 1926 - OSHA Construction Industry Regulations

3. NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Basic 14th Ed.

4. NFPA 70E - Standard for Electrical Safety in the Workplace, 2024

5. Ugly’s Electrical References, 2023

6. NFPA 72 - National Fire Alarm and Signaling Code, 2022 Edition

7. Understanding Electrical Theory for NEC Applications

8. ANSI Z535.4-2023, Product Safety Signs and Labels, American National Standard for Product Safety Signs and Labels

9. International Residential Code (IRC), 2024

Frequently asked questions

Is the NASCLA exam open book?

Yes. You bring the approved reference books, so knowing where things are in the books matters as much as knowing the material.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Real exam questions are confidential. Practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.

When will I get it?

Online courses are delivered by email after purchase. Books ship from our fulfillment center, 1 Exam Prep; shipping options are shown at checkout.

Does buying make me eligible to test?

No. NASCLA approves you to sit the exam and your state board decides on your license. This package gets you ready for the test itself.
