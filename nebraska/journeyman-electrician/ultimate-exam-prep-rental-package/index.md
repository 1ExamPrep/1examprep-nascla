## Exam Details

The Nebraska State Electrical Division administers examinations for Journeyman Electricians and participates in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program. Candidates pursuing the NASCLA pathway for Nebraska must take the examination through the Nebraska State Electrical Division rather than transferring an examination result from another administering jurisdiction.

Nebraska requires the applicable NASCLA examination date to be after **August 1, 2025** for acceptance. Candidates should complete Nebraska's eligibility process before scheduling their examination.

Nebraska currently bases electrical examinations on the **2023 National Electrical Code**. The Nebraska State Electrical Division adopted the 2023 NEC beginning August 1, 2024, and electrical examinations transitioned to the 2023 code at that time.

Nebraska identifies several important examination subjects for electrical license applicants, including:

- National Electrical Code - 2023
- Basic Electricity and Electrical Theory
- Nebraska State Electrical Act
- Emergency Circuits, including Fire Alarm Circuits

NASCLA Journeyman Electrician preparation should also include interpreting plans and specifications, electrical safety, testing and troubleshooting, general code requirements, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, equipment for general use, special occupancies, special equipment, and special conditions and communication systems.

National Electrical Code study should include services, feeders, branch circuits, conductor ampacity, overcurrent protection, grounding and bonding, wiring methods, raceways, boxes, cables, motors, transformers, electrical equipment, special occupancies, and communication systems.

After Nebraska approves a candidate to test, eligibility information is submitted to **PSI**. PSI then provides scheduling information for the examination. Nebraska instructs candidates to complete the state examination application and receive approval before scheduling.

Passing the examination does not automatically create a Nebraska Journeyman Electrician license. The Nebraska State Electrical Board remains responsible for determining whether the applicant satisfies the experience, education, application, examination, and other requirements for licensure.

## State Requirements

The **Nebraska State Electrical Division** administers statewide electrical licensing. A Nebraska Journeyman Electrician may install electrical wiring, apparatus, and equipment and may supervise apprentice electricians within the scope authorized by the state license.

An applicant for a Nebraska Journeyman Electrician license must generally have at least **four years, or 8,000 hours, of verifiable experience** acceptable to the Board in the electrical trade. Registration as an apprentice electrician during those years may constitute evidence of the required experience when approved by the Board.

Nebraska provides an education-based experience credit. Successful completion of an approved two-year post-high-school electrical course resulting in an **Associate's degree in Electrical Technology** may provide one year of work-experience credit. This can allow an applicant to qualify with three years of acceptable verifiable experience together with the qualifying degree.

Qualified electrical experience gained during military service may also receive work-experience credit when accepted by the Nebraska State Electrical Board.

Nebraska also distinguishes between unrestricted and restricted Journeyman Electrician licenses based on in-state experience. If an applicant has fewer than **4,000 hours of verifiable electrical experience in Nebraska**, the resulting journeyman license is restricted and cannot be used for reciprocity with another state.

The Nebraska State Electrical Board maintains reciprocal Journeyman Electrician agreements with Alaska, Arkansas, Colorado, Iowa, Idaho, Minnesota, Montana, New Mexico, North Dakota, Oklahoma, South Dakota, Texas, and Wyoming. Reciprocity has its own requirements and should be treated separately from the NASCLA examination pathway.

Nebraska currently lists a **$90 application fee** for the Journeyman Electrician license. License purchase fees depend on the year in which the license is issued. These government licensing charges are separate from the price of this Ultimate Exam Prep Rental Package.

Nebraska also requires electrical license and apprenticeship applicants to complete the applicable lawful-presence attestation associated with the state's licensing process.
