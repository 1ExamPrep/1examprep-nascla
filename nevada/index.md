## Quick answers

### Does Nevada accept the NASCLA exam?

Yes. Nevada accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors in place of its state trade exam. You still apply to the Nevada board and meet its other requirements, which usually include a business and law exam.

### Which NASCLA exams can I prepare for in Nevada?

### How do I register for the NASCLA exam?

Apply through NASCLA at ned.nascla.org. You must be pre-approved before you can schedule, and then you apply to the Nevada board with your passing record.
