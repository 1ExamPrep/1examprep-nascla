Licensing Steps

New Jersey regulates several construction-related professions through specialized licensing boards. The exact process depends on the professional license or certification being pursued.

Step 1: Identify Your Trade or Professional License

Determine whether your goal involves electrical contracting, HVACR contracting, master plumbing, alarm systems, locksmith work, or another regulated profession. Different occupations have different licensing requirements.

Step 2: Review the Applicable Board's Qualifications

Examine the education, experience, training, examination, and other qualification requirements established for your profession.

Step 3: Prepare the Required Application

Complete the licensing application and gather the supporting records requested by the applicable New Jersey licensing board.

Step 4: Identify Required Examinations

Determine whether your professional classification requires a Business and Law examination, a technical examination, or additional examinations.

Step 5: Study the Approved References

Use the New Jersey NASCLA 2nd Edition to prepare for applicable business and law subjects. Study separate technical references associated with your trade examination.

Step 6: Complete Examination Registration

Follow the authorization and scheduling procedures established by the appropriate licensing authority and examination provider.

Step 7: Complete Your Examination and Licensing Requirements

Attend the required examination, follow the testing provider's procedures, and complete any remaining requirements established by the responsible licensing board.

Passing an examination is one component of professional licensing. License issuance remains subject to the applicable board's approval requirements.

State Requirements

New Jersey regulates professional contractors in several skilled trades through the New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs and its associated occupational licensing boards.
