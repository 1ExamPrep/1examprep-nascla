## About this product

The *New Jersey NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 2nd Edition*, published under the authority of the New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs and prepared by NASCLA, provides essential reference material for candidates seeking a New Jersey general contractor license. This guide aligns with the NASCLA Accredited Examination program and covers core topics such as contractor business organization, licensing laws, lien laws, insurance, tax requirements, project management, safety regulations, and labor laws applicable to New Jersey contractors. Used in conjunction with the official NASCLA Business and Law Exam, this manual is an approved reference source for One Exam Prep’s contractor license exam preparation courses, helping applicants meet state construction licensing standards through structured review of key administrative and financial principles for residential and commercial contracting in New Jersey.

NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, New Jersey 2nd Edition is organized into three sections.

Part 1 focuses on planning and starting your business. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

Part 2 covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities, and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors, and customers.

Part 3 provides valuable information to assist you in running the administrative functions of your business. Financial management, tax basics, and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and can cause you serious problems if you do not give them proper attention.
