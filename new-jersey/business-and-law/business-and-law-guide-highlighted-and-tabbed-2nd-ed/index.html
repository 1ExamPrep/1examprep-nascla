About this product

The New Jersey NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, NJ 2nd Edition; Highlighted & Tabbed, offered by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, is the officially approved reference manual for the New Jersey contractor licensing examinations administered by PSI under the authority of the New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs. Developed by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA), this guide provides comprehensive coverage of topics required for licensure, including business organization, financial management, contract administration, safety regulations, and state-specific construction laws. The Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep edition comes pre-highlighted and tabbed according to PSI’s open-book exam structure, allowing efficient navigation during testing. Designed for General, Building, and Trade Contractors, this NASCLA manual ensures compliance with New Jersey’s regulatory standards and supports candidates in demonstrating knowledge of business, law, and project management practices essential for state contractor certification.

NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, New Jersey Edition is organized into three sections.

Part 1 focuses on planning and starting your business. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

Part 2 covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities, and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors, and customers.

Part 3 provides valuable information to assist you in running the administrative functions of your business. Financial management, tax basics, and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and can cause you serious problems if you do not give them proper attention.

Frequently asked questions

Is the NASCLA exam open book?

Yes. You bring the approved reference books, so knowing where things are in the books matters as much as knowing the material.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Real exam questions are confidential. Practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.
