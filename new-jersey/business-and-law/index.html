New Jersey Business & Law questions

What is the New Jersey NASCLA Contractor's Guide, 2nd Edition?

It is a New Jersey-specific business, law, and project management reference published by NASCLA. The guide covers contractor business planning, licensing, insurance, estimating, contracts, project management, financial administration, and related responsibilities.

Is the New Jersey NASCLA 2nd Edition an approved examination reference?

Yes. The New Jersey 2nd Edition is identified as a reference for applicable Business and Law examinations associated with several regulated construction and trade professions.

What is the ISBN of the New Jersey NASCLA 2nd Edition?

The ISBN-13 is 978-1-948558-24-2.

When did the New Jersey NASCLA 2nd Edition become the required edition?

NASCLA designated the New Jersey 2nd Edition as the examination basis beginning June 1, 2025, following the transition period from the first edition.

Does this publication replace technical trade codebooks?

No. The NASCLA guide focuses on business, law, and project management. Technical examinations may require additional trade-specific codes and reference publications.

Are highlighted and tabbed reference books allowed during the examination?

Reference markings and tabs must comply with the specific examination requirements. The testing facility proctor makes the final determination regarding the acceptance of reference materials.
