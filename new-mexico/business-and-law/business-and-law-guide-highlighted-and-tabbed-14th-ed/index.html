Important New Mexico Open-Book Examination Rules:

  • Only designated reference materials may be brought into the examination room.

Licensing Steps

New Mexico contractor licensing is administered through the Construction Industries Division of the New Mexico Regulation and Licensing Department.

The process involves selecting the appropriate contractor classification, establishing a qualifying party, completing applicable examination requirements, and submitting the company licensing application.

Step 1: Identify Your Contractor Classification

Determine the classification corresponding to the construction services your business intends to perform. New Mexico maintains different classifications for general construction, electrical, mechanical, and specialty activities.

Step 2: Establish Your Qualifying Party

Identify the individual responsible for satisfying the experience and examination requirements associated with the requested contractor classification.

Step 3: Submit the Qualifying Party Application

Prepare the qualifying party application and required work experience documentation. Examination authorization follows the applicable review and approval process.

Step 4: Satisfy the Business and Law Requirement

Complete the New Mexico Business and Law examination or an approved Business and Law course.

Step 5: Complete the Required Trade Examination

Prepare for and complete the technical examination associated with your contractor classification when required.

Step 6: Submit the Company License Application

Prepare the contractor license application and provide the required business and qualifying-party documentation.

Step 7: Complete Licensing Approval

Submit applicable bonding, tax registration, and organizational documents and complete the remaining requirements established by the Construction Industries Division.

Contractors must obtain the appropriate license before performing construction activities requiring authorization under New Mexico law.
