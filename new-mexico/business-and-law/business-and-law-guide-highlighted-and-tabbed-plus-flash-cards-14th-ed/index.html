Licensing Steps

New Mexico contractor licensing is administered through the Construction Industries Division of the New Mexico Regulation and Licensing Department.

The licensing process generally involves selecting a contractor classification, establishing a qualifying party, satisfying applicable examinations, and completing the business license application.

Step 1: Identify Your Contractor Classification

Determine the classification that corresponds to the construction services your business intends to perform. New Mexico maintains classifications for general construction, electrical, mechanical, and specialty work.

Step 2: Establish Your Qualifying Party

Identify the individual responsible for satisfying the experience and examination requirements associated with the requested classification.

Step 3: Complete the Qualifying Party Application

Prepare the appropriate application and supporting experience documentation required for qualification.

Step 4: Satisfy the Business and Law Requirement

Complete the New Mexico Business and Law examination or an approved Business and Law course.

Step 5: Complete the Required Trade Examination

Prepare for and complete the technical examination required for the selected contractor classification, when applicable.

Step 6: Submit the Contractor License Application

Prepare the company licensing application and provide the required business and qualifying-party documentation.

Step 7: Complete Licensing Approval

Provide applicable bonding, tax registration, and organizational records and complete the remaining licensing requirements.

Contractors must obtain the appropriate authorization before performing construction activities that require a New Mexico contractor license.
