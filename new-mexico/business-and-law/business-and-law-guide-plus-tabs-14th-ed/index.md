**Important PSI Reference Material Rules:**

- Only approved reference materials are permitted in the examination room.

## Licensing Steps

New Mexico contractor licensing is administered through the Construction Industries Division of the New Mexico Regulation and Licensing Department.

The process generally involves establishing a qualifying party, satisfying the applicable examination requirements, and submitting the business licensing application.

**Step 1: Identify Your Contractor Classification**

Determine the classification appropriate for the construction services you intend to provide. New Mexico maintains separate classifications for general construction, electrical, mechanical, and specialty work.

**Step 2: Establish Your Qualifying Party**

Identify the individual who will satisfy the experience and examination requirements for the requested classification.

**Step 3: Submit the Qualifying Party Application**

Complete the required application and work experience affidavits. The qualifying party must receive approval before scheduling the applicable examinations.

**Step 4: Satisfy the Business and Law Requirement**

Complete the New Mexico Business and Law examination or an approved Business and Law course.

**Step 5: Complete Any Required Trade Examination**

Prepare for and complete the technical examination required for your contractor classification.

**Step 6: Prepare the Company Application**

Submit the appropriate contractor license application with the required business documentation, qualifying-party information, and supporting materials.

**Step 7: Complete Licensing Approval**

Provide the applicable bonding, tax registration, business entity, and other required documentation to complete the licensing process.

Contractors must satisfy the applicable licensing requirements before performing regulated contracting work in New Mexico.
