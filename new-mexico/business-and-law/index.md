## New Mexico Business & Law questions

### What is the New Mexico NASCLA Contractor's Guide Basic 14th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Basic 14th Edition, a reference publication identified for New Mexico contractor Business and Law examination preparation.

### Is the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition an approved New Mexico examination reference?

Yes. NASCLA identifies the Basic 14th Edition as a reference for the New Mexico Business and Law examination.

### Who administers New Mexico contractor examinations?

PSI Services provides examination services for contractor licensing administered through the New Mexico Construction Industries Division.

### What is the ISBN of the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition?

The ISBN-13 is 978-1-948558-39-6.

### Can I use tabs in my New Mexico contractor examination reference book?

Permanent tabs are permitted when they comply with examination requirements. Temporary tabs and removable sticky notes are prohibited. Testing personnel determine whether the reference materials meet the applicable rules.

### Does New Mexico require every qualifying party to pass the Business and Law examination?

New Mexico permits the Business and Law requirement to be satisfied through the examination or an approved Business and Law course.
