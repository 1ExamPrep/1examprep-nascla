## Quick answers

### Does New Mexico accept the NASCLA exam?

Yes. New Mexico accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors in place of its state trade exam. You still apply to the New Mexico board and meet its other requirements, which usually include a business and law exam.

### Which NASCLA exams can I prepare for in New Mexico?

### How do I register for the NASCLA exam?

Apply through NASCLA at ned.nascla.org. You must be pre-approved before you can schedule, and then you apply to the New Mexico board with your passing record.
