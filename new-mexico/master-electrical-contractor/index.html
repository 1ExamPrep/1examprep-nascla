New Mexico Master Electrical questions

Does New Mexico accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination?

Yes. The New Mexico Construction Industries Division participates in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and accepts the Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination in lieu of the applicable trade examination for the EE-98 license pathway.

Does Passing NASCLA Automatically Give Me a New Mexico EE98 License?

No. Passing the NASCLA examination can satisfy the accepted trade-examination component, but candidates must still complete the applicable New Mexico qualifying-party, Business and Law, licensing, and application requirements.

Is there an additional requirement for using the NASCLA exam in New Mexico?

Yes. Candidates using New Mexico's NASCLA acceptance pathway must provide a letter of good standing from another state agency showing that they have been licensed for at least one year.

Are the Books Highlighted and Tabbed?

Yes. All included books are highlighted and tabbed to support organized exam preparation and reference navigation.

What Is Contractor Compliance Guidance?

Contractor Compliance Guidance provides assistance understanding requirements and responsibilities associated with operating a New Mexico contracting business.

How Should I Prepare for an Open-Book Electrical Examination?

Study the electrical concepts first and practice using the references throughout your preparation. Learn the NEC structure, indexes, tables, common articles, and supporting references. Practice timed lookups and review missed questions so you become more comfortable choosing the correct book and locating information efficiently.
