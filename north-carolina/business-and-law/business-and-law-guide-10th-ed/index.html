Open Book Test

The North Carolina Business and Law examination is an open-book test. Candidates may bring approved reference publications into the testing center according to PSI examination requirements.

The NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, North Carolina General Contractors Edition, 10th Edition, is an approved reference.

Open-book testing still requires careful preparation. Candidates must understand the examination subjects, recognize important terminology, and locate relevant information within the permitted references.

Important North Carolina Examination Reference Rules:

  • Only approved reference publications may be used during the examination.
  • Reference books may be highlighted and underlined before the examination.
  • Permanent indexing tabs may be used when they meet PSI requirements.
  • Removable tabs and temporary sticky notes are not permitted.
  • No handwritten notes may be added to the reference materials.
  • Candidates may not write in references during the examination.
  • Loose papers and unauthorized attached materials are prohibited.
  • Testing personnel determine whether the reference materials and tabs are acceptable.

Permanent indexing tabs can support efficient navigation when they comply with the testing provider's requirements.

Reference materials must remain within the examination's permitted preparation and marking rules.

Licensing Steps

North Carolina general contractor licensing is regulated by the North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors.

Applicants must satisfy the requirements associated with their requested classification and license limitation before receiving a general contractor license.

Step 1: Determine Your Contractor Classification

Identify the classification that corresponds to the construction activities your business intends to perform. North Carolina recognizes classifications including building, residential, highway, public utilities, and specialty construction.

Step 2: Review Licensing Requirements

Review the Board's application requirements, financial qualifications, examination obligations, and other requirements associated with your requested license.

Step 3: Prepare the License Application

Complete the appropriate application and gather the supporting documentation required by the Board.

Step 4: Obtain Examination Eligibility

Follow the Board's process for examination authorization and complete any required candidate registration procedures.

Step 5: Study the Approved References

Prepare with the NASCLA North Carolina 10th Edition for business and law subjects. Review additional technical reference publications required for your selected examination.

Step 6: Complete the Required Examination

Schedule and attend the applicable PSI examination. Bring the required identification and permitted reference materials.

Step 7: Complete the Licensing Process

After satisfying examination requirements, complete any remaining Board review and approval procedures.

Passing an examination is only one component of obtaining a general contractor license. Applicants must satisfy the Board's licensing requirements before performing work that requires licensure.
