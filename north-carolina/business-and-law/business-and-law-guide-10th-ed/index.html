About this product

The North Carolina NASCLA Contractor's Guide to Business, Law and Project Management NC General, 10th Edition by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep serves as the official reference manual approved by the North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors (NCLBGC). This comprehensive guide provides essential coverage of state-specific business regulations, licensing laws, project management principles, contract administration, lien laws, and financial management practices. Developed under the NASCLA framework, the 10th Edition is structured to prepare candidates for the North Carolina General Contractor licensing examination. It includes detailed explanations of recordkeeping, labor compliance, risk management, and safety standards applicable to general contracting operations. As an authoritative study and reference resource, this manual supports contractors in achieving exam readiness and maintaining regulatory compliance within North Carolina’s construction industry.


NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management North Carolina General, 10th Edition is organized into 3 sections.

Part 1 focuses on business planning and start up. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

Part 2 is centered on fundamentals that you will need to operate a successful construction business. This section will cover estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors and customers.

Part 3 gives you valuable information for running the administrative function of your business. Financial management, tax basics and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and can cause you serious problems if you do not give them the proper attention.

Frequently asked questions

Is the NASCLA exam open book?

Yes. You bring the approved reference books, so knowing where things are in the books matters as much as knowing the material.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Real exam questions are confidential. Practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.
