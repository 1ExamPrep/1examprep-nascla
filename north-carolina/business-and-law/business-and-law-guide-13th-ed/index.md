## About this product

The *North Carolina NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Electrical 13th Edition* is an official reference manual used for the North Carolina Electrical Contractor licensing examination, approved by the North Carolina State Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors (NCBEEC). Published by NASCLA (National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies) and distributed through Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, this 13th edition covers essential business organization, licensing laws, project management principles, lien laws, labor regulations, safety standards, and financial management practices specific to electrical contractors operating in North Carolina. It is structured to support candidates preparing for NASCLA-accredited electrical exams and aligns with state contractor license requirements, emphasizing compliance, accounting, and risk management relevant to electrical contracting projects.

The ***North Carolina* *NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management - Electrical 13th Edition** *is organized into 3 sections.

**Part 1** focuses on planning and starting your business. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

**Part 2** covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities, and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors, and customers.

**Part 3** provides valuable information to assist you in running the administrative functions of your business. Financial management, tax basics, and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and can cause you serious problems if you do not give them proper attention.
