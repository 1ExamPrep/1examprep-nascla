Licensing Steps

North Carolina general contractor licensing is administered through the North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors.

Applicants must satisfy the requirements associated with their requested classification and license limitation.

Step 1: Identify Your Contractor Classification

Determine the classification appropriate for the construction services your business intends to perform. North Carolina recognizes building, residential, highway, public utilities, and specialty classifications.

Step 2: Review Licensing Qualifications

Review the Board's application requirements, financial qualifications, examination obligations, and responsibilities associated with your requested license limitation.

Step 3: Prepare Your Licensing Application

Complete the appropriate application and gather the supporting documentation required by the Board.

Step 4: Complete Examination Authorization

Follow the Board's procedures for establishing examination eligibility and scheduling through the designated testing provider.

Step 5: Study the Approved Reference Materials

Use the NASCLA North Carolina 10th Edition to review business and law subjects. Practice navigating with the installed tabs and review the highlighted passages.

Step 6: Complete the Required Examination

Step 7: Complete Licensing Approval

After satisfying examination requirements, complete any remaining documentation and approval procedures established by the North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors.
