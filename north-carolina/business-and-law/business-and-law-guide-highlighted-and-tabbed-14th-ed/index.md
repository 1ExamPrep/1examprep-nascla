## Open Book Test

This professionally highlighted and tabbed NASCLA guide supports reference-navigation practice before examination day.

**Important Examination Reference Guidelines:**

- Only designated reference publications may be brought into the examination room.
- Reference books must be properly bound; loose-leaf references are not permitted.
- Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and annotated before the examination in accordance with the Board's rules.
- Permanent indexing tabs are permitted when they meet examination requirements.
- Temporary tabs, removable sticky notes, stapled tabs, and divider tabs are not permitted.
- Unauthorized loose papers and additional attached materials are prohibited.
- Candidates may not write in reference publications during the examination.
- Testing personnel determine whether reference materials and tabs meet the examination requirements.

**IMPORTANT: TESTING FACILITY TAB ACCEPTANCE**

Please note that the Testing Facility Proctor is the deciding factor on whether or not tabs will be accepted and allowed into the exam. If the tabs do not adhere properly, they will not be accepted.

## Licensing Steps

North Carolina electrical contractor licensing is administered through the North Carolina State Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors.

The process includes selecting an electrical contractor classification, meeting examination qualifications, completing the applicable examination, and applying for licensure.

**Step 1: Choose Your Electrical Contractor Classification**

Determine whether your intended contracting work requires a Limited, Intermediate, Unlimited, or Special Restricted license.

**Step 2: Review the Qualification Requirements**

Study the Board's eligibility standards for the requested classification and prepare the required experience information.

**Step 3: Submit Your Examination Application**

Complete the Board's examination application and submit the supporting documents required for consideration.

**Step 4: Receive Examination Authorization**

After approval, the Board provides a Notice of Approval identifying the authorized examination classification and scheduling period.

**Step 5: Schedule Your PSI Examination**

Complete the examination scheduling process after receiving the required PSI eligibility information.

**Step 6: Prepare with Approved References**

Study the highlighted and tabbed NASCLA guide for business and law subjects. Review the applicable electrical codes, licensing laws, and Board rules separately.

**Step 7: Complete the Examination**

Attend your scheduled examination with the required identification and permitted reference publications.

**Step 8: Complete the Licensing Application**

After meeting examination requirements, submit the appropriate licensing application and complete any remaining Board requirements.

Contractors must satisfy the Board's applicable requirements before performing electrical contracting work that requires licensure.
