Licensing Steps

North Carolina general contractor licensing is regulated by the North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors. Applicants must meet the requirements associated with their requested classification and license limitation.

Step 1: Identify Your Contractor Classification

Determine the classification appropriate for the construction work your business intends to perform. North Carolina recognizes building, residential, highway, public utilities, and specialty contractor classifications.

Step 2: Review Licensing Qualifications

Review the Board's requirements for your requested classification and license limitation, including financial qualifications and examination responsibilities.

Step 3: Prepare Your Application

Complete the licensing application and gather the required supporting documentation.

Step 4: Obtain Examination Authorization

Follow the Board's process for examination eligibility and complete the appropriate scheduling procedures through PSI.

Step 5: Prepare with the NASCLA Guide

Review highlighted material, practice using the installed chapter tabs, and incorporate the flash cards into your study schedule. Prepare separately with additional technical references required for your examination.

Step 6: Complete the Required Examination

Attend your scheduled examination with the required identification and permitted reference materials.

Step 7: Complete Licensing Approval

After satisfying the examination requirements, complete the remaining licensing procedures established by the North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors.

Passing an examination is one part of the licensing process. Applicants must satisfy all applicable Board requirements before receiving a general contractor license.
