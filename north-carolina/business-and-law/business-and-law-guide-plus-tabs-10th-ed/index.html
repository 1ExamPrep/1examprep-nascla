Licensing Steps

North Carolina general contractor licensing is administered through the North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors. Applicants must satisfy the requirements associated with their requested classification and license limitation.

Step 1: Determine Your Contractor Classification

Identify the classification corresponding to the construction services your business intends to perform. North Carolina recognizes building, residential, highway, public utilities, and specialty classifications.

Step 2: Review Licensing Qualifications

Review the Board's application requirements, financial qualifications, and examination obligations associated with your requested license.

Step 3: Prepare the License Application

Complete the applicable application and gather the supporting documentation required by the Board.

Step 4: Obtain Examination Eligibility

Step 5: Prepare Your Examination References
