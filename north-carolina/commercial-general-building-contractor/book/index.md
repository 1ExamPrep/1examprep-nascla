## Exam Details

The NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors is administered by **PSI** through the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies.

The examination measures knowledge of commercial construction methods, project administration, building materials, technical requirements, and contracting practices.

- **Examination:** NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors.
- **North Carolina Classification:** Building Contractor.
- **Accrediting Organization:** National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA).
- **Testing Provider:** PSI Services.
- **Scored Questions:** 115.
- **Additional Questions:** 10 unscored pretest questions.
- **Passing Score:** 81 correct answers from 115 scored questions.
- **Time Allowed:** 330 minutes, or 5 hours and 30 minutes.
- **Examination Format:** Open book.
- **Testing Method:** Computer-based examination.
- **Major Content Areas:** General requirements, procurement, contracting, site construction, concrete, masonry, metals, wood, building envelopes, finishes, mechanical systems, and electrical systems.

Procurement and contracting requirements represent the largest examination category, followed by general construction requirements and site construction.

These three subjects account for 71 of the 115 scored questions, making them important preparation priorities.

## Open Book Test

The **North Carolina NASCLA General Contractor examination is an open book test**. Candidates may consult the designated reference publications during testing, subject to PSI examination policies.

The examination uses an extensive technical reference library covering construction methods, building codes, project management, materials, and safety.

Reference books are not supplied at the testing center. Candidates must bring their own permitted publications.

Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before examination day. Permanent tabs are permitted.

Handwritten notes, loose papers, inserted materials, and temporary tabs are prohibited.

Code questions are based on the editions identified in the examination reference list. Candidates should develop familiarity with those specific code editions.

PSI provides a physical diagram and blueprint packet at the testing center for applicable questions.

A silent, nonprinting, nonprogrammable calculator is permitted.

Because the examination covers many reference books, efficient navigation is an important preparation skill. Practice locating relevant information using indexes, tables of contents, technical definitions, and permanent tabs.

## Licensing Steps

North Carolina accepts NASCLA examination results for the Building Contractor classification. Candidates complete the NASCLA examination process separately from the state's licensing application.

1. **Review the examination:** Understand the NASCLA Commercial General Building examination scope and its acceptance by North Carolina.
2. **Apply through NASCLA:** Submit the examination application for NASCLA approval.
3. **Receive authorization:** Obtain examination eligibility after NASCLA processes the application.
4. **Schedule with PSI:** Register for the approved examination.
5. **Prepare study references:** Organize the permitted books and review the examination content outline.
6. **Complete the examination:** Take the NASCLA Commercial General Building examination.
7. **Request transcript delivery:** Use NASCLA's National Examination Database to transmit examination results to North Carolina.
8. **Apply for North Carolina licensing:** Submit the required application to the North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors.
9. **Meet state qualifications:** Complete the applicable financial, character, and administrative requirements.
10. **Receive licensing approval:** Complete the Board's procedures before conducting regulated contracting work.

NASCLA examination approval provides one year of eligibility, with a maximum of three examination attempts during that period.

Passing the NASCLA examination does not independently grant a North Carolina contractor license.

## North Carolina State Requirements

The **North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors** regulates general contractor licensing under Chapter 87 of the North Carolina General Statutes.

North Carolina recognizes the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors as an accepted examination for its Building Contractor classification.

Applicants must be at least 18 years old, possess good moral character, demonstrate financial responsibility, and satisfy applicable application requirements.

North Carolina Building Contractor licenses are issued under three limitations:

- **Limited:** Individual projects valued up to $750,000.
- **Intermediate:** Individual projects valued up to $1,500,000.
- **Unlimited:** No individual project-value limitation.

The Building classification authorizes qualifying construction work involving residential, commercial, industrial, public, and private buildings.

License classification determines the type of work authorized, while the license limitation establishes the permitted individual project value.

Using the NASCLA examination also provides a pathway to submit examination transcripts to other participating licensing jurisdictions, subject to each jurisdiction's requirements.
