About this package

The North Carolina NASCLA Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors Complete Book Set provides all state-approved trade references required for the NASCLA-accredited commercial general building contractor licensing exam in North Carolina. The set includes codes and manuals covering structural design principles, concrete and masonry standards, carpentry and framing, roofing systems, sitework and excavation practices, safety regulations, project management, estimating and bidding, and contractor business and law requirements. Aligned with One Exam Prep’s structured exam-prep format, the collection supplies the technical criteria and regulatory guidance essential for performing compliant commercial building construction throughout North Carolina.

Frequently asked questions

Is the NASCLA exam open book?

Yes. You bring the approved reference books, so knowing where things are in the books matters as much as knowing the material.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Real exam questions are confidential. Practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.

When will I get it?

Online courses are delivered by email after purchase. Books ship from our fulfillment center, 1 Exam Prep; shipping options are shown at checkout.

Does buying make me eligible to test?

No. NASCLA approves you to sit the exam and your state board decides on your license. This package gets you ready for the test itself.
