About this product

The North Carolina NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor Exam Pre-Printed Tabs, provided by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep, are specifically designed for candidates preparing for the NASCLA Accredited Commercial General Building Contractor examination administered by PSI and approved by the North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors (NCLBGC). These durable, color-coded adhesive tabs correspond to all NASCLA-authorized reference books, including the International Building Code (IBC), OSHA Construction Industry Regulations, and the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management. Each tab is pre-labeled according to PSI’s open-book exam structure, allowing for efficient navigation of key code provisions and reference materials during testing. Created to enhance accuracy and speed, this tab set ensures full compliance with North Carolina contractor examination requirements and supports successful preparation for the NASCLA-accredited Commercial General Building Contractor license.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the NASCLA exam open book?

    Yes. You bring the approved reference books, so knowing where things are in the books matters as much as knowing the material.

    Are these the actual exam questions?

    No. Real exam questions are confidential. Practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.

    When will I get it?

    Online courses are delivered by email after purchase. Books ship from our fulfillment center, 1 Exam Prep; shipping options are shown at checkout.

    Does buying make me eligible to test?

    No. NASCLA approves you to sit the exam and your state board decides on your license. This package gets you ready for the test itself.
