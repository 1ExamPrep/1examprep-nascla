State Requirements

General contractor licensing in North Carolina is administered by the North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors. Licenses are organized by classification and limitation. Classification determines the type of contracting work that can be performed, while limitation determines the permitted project size.

The North Carolina Building classification covers broad building construction and demolition activities, including commercial, industrial, institutional, and residential construction within the scope established by the Board.

An applicant must identify a qualifier or qualifying party who has successfully passed an examination accepted by the Board. The North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors accepts the NASCLA examination for the Building classification.

Passing NASCLA is not the same as receiving a North Carolina contractor license. NASCLA provides an accepted examination credential. Applicants must still complete the North Carolina licensing process and satisfy the Board's applicable application, qualifier, financial responsibility, classification, and limitation requirements.
