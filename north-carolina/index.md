## Quick answers

### Does North Carolina accept the NASCLA exam?

Yes. North Carolina accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors in place of its state trade exam. You still apply to the North Carolina board and meet its other requirements, which usually include a business and law exam.

### Which NASCLA exams can I prepare for in North Carolina?

### How do I register for the NASCLA exam?

Apply through NASCLA at ned.nascla.org. You must be pre-approved before you can schedule, and then you apply to the North Carolina board with your passing record.
