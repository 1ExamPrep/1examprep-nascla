## About this product

The *Ohio NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 4th Edition* is an official reference manual published under the authority of the Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board (CILB) and developed by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA). This comprehensive guide provides Ohio contractors with essential information on business organization, contract law, lien rights, safety regulations, financial management, and project supervision, serving as a primary text for the Ohio General, Building, Residential, and Specialty Contractor licensing exams. Used by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep for structured contractor exam preparation, it aligns with NASCLA’s standardized exam content, offering verified guidance for candidates seeking contractor licensing compliance and legal competency across multiple jurisdictions.

NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Ohio 4th Edition is organized into three sections.

Part 1 focuses on planning and starting your business. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

Part 2 Covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities, and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors, and customers.

Part 3 provides valuable information to assist you in running the administrative functions of your business. Financial management, tax basics, and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and can cause you serious problems if you do not give them proper attention.
