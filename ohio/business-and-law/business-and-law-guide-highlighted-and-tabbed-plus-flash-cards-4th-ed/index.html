State Requirements

The Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board operates within the Ohio Department of Commerce and regulates commercial contractors in its five specialty trades.

Ohio Revised Code Chapter 4740 establishes important provisions relating to construction industry licensing and professional responsibilities.

Important Ohio Contractor Licensing Requirements:

  • Appropriate Trade License: Obtain the authorization corresponding to the regulated work performed.
  • Experience Qualifications: Satisfy the applicable trade experience standards or another recognized qualification pathway.
  • Examination Application: Submit the required documentation for Board review.
  • Background Checks: Complete applicable background-check requirements.
  • Required Examinations: Complete the Business and Law examination and the technical examination for the selected trade.
  • Liability Insurance: Maintain the coverage required for the contractor license.
