State Requirements

The Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board operates within the Ohio Department of Commerce and administers licensing requirements for designated commercial specialty contractors.

Ohio contractor licensing laws include provisions in Ohio Revised Code Chapter 4740 and related administrative rules.

Important licensing responsibilities include:

  • Appropriate Trade License: Qualify for the specialty corresponding to the regulated commercial work performed.
  • Experience Qualifications: Meet the Board's applicable trade experience standards or another recognized qualification pathway.
  • Examination Approval: Complete the required application and approval procedures.
  • Background Checks: Satisfy the applicable background-check requirements.
  • Required Examinations: Complete the Business and Law examination and the examination associated with the selected trade.
  • Insurance Responsibilities: Maintain the liability insurance coverage required for licensure.
  • Professional Compliance: Follow applicable Ohio contractor licensing laws and Board regulations.

Ohio's state licensing program applies to specified commercial specialty trades. Contractors may also encounter local registration, permitting, and other requirements associated with the communities where they perform work.
