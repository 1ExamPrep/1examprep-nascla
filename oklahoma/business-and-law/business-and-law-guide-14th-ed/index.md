## Open Book Test

The **Oklahoma contractor Business and Law examinations are open-book tests**. PSI permits candidates to bring designated reference publications into the examination center.

The NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Basic 14th Edition is an approved examination reference.

Open-book testing allows candidates to consult authorized publications while answering questions. However, success requires understanding the subjects, identifying the appropriate references, and locating information efficiently.

Familiarity with the NASCLA guide's organization is therefore an important preparation skill.

**Important Oklahoma Open-Book Examination Rules:**

- Candidates are responsible for bringing their approved references to the examination center.
- Only designated reference materials may be used during testing.
- Reference materials may be highlighted or underlined before the examination.
- References may be indexed with permanent tabs.
- Temporary tabs, including removable sticky notes, are not permitted.
- Unauthorized handwritten notes and added reference content are prohibited.
- Candidates must follow PSI requirements for permitted reference markings and binding.

**Additional Oklahoma Examination References:** PSI identifies state-specific legal materials alongside the NASCLA publication. These include contractor licensing laws, Construction Industries Board regulations, lien laws, and workers' compensation provisions, depending on the examination.

Studying both general business concepts and Oklahoma-specific regulations helps candidates prepare for the different subjects covered by their examination.
