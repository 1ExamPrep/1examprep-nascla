Test Information and Study Materials

The NASCLA Basic 14th Edition is organized into three major sections that address the responsibilities involved in establishing and operating a construction business.

The first section focuses on developing and organizing a contracting business.

Topics include creating a business plan, choosing a business structure, understanding licensing and insurance responsibilities, and developing basic management and marketing skills.

These subjects provide a foundation for understanding the decisions involved in starting professional construction operations.

Part 2: Construction Business Operations

The second section addresses the practical management responsibilities associated with construction projects.

Topics include estimating, contract management, project scheduling, project management, workplace safety, environmental responsibilities, and relationships with employees, subcontractors, and customers.

These subjects help contractors understand how construction projects are planned, coordinated, and managed.

Part 3: Business Administration

The third section covers financial management, taxation fundamentals, and lien laws.

The publication's three-part structure provides a useful framework for dividing your preparation into manageable study sessions.
