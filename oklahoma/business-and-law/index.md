## Oklahoma Business & Law questions

### What is the Oklahoma NASCLA Contractor's Guide, Basic 14th Edition?

It is a contractor business, law, and project management reference published by NASCLA and identified for applicable Oklahoma Business and Law examinations.

### Is the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition an approved Oklahoma examination reference?

Yes. PSI identifies the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition as an examination reference for Oklahoma contractor Business and Law testing.

### What is the ISBN of the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition?

The ISBN-13 is 978-1-948558-39-6.

### Who administers Oklahoma contractor examinations?

PSI Services administers the examinations associated with the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board.

### How many questions are on the Oklahoma Business and Law exam?

The electrical, mechanical, and plumbing Business and Law examinations each contain 50 questions and allow 120 minutes. Passing scores vary by trade.

### What subjects does the NASCLA Basic 14th Edition cover?

The guide covers business planning, licensing, insurance, estimating, contracts, scheduling, project management, workplace safety, financial management, taxation fundamentals, and lien laws.
