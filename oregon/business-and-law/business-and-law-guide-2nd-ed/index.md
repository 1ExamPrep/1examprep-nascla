## About this product

The *Oregon NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Oregon Construction Contractors 2nd Edition* is an official NASCLA publication approved by the Oregon Construction Contractors Board (CCB) as the core reference for the Oregon contractor licensing exam. Published by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies, this guide outlines essential regulations, business organization principles, lien laws, contract requirements, bidding procedures, and safety practices specific to Oregon’s construction industry. The text supports contractor candidates preparing for the NASCLA-accredited trade and business law examinations, emphasizing Oregon statutory requirements and administrative rules governing residential and commercial construction. It is a primary study resource distributed through Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep to assist Oregon applicants seeking compliance with NASCLA standards and state licensing criteria.

The OREGON NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Oregon Construction Contractors, 2nd Edition is organized into three sections.

Part 1 Focuses on planning and starting your business. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

Part 2 Covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors and customers.

Part 3 Provides valuable information to assist you in running the administrative function of your business. Financial management, tax basics, and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and failure proper attention can cause serious problems.
