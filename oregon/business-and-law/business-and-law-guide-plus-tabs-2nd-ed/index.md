## About this product

The *Oregon NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Oregon Construction Contractors, 2nd Edition* is the approved reference for the business-and-law portion of the Oregon contractor licensing exam under the NASCLA format. The Tabs Bundle Pak edition includes the textbook plus quick-reference section tabs to facilitate faster navigation during open-book exam conditions. Content covers business entity formation, licensing and insurance obligations, bidding and estimating, contract management, scheduling, job-site safety, environmental obligations, financial controls, taxation, and lien laws, all tailored to Oregon regulations and exam scope.

In addition to receiving the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Oregon Construction Contractors 1st Edition you will also receive our quick reference tabs.

Our permanent quick reference tabs will help you navigate quickly through this book during your exam. These tabs are self-adhesive and must be added at least one day prior for sufficient adhesive power.
