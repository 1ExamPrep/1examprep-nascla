## Licensing Steps

Obtaining an Oregon Construction Contractors Board license involves completing applicable education, examination, business registration, bonding, insurance, and application requirements.

**Step 1: Identify a Responsible Managing Individual**

Select the owner or employee who will serve as the business's Responsible Managing Individual (RMI). This individual is responsible for managing or supervising construction activities.

**Step 2: Complete Required Pre-License Education**

The standard licensing pathway requires the RMI to complete 16 hours of approved pre-license education.

**Step 3: Study the Oregon NASCLA Manual**

Review the Oregon 2nd Edition and use the matching chapter tabs to organize your study of business planning, contractor laws, contracts, estimating, and project management.

**Step 4: Pass the Contractor Examination**

Follow the examination scheduling instructions and complete the Oregon CCB examination through PSI.

**Step 5: Select the Appropriate Contractor Endorsement**

Determine which residential, commercial, or other applicable endorsement corresponds to the construction activities the business intends to perform.

**Step 6: Complete Business Registration**

Register the business with the Oregon Secretary of State when required for its organizational structure or business name.

**Step 7: Obtain Bonding and Insurance**

Secure the required CCB surety bond, general liability insurance, and applicable workers' compensation coverage.

**Step 8: Submit the License Application**

Provide the required application materials, examination results, bond, insurance documentation, and other supporting records to the CCB.

**Step 9: Receive License Approval**

Complete the licensing process before performing construction work that requires a CCB contractor license.

Oregon applicants must submit their license application within two years after passing the examination.
