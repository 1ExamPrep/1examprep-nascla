About this product

The Oregon NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 2nd Edition—published by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA)—is the official reference manual recognized by the Oregon Construction Contractors Board (CCB) for contractor licensing exams. This comprehensive guide covers essential topics such as contractor business organization, estimating, contract law, lien rights, insurance, tax requirements, and project management principles aligned with Oregon state regulations. The pre-printed tabs from Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep enhance navigation for faster reference during the open-book CCB exam. This resource is integral to Oregon general, residential, and specialty contractor candidates preparing for NASCLA-based licensing evaluations, ensuring compliance with state construction laws and business practices.

Frequently asked questions

Is the NASCLA exam open book?

Yes. You bring the approved reference books, so knowing where things are in the books matters as much as knowing the material.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Real exam questions are confidential. Practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.

When will I get it?

Online courses are delivered by email after purchase. Books ship from our fulfillment center, 1 Exam Prep; shipping options are shown at checkout.

Does buying make me eligible to test?

No. NASCLA approves you to sit the exam and your state board decides on your license. This package gets you ready for the test itself.
