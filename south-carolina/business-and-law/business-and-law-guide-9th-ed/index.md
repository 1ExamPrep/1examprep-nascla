## Licensing Steps

The South Carolina Contractors Licensing Board administers commercial contractor licensing through a process involving trade qualifications, examinations, business documentation, and financial responsibility requirements.

**Step 1: Identify Your Contractor Classification**

Determine which general contractor or mechanical contractor classification corresponds to the commercial construction work you intend to perform.

**Step 2: Review Experience Requirements**

Applicants generally document at least two years of qualifying work experience performed within the preceding five years for the requested classification or subclassification. Applicable waiver and reciprocity pathways have different documentation provisions.

**Step 3: Identify Your Qualifying Party**

Determine who will serve as the Primary Qualifying Party or Qualifying Party responsible for the requested contractor classification.

**Step 4: Prepare for the Business Management and Law Examination**

Study the South Carolina Commercial NASCLA 9th Edition and become familiar with construction business planning, contracts, financial management, and professional responsibilities.

**Step 5: Complete Applicable Technical Qualification Requirements**

Meet the technical examination requirement for the requested classification or qualify through an accepted alternative pathway.

**Step 6: Complete the Business Management and Law Examination**

Register through PSI Services and pass the South Carolina Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors examination.

**Step 7: Prepare Business and Financial Documentation**

Complete applicable South Carolina business registration requirements and prepare the financial statement or surety bond documentation supporting the requested contractor group limitation.

**Step 8: Submit the Licensing Application**

Provide the required commercial contractor application, qualifying party information, examination results, and supporting materials to the Board.

**Step 9: Obtain Contractor License Approval**

Complete the applicable Board requirements before performing construction work requiring a South Carolina commercial contractor license.

Licensing approval involves satisfying both examination requirements and the professional qualifications applicable to the requested classification.
