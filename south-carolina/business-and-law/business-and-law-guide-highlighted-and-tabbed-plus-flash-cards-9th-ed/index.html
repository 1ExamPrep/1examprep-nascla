The South Carolina Contractors Licensing Board, operating through the South Carolina Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation, oversees licensing for applicable commercial general and mechanical contractors.

South Carolina Commercial Business Management and Law Examination Information:

  • Examination: Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors

Licensing Steps

South Carolina commercial contractor licensing involves meeting applicable qualification, examination, business, and financial responsibility requirements.

Step 1: Identify Your Contractor Classification

Determine which commercial general contractor or mechanical contractor classification corresponds to the construction work you intend to perform.

Step 2: Review Experience Requirements

Review the qualifying experience and documentation requirements associated with your selected classification or subclassification.

Step 3: Identify the Qualifying Party

Determine the individual who will satisfy the examination and qualification requirements associated with the contracting business.

Step 4: Prepare for Business Management and Law

Study the NASCLA South Carolina Commercial Contractors 9th Edition. Review highlighted passages, practice using the installed tabs, and reinforce concepts with the included flash cards.

Step 5: Complete Technical Qualification Requirements

Prepare for the technical examination associated with the selected classification or meet an accepted alternative qualification pathway.

Step 6: Pass the Business Management and Law Examination

Complete the applicable examination administered by PSI Services.

Step 7: Prepare Business Documentation

Complete applicable business registration requirements and prepare the financial documentation supporting the requested contractor group limitation.

Step 8: Submit the Licensing Application

Provide the required application, qualifying party information, examination results, and supporting documentation to the South Carolina Contractors Licensing Board.

Step 9: Complete Licensing Approval

Satisfy the remaining requirements before performing commercial construction work requiring a South Carolina contractor license.

The exact licensing pathway depends on the classification, requested group limitation, business organization, and applicable qualification provisions.
