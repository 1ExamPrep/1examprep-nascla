Exam Details

The South Carolina Contractors Licensing Board, operating through the South Carolina Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation, administers licensing for applicable commercial general and mechanical contractors.

PSI Services administers the South Carolina Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors examination. The NASCLA South Carolina Commercial Contractors 9th Edition is its designated reference.

South Carolina Commercial Business Management and Law Examination Information:

  • Examination: Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors
  • Licensing Authority: South Carolina Contractors Licensing Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI Services

  • Time Allowed: 125 minutes

Open Book Test

The South Carolina Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult the approved NASCLA South Carolina Commercial Contractors 9th Edition reference according to PSI examination requirements.

Open-book testing still requires preparation. Candidates need to understand the subjects, recognize relevant terminology, and locate information efficiently within the available testing time.

The included NASCLA chapter tabs help you practice navigating the manual before examination day.

Important Open-Book Examination Rules:

  • Candidates must bring their designated examination references.
  • Approved reference manuals may be highlighted or underlined.
  • Reference indexing must comply with PSI requirements.
  • Permanent tabs are permitted when properly attached and compliant with examination rules.
  • Temporary removable tabs, including sticky-note-style tabs, are not permitted.
  • Unauthorized handwritten notes and loose inserted papers are prohibited.
  • Testing personnel inspect references and determine whether materials meet admission requirements.

Reference tabs must be permanent and securely attached. Candidates should practice using their organized reference before examination day rather than relying on unfamiliar chapter locations during testing.

Licensing Steps

South Carolina commercial contractor licensing involves meeting applicable experience, qualification, examination, business, and financial responsibility requirements.

Step 1: Select Your Contractor Classification

Identify the commercial general contractor or mechanical contractor classification that corresponds to your intended construction work.

Step 2: Review Qualification Requirements

Determine the applicable experience, examination, and documentation requirements for the selected classification.

Step 3: Identify the Qualifying Party

Determine the appropriate individual responsible for meeting the business's qualifying requirements.

Step 4: Prepare for Business Management and Law

Study the NASCLA South Carolina Commercial Contractors 9th Edition and practice locating important information using the included tabs.

Step 5: Satisfy Technical Qualification Requirements

Complete the appropriate technical examination or an accepted alternative qualification pathway.

Step 6: Pass the Business Management and Law Examination

Complete the required South Carolina commercial Business Management and Law examination through PSI.

Step 7: Prepare Business Documentation

Complete applicable business registration requirements and assemble the financial documentation associated with the requested group limitation.

Step 8: Submit the License Application

Provide the required application materials, qualifying party information, examination records, and supporting documentation.

Step 9: Obtain License Approval

Complete the Board's licensing requirements before performing commercial contracting work requiring authorization.

The licensing process may differ depending on the classification, requested group limitation, and applicable qualification pathway.
