## About this product

The *South Carolina NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 8th Edition* is an official publication developed by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA) and recognized by the South Carolina Contractors Licensing Board. Used in conjunction with Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep’s materials, this guide provides comprehensive coverage of the business and law knowledge required for the South Carolina Commercial Contractors licensing examination. It includes critical sections on bidding procedures, contract administration, lien laws, tax responsibilities, financial management, safety regulations, and environmental compliance standards specific to South Carolina. The pre-printed tabs allow quick reference during open-book exam sessions, aligning with NASCLA’s standardized exam framework for multi-state contractor licensing. This resource is essential for candidates pursuing general building, mechanical, or specialty contractor licenses under South Carolina’s state-administered licensing structure through PSI Exams and NASCLA-accredited partners.
