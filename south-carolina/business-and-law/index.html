South Carolina Business & Law questions

What is the South Carolina Commercial NASCLA 9th Edition?

It is the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, South Carolina Commercial Contractors, 9th Edition, a designated reference for the South Carolina Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors examination.

How many questions are on the examination?

The South Carolina Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors examination contains 50 questions.

How long is the South Carolina commercial Business and Law exam?

Candidates have 125 minutes to complete the examination.

What score is required to pass?

The passing requirement is 35 correct answers out of 50 questions, equivalent to 70%.

Who administers South Carolina commercial contractor examinations?

PSI Services administers examinations associated with the South Carolina Contractors Licensing Board.

Does South Carolina require contractor experience?

Applicants generally document at least two years of qualifying work experience within the preceding five years for the requested commercial classification or subclassification, subject to applicable qualification exceptions.
