Exam Details

The South Carolina Residential Builders Commission, administered through the South Carolina Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation, oversees residential builder licensing and related residential specialty contractor credentials.

PSI Services administers the examinations associated with the Commission's licensing program.

South Carolina Residential Business Management and Law Examination Information:

  • Examination: Business Management and Law for Residential Builders
  • Licensing Authority: South Carolina Residential Builders Commission
  • Testing Provider: PSI Services
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Number of Scored Questions: 50 multiple-choice questions
  • Time Allowed: 120 minutes
  • Passing Requirement: 34 correct answers, equivalent to 68%
  • Approved NASCLA Reference: Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, South Carolina Residential Builders, 9th Edition

The NASCLA Residential Builders 9th Edition became an accepted examination reference on October 9, 2026, alongside the 8th Edition. Beginning April 9, 2027, the examination is scheduled to use the 9th Edition exclusively.

Open Book Test

The South Carolina Business Management and Law for Residential Builders examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult the approved NASCLA South Carolina Residential Builders reference during testing.

An open-book examination still requires preparation. Candidates must understand important construction business terminology, identify the subject of each question, and locate the relevant information efficiently.

Important Open-Book Examination Rules:

  • Candidates must bring their approved reference materials to the examination center.
  • The NASCLA South Carolina Residential Builders 9th Edition is an authorized Business Management and Law reference.
  • Approved reference books may be highlighted or underlined before testing.
  • Reference books may be indexed with permanent tabs.
  • Temporary removable tabs, including sticky notes, are not permitted.
  • Unauthorized handwritten notes and additional loose or attached papers are prohibited.
  • Reference materials must meet PSI examination-center requirements.

Reference Tab Acceptance: Please note that the Testing Facility Proctor is the deciding factor on whether or not tabs will be accepted and allowed into the exam. If the tabs do not adhere properly, they will not be accepted.

The open-book format makes learning the publication's organization particularly valuable. Familiarity with chapter locations can help candidates find information about contracts, insurance, estimating, licensing, and financial administration.

Practice identifying the correct subject and locating supporting information during your study sessions rather than relying on the reference book for the first time on examination day.

Licensing Steps

The South Carolina Residential Builders Commission administers the process for obtaining a residential builder license. Applicants generally complete qualification, examination, and application requirements before receiving authorization.

Step 1: Determine Your License Classification

Identify whether the intended work falls within residential building or another residential specialty classification regulated by the Commission.

Step 2: Review Experience Requirements

Residential builder applicants generally need one year of Commission-approved experience under the supervision of a licensed builder or qualifying contractor.

Step 3: Submit the Examination Eligibility Application

Prepare the required application and supporting experience documentation for Commission review.

Step 4: Obtain Examination Authorization

After approval, receive the examination eligibility information needed to schedule testing through PSI Services.

Step 5: Prepare for Business Management and Law

Study the NASCLA South Carolina Residential Builders 9th Edition, focusing on licensing, estimating, contracts, project management, insurance, and financial responsibilities.

Step 6: Prepare for the Residential Builder Technical Examination

Review residential construction practices and the technical references identified for the separate residential builder examination.

Step 7: Complete the Required Examinations

Complete the applicable Business Management and Law and residential builder technical examinations within the examination eligibility period.

Step 8: Complete the Licensing Application

After satisfying the examination requirements, submit the remaining application documentation and meet the Commission's licensing obligations.

Step 9: Obtain License Approval

Complete the Commission's approval process before performing residential construction work that requires a South Carolina residential builder license.

State Requirements

The South Carolina Residential Builders Commission regulates residential builders and applicable residential specialty contractors throughout the state.

The licensing program establishes professional qualifications and examination requirements for individuals seeking authorization to perform regulated residential construction work.

Important South Carolina Residential Builder Licensing Requirements:

  • Applicable Classification: Obtain the appropriate residential builder or specialty contractor credential.
  • Qualifying Experience: Demonstrate the experience required for the requested license.
  • Commission Approval: Receive examination eligibility before scheduling the required examinations.
  • Business Management and Law Examination: Pass the residential Business Management and Law examination.
  • Technical Examination: Pass the applicable residential builder technical examination unless an approved waiver applies.
  • Examination Eligibility: Complete the required examinations within the authorized eligibility period.
  • Application Documentation: Submit the required materials for licensing approval.
  • Professional Compliance: Follow South Carolina residential building laws and Commission regulations.

South Carolina residential builder applicants generally have one year of examination eligibility and up to three examination attempts within a twelve-month period. Specific requirements also apply to retesting following unsuccessful attempts.

Some applicants qualify for a residential builder technical examination waiver through an eligible existing license or recognized interstate licensing pathway. The Business Management and Law requirement remains applicable.

Residential contractor licensing is separate from the licensing administered by the South Carolina Contractors Licensing Board for commercial general and mechanical contractors.
