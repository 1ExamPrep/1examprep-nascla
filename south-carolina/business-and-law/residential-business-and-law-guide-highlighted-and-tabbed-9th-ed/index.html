The South Carolina Business Management and Law for Residential Builders examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult designated reference materials during testing, including the applicable NASCLA South Carolina Residential Builders publication.

Important Examination Reference Guidelines:

  • Bring the designated reference book to the examination center.
