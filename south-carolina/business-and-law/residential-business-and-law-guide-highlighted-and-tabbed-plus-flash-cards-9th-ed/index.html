Exam Details

The South Carolina Residential Builders Commission, operating through the South Carolina Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation, oversees residential builder licensing and applicable residential specialty contractor credentials.

PSI Services administers the examinations associated with the Commission's licensing program.

South Carolina Residential Business Management and Law Examination Information:

  • Examination: Business Management and Law for Residential Builders
  • Licensing Authority: South Carolina Residential Builders Commission
  • Testing Provider: PSI Services
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Scored Questions: 50 multiple-choice questions
  • Time Allowed: 120 minutes
  • Passing Requirement: 34 correct answers, equivalent to 68%
  • NASCLA Reference: Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, South Carolina Residential Builders, 9th Edition

Open Book Test

The South Carolina Business Management and Law for Residential Builders examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult the designated NASCLA South Carolina Residential Builders reference during testing.

Open-book testing requires preparation and familiarity with the publication. Candidates should understand important terminology, recognize examination subjects, and locate supporting information efficiently.

The professionally highlighted and tabbed NASCLA guide supports these skills by making important passages easier to review and chapters easier to locate.

Important Examination Reference Guidelines:

State Requirements

The South Carolina Residential Builders Commission regulates residential builders and applicable residential specialty contractors.

The licensing program establishes professional qualifications, examination requirements, and responsibilities associated with regulated residential construction activities.

Important South Carolina Residential Builder Licensing Responsibilities:

  • Appropriate Credential: Obtain the license or registration corresponding to the residential work performed.
  • Qualifying Experience: Meet the experience requirements for the selected classification.
  • Examination Eligibility: Complete the Commission's authorization procedures.
  • Business Management and Law: Pass the required examination.
  • Technical Knowledge: Meet the applicable technical examination requirement unless an approved alternative applies.
  • Licensing Documentation: Submit the required application materials.
  • Professional Compliance: Follow applicable South Carolina residential construction laws and Commission regulations.

Residential builder licensing is separate from South Carolina's commercial general and mechanical contractor licensing program. The appropriate examination depends on the credential being pursued.
