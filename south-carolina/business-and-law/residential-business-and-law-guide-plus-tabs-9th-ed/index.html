Exam Details

The South Carolina Residential Builders Commission, operating through the South Carolina Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation, oversees residential builder and applicable residential specialty contractor licensing.

South Carolina Residential Business Management and Law Examination Information:

Open Book Test

The South Carolina Business Management and Law for Residential Builders examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult the authorized NASCLA South Carolina Residential Builders reference during testing.

Open-book testing requires preparation and reference familiarity. Candidates need to recognize examination topics, understand important terminology, and locate relevant information efficiently.

The chapter tabs included in this bundle help you practice navigating the manual before examination day.

Important South Carolina Open-Book Examination Rules:

  • Candidates must bring their approved reference publications to the testing center.
  • The NASCLA South Carolina Residential Builders 9th Edition is an approved Business Management and Law reference.
  • Approved references may be highlighted or underlined before examination day.
  • Reference manuals may be indexed with permanent tabs.
  • Permanent tabs are defined as tabs that would tear the page if removed.
  • Temporary removable tabs, including sticky notes, are not permitted.
  • Reference books must not contain unauthorized handwritten notes or additional loose or attached papers.
  • Testing personnel inspect references for compliance with examination rules.

State Requirements

The South Carolina Residential Builders Commission regulates residential builders and applicable residential specialty contractors.

The Commission establishes examination requirements, experience qualifications, and professional responsibilities for regulated residential construction work.

Important South Carolina Residential Builder Licensing Requirements:

  • Appropriate Classification: Obtain the license or registration corresponding to the residential work performed.
  • Qualifying Experience: Document the experience required for the selected classification.
  • Commission Approval: Obtain examination eligibility before scheduling the required tests.
  • Business Management and Law Examination: Complete the required examination.
  • Technical Examination: Satisfy the technical examination requirement unless an approved waiver applies.
  • Examination Eligibility: Complete required testing within the authorized eligibility period.
  • Application Documentation: Submit all applicable licensing materials.
  • Professional Compliance: Follow South Carolina residential construction laws and Commission regulations.

Examination eligibility is generally valid for one year. Candidates are allowed three attempts within a twelve-month period; additional requirements apply following unsuccessful attempts.

Certain experienced or previously licensed applicants may qualify for a residential builder technical examination waiver. However, the Business Management and Law requirement remains applicable.

South Carolina residential builder licensing is separate from the commercial contractor licensing program administered by the South Carolina Contractors Licensing Board.
