About this product

This textbook, published by NASCLA (National Association of State Contractor Licensing Agencies) for the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission licensing exam, covers three major sections: business planning and startup (including licensing, insurance, business structure and marketing); construction business operations (estimating, contracts, scheduling, project management, safety, environmental, and workforce relationships); and administrative/financial functions (tax basics, lien laws, business finances and regulatory compliance). The edition is spiral-bound and includes pre-printed tabs to assist exam candidates in navigating the material efficiently.

Frequently asked questions

Is the NASCLA exam open book?

Yes. You bring the approved reference books, so knowing where things are in the books matters as much as knowing the material.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Real exam questions are confidential. Practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.

When will I get it?

Online courses are delivered by email after purchase. Books ship from our fulfillment center, 1 Exam Prep; shipping options are shown at checkout.

Does buying make me eligible to test?

No. NASCLA approves you to sit the exam and your state board decides on your license. This package gets you ready for the test itself.
