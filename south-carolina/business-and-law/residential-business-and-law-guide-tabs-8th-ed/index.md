## About this product

This textbook, published by NASCLA (National Association of State Contractor Licensing Agencies) for the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission licensing exam, covers three major sections: business planning and startup (including licensing, insurance, business structure and marketing); construction business operations (estimating, contracts, scheduling, project management, safety, environmental, and workforce relationships); and administrative/financial functions (tax basics, lien laws, business finances and regulatory compliance).
