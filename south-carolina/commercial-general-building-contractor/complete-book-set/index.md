## Open Book Test

The South Carolina NASCLA Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors is an open-book test. Candidates may consult approved references during the examination, subject to the current examination-provider rules.

Open-book testing does not provide enough time to learn an unfamiliar collection of more than twenty references during the exam. Candidates should know the purpose of every book, understand how each reference is organized, and practice locating information before test day.

Begin by reviewing the table of contents and index in each reference. Group the books by subject so the correct source can be selected quickly. Helpful categories include building code, accessibility, safety, business, project management, concrete, steel, wood framing, masonry, roofing, excavation, building systems, and environmental compliance.

For code and standard questions, practice using definitions, chapter headings, tables, exceptions, commentary, figures, notes, and cross-references. Read enough surrounding material to determine whether a limitation or related provision affects the answer.

For construction-management questions, identify whether the subject involves planning, scheduling, estimating, procurement, documentation, supervision, risk, quality control, or closeout before choosing a reference.

For structural questions, determine whether the topic involves cast-in-place concrete, precast concrete, post-tensioning, reinforcing steel, steel joists, steel deck, masonry, or wood trusses. Selecting the correct technical guide is the first step toward locating the answer efficiently.

For trade-method questions, use the carpentry, roofing, excavation, masonry, gypsum, and commercial construction references to review accepted construction practices, materials, installation methods, and field procedures.

A practical open-book method begins by reading the entire question. Identify the main subject, select the most likely reference, use the index or chapter structure, locate the applicable information, review the surrounding section, and compare the source with the available answer choices.

Candidates should complete timed navigation exercises using the complete reference package. Record which books and subjects take too long to search, then repeat those areas until the location process becomes more familiar.

Testing-center rules may address highlighting, tabs, notes, attachments, loose materials, book condition, and permitted tools. Candidates are responsible for reviewing and following the current examination requirements.
