South Carolina Commercial GC questions

Does South Carolina Accept the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor Exam?

Yes. South Carolina participates in the NASCLA Commercial Exam program, and the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination can be applied toward the technical examination requirement for the applicable Unlimited Building classification pathway.

How Many Questions Are on the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor Exam?

The examination contains 115 questions. Candidates are allowed 330 minutes and need at least 81 correct answers to achieve the 70% passing score.

Does Passing NASCLA Automatically Give Me a South Carolina Contractor License?

No. The NASCLA examination satisfies an accepted technical examination component. Applicants must still complete the South Carolina qualifying-party, Business Management and Law, experience, financial, application, and other applicable licensing requirements.

Does South Carolina Require a Business Management and Law Exam?

Yes. South Carolina requires individuals to pass the South Carolina Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors Exam before requesting qualifying-party certification.

Why Are Highlighted and Tabbed Books Helpful for an Open-Book Exam?

Highlighting and tabs can make important sections easier to identify and navigate. Their greatest value comes from using them regularly during preparation so you become familiar with the structure of each reference before test day.

Please allow up to 15 business days for tabbed and highlighted book package orders.
