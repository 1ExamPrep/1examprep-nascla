Quick answers

Does South Carolina accept the NASCLA exam?

Yes. South Carolina accepts the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors in place of its state trade exam. You still apply to the South Carolina board and meet its other requirements, which usually include a business and law exam.

Which NASCLA exams can I prepare for in South Carolina?

Commercial General Building Contractor (7 products from $199.99); Master Electrical Contractor (1 products from $1,995.00); Residential Electrical Contractor (5 products from $295.00); Business and Law (9 products from $19.95). Each has its own page with the books, tabbed sets, rentals and online courses for that exam.

How do I register for the NASCLA exam?

Apply through NASCLA at ned.nascla.org. You must be pre-approved before you can schedule, and then you apply to the South Carolina board with your passing record.
