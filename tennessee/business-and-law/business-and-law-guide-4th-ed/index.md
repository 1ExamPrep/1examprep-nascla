## Licensing Steps

The Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors administers licensing requirements for contractors working within regulated construction classifications.

The licensing process involves determining the appropriate classification, satisfying examination requirements, preparing business documentation, and obtaining Board approval.

**Step 1: Identify Your Contractor Classification**

Determine which Tennessee contractor classification corresponds to the construction services your business intends to provide.

**Step 2: Review Licensing Requirements**

Study the Board's requirements for the selected classification, including applicable examinations, financial responsibility, and business documentation.

**Step 3: Identify the Qualifying Agent**

Determine who will take the required examination or examinations on behalf of the contractor business. Tennessee recognizes qualifying agents who meet the Board's applicable requirements.

**Step 4: Prepare for Business and Law**

Study the NASCLA Tennessee 4th Edition, focusing on contractor licensing, estimating, contracts, project management, insurance, and financial administration.

**Step 5: Prepare for the Trade Examination**

Complete the technical preparation required for contractor classifications that involve a separate trade examination.

**Step 6: Complete the Required Examinations**

Register with the authorized examination provider and pass the applicable Business and Law and trade examinations.

**Step 7: Prepare Financial and Business Records**

Assemble the financial statements, organizational information, and other materials required for the requested contractor license.

**Step 8: Submit the Contractor Application**

Complete the Board's application process and provide the examination information and supporting documentation.

**Step 9: Obtain Licensing Approval**

Satisfy the remaining Board requirements before performing construction work requiring a Tennessee contractor license.

Some Tennessee contractor classifications require additional examination preapproval. The licensing process depends on the classification and type of construction work involved.
