## Licensing Steps

The Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors administers licensing requirements for contractors working within regulated construction classifications.

The process involves identifying the appropriate classification, meeting examination requirements, preparing business documentation, and obtaining Board approval.

**Step 1: Determine Your Contractor Classification**

Identify the Tennessee contractor classification corresponding to the construction services your business intends to provide.

**Step 2: Review Applicable Requirements**

Determine which examinations, financial documentation, and business qualifications apply to your classification.

**Step 3: Identify the Qualifying Agent**

Designate the individual responsible for completing the required examination or examinations on behalf of the contractor business.

**Step 4: Prepare for the Business and Law Examination**

Study the highlighted NASCLA Tennessee 4th Edition, focusing on licensing, estimating, contracts, safety, labor law, and financial responsibilities.

**Step 5: Prepare for Any Required Trade Examination**

Review the technical subjects and authorized references associated with the selected contractor classification.

**Step 6: Complete the Required Examinations**

Schedule and complete the applicable examinations with the authorized testing provider.

**Step 7: Prepare Financial and Business Documentation**

Assemble the required financial statements, business organization information, and supporting materials.

**Step 8: Submit the Contractor License Application**

Complete the application and provide examination results and other required documentation to the Board.

**Step 9: Complete the Board Approval Process**

Satisfy the remaining licensing requirements before performing construction activities requiring a Tennessee contractor license.

Certain mechanical and plumbing examination classifications require Board preapproval before testing. Requirements depend on the classification requested.
