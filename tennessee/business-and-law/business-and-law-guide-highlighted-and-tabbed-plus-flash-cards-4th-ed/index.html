Exam Details

The Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors, within the Tennessee Department of Commerce and Insurance, oversees contractor licensing for regulated construction activities.

Contractor applicants generally complete the Tennessee Contractor Business and Law examination. Certain classifications also require a separate technical trade examination.

Open Book Test

The Tennessee Contractor Business and Law examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult the authorized NASCLA Tennessee 4th Edition reference during testing.

Prov permits highlighting and permanent tabs in authorized reference books. Movable tabs and Post-it notes are prohibited, and candidates may not mark the reference during testing.

Important Tennessee Open-Book Examination Guidelines:

  • Bring the authorized NASCLA Tennessee 4th Edition reference.
  • Become familiar with the table of contents and chapter organization before examination day.
  • Highlighting is allowed in approved reference publications.
  • Permanent tabs must comply with examination-center requirements.
  • Movable tabs and sticky notes are not permitted.
  • Marking reference materials during the examination is prohibited.
  • Reference books are subject to inspection by testing personnel.
  • Only authorized examination materials may be used in the testing room.

The Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors administers contractor licensing through a process involving classification selection, examinations, business documentation, and Board approval.

State Requirements

The Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors regulates contractor licensing and establishes professional requirements for construction businesses operating within applicable classifications.

Licensing obligations depend on the type of construction work, the requested classification, and the contractor's business structure.

Important Tennessee Contractor Licensing Responsibilities:

  • Appropriate Classification: Obtain authorization corresponding to the construction work performed.
  • Business and Law Examination: Meet the applicable examination requirement.
  • Trade Examination: Complete a technical examination when required.
  • Qualifying Agent: Identify an individual who meets the relevant examination qualification requirements.
  • Business Documentation: Provide the required organizational and registration information.
  • Financial Responsibility: Submit financial information associated with the requested licensing authorization.
  • Insurance: Meet applicable insurance requirements.
  • Professional Compliance: Follow Tennessee contractor licensing laws and Board regulations.

Tennessee recognizes contractor classifications covering residential, commercial, industrial, mechanical, electrical, and other construction activities.

Certain mechanical and plumbing examination classifications require Board preapproval before testing. Contractors must follow the procedures associated with their intended classifications.
