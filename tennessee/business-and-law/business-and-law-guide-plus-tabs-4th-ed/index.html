Open Book Test

The Tennessee Contractor Business and Law examination is an open-book test. Candidates may bring the authorized NASCLA Tennessee 4th Edition reference to the examination center.

Prov permits highlighting and permanent tabs in authorized reference books. Movable tabs, Post-it notes, and marking the reference during testing are not permitted.

Because the examination is timed, understanding the material and knowing where to find important information are valuable preparation skills.

Important Tennessee Open-Book Examination Guidelines:

  • Bring the authorized NASCLA Tennessee 4th Edition publication to the examination center.
  • Become familiar with the table of contents and chapter organization before testing.
  • Highlighting is permitted under the testing provider's reference rules.
  • Permanent reference tabs are allowed when they meet examination requirements.
  • Movable tabs and Post-it notes are prohibited.
  • Do not mark or write in the reference during testing.
  • Follow the examination center's reference inspection procedures.
  • Use only materials authorized for the specific examination.
