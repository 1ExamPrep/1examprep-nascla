## Exam Details

NASCLA developed its electrical examinations using occupational analysis and input from electrical subject matter experts to address knowledge and skills associated with electrical installation, maintenance, repair, troubleshooting, safety, and code compliance.

The NASCLA electrical program includes examinations for Electrical Contractors or Master Electricians, Journeyman Electricians, and Residential Electrical Contractors. These examinations are intended to provide participating regulatory agencies with a standardized electrical trade-testing option.

## Licensing Steps

Tennessee regulates electrical work through several licensing pathways. A NASCLA examination result by itself does not automatically authorize electrical work throughout Tennessee.

1. **Determine which Tennessee electrical credential applies.** Identify whether the work requires a Tennessee CE Electrical Contractor license, a Limited Licensed Electrician credential, or a license required by a local government.
2. **Review the requirements for that credential.** State contractor licensing, LLE licensing, and local electrical licensing each have separate requirements and scopes of authority.
3. **Prepare for the applicable trade examination.** Study electrical code requirements, theory, calculations, workplace safety, troubleshooting, wiring methods, equipment, and specialty electrical systems.
4. **Complete the required examination registration.** Follow the application and scheduling instructions associated with the examination and licensing pathway being pursued.
5. **Take the applicable examination.** Follow all testing rules concerning identification, authorized books, calculators, permanent tabs, and prohibited materials.
6. **Complete the licensing application.** Submit the forms, fees, documentation, and other information required by the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors or applicable local licensing authority.
7. **Receive the appropriate credential before performing regulated work.** Electrical work should be performed only within the scope authorized by the license or credential held.
8. **Maintain the license or credential.** Follow the renewal and compliance requirements that apply to the specific Tennessee or local electrical license.

## State Requirements

The **Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors** regulates contractors and Limited Licensed Electricians at the state level. Tennessee's electrical licensing structure differs from states that issue one statewide journeyman electrician license covering every jurisdiction.

A Tennessee contractor license is generally required before bidding, offering to perform, or contracting for construction projects of **$25,000 or more**. Electrical subcontractors are also subject to contractor licensing requirements when the electrical portion of a qualifying project reaches the applicable $25,000 threshold.

Tennessee's full electrical contractor classification is **CE – Electrical Contracting**. The full CE classification requires a trade examination and covers electrical work for buildings and structures. Tennessee's contractor licensing process also requires new contractor applicants to pass the Tennessee Business and Law examination.

Tennessee also issues the **Limited Licensed Electrician, or LLE**, credential. The LLE applies to electricians performing work in municipalities that use the Tennessee Division of Fire Prevention for permits or inspections and covers qualifying projects costing less than $25,000.

Local requirements remain important. Tennessee directs electricians to check with the local government where the work will be performed because a municipality may accept the state LLE license or may require its own local electrical license for permits and inspections.

The LLE license is issued to individuals who pass the applicable LLE trade examination and complete the state's application process. The LLE examination is separate from a NASCLA Journeyman Electrician examination and from Tennessee's CE Electrical Contractor examination.

For contractor examinations, Tennessee changed trade examination providers in 2026. Beginning **July 1, 2026**, Prov began administering trade examinations for the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors. New trade-exam scheduling beginning on that date is handled through Prov. The Tennessee Board's contractor examinations are open book.

Contractor applicants should also understand the role of the Qualifying Agent. The Qualifying Agent is the individual designated to take the applicable examination for the entity obtaining the contractor license. The person taking the Business and Law examination does not have to be the same person who takes the required trade examination.

A Tennessee contractor license is issued with both a classification and a monetary limit. The classification determines the type of work the contractor may perform, while the monetary limit controls the maximum project amount for which the contractor is authorized to contract.

These distinctions are important when preparing for an electrical examination. A journeyman-level electrical exam may demonstrate technical knowledge, but candidates must still identify and obtain the specific Tennessee state or local credential required for their intended scope of work.
