## UTAH-NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Utah 4th Edition

The book titled “UTAH-NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Utah 4th Edition” (ISBN 978-1-948558-15-0) published by the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA) is the designated reference for the Utah Division of Occupational and Professional Licensing exam and covers three core sections: (1) business planning and startup including licensing, insurance and business structure; (2) construction-business fundamentals such as estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities; and (3) administrative operations including financial management, tax basics and lien law.

Our tabbed and highlighted exam ready books are skillfully tabbed and highlighted by professionals. Your book will come affixed with permanent quick reference tabs printed with the chapter titles and other important sections to help you quickly and easily navigate through the book during your exam. Key areas will be vibrantly highlighted so you know what subjects are relevant while saving time preparing for your exam.

The *NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management *is organized into 3 sections.

**Part 1** focuses on planning and starting your business. This section will help you formulate a business plan, choose a business structure, understand licensing and insurance requirements and gain basic management and marketing skills.

**Part 2** covers fundamentals you will need to know in order to operate a successful construction business. This section covers estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities, and building good relationships with employees, subcontractors, and customers.

**Part 3** provides valuable information to assist you in running the administrative functions of your business. Financial management, tax basics, and lien laws are covered. Effective management of these areas of business is vital and can cause you serious problems if you do not give them proper attention.
