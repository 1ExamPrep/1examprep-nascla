The Utah Division of Professional Licensing administers contractor licensing through requirements involving classification selection, education, professional qualifications, examinations, business documentation, and licensing approval.

Step 2: Review Qualification Requirements

Step 3: Complete Applicable Pre-Licensure Education

Step 4: Prepare for Business and Law

Step 5: Prepare for Any Additional Examination

Step 6: Complete Required Testing

Step 7: Organize Business Documentation

Satisfy the remaining requirements before performing construction activities requiring a Utah contractor license.
