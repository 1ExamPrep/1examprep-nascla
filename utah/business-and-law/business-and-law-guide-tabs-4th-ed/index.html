About this product

The Utah NASCLA Contractor's Guide to Business, Law, and Project Management, 4th Edition; Pre-Printed Tabs by Pass Your NASCLA Exam Prep is a companion reference tool designed for use with the official NASCLA Business and Law Manual approved by the Utah Division of Occupational and Professional Licensing (DOPL). These pre-printed tabs correspond to key chapters such as business organization, licensing requirements, contract law, financial management, labor regulations, and safety compliance. They provide efficient navigation for open-book NASCLA contractor examinations, ensuring quick access to vital information during testing. Frequently asked questions

Is the NASCLA exam open book?

Yes. You bring the approved reference books, so knowing where things are in the books matters as much as knowing the material.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Real exam questions are confidential. Practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.

When will I get it?

Online courses are delivered by email after purchase. Books ship from our fulfillment center, 1 Exam Prep; shipping options are shown at checkout.

Does buying make me eligible to test?

No. NASCLA approves you to sit the exam and your state board decides on your license. This package gets you ready for the test itself.
