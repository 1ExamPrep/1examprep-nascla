Open Book Test

The Virginia Class A and Class B Contractor Business examinations are open-book tests. Candidates may consult authorized reference publications during testing, subject to PSI's examination-center requirements.

An open-book examination still requires careful preparation. Candidates need to understand construction business terminology, recognize which subject a question addresses, and locate relevant information efficiently.

The Virginia NASCLA 11th Edition supports these skills by organizing business, law, and project management subjects into a structured reference.

Important Virginia Open-Book Examination Rules:

  • Bring the authorized reference materials for the examination portions being taken.
  • Become familiar with each reference's table of contents and chapter organization before testing.
  • Approved references may be highlighted or underlined before the examination.
  • Reference materials may be indexed with permanent tabs.
  • Removable tabs, including temporary index flags, are not permitted.
  • Reference materials containing unauthorized writing are not allowed.
  • Candidates may not write in reference books during the examination.
  • Testing personnel inspect reference materials for compliance with examination rules.

Important Reference Tab Notice: The Testing Facility Proctor makes the final determination regarding whether reference tabs are acceptable. Tabs that do not adhere properly may be rejected.

Licensing Steps

The Virginia Board for Contractors administers contractor licensing through requirements involving business qualifications, pre-license education, examinations, financial documentation, and professional classifications.

Step 1: Identify Your Contractor License Class

Determine whether your business requires a Virginia Class A, Class B, or Class C contractor license based on the applicable licensing criteria.

Step 2: Select Your Classification or Specialty

Identify the classifications and specialty designations corresponding to the construction services your business intends to perform.

Step 3: Complete Pre-License Education

New Virginia contractor license applicants must complete eight hours of Board-approved pre-license education through an eligible member of responsible management or Designated Employee.

Step 4: Identify the Designated Employee

For Class A and Class B licensing, designate a qualified individual to complete the applicable business examinations.

Step 5: Prepare for the Business Examinations

Study the Virginia NASCLA 11th Edition and the other references designated for the required examination portions.

Step 6: Complete Required Examinations

Schedule and complete the applicable examinations through PSI Services before submitting the license application.

Step 7: Identify Qualified Individuals

Provide an appropriate Qualified Individual for each classification or specialty requested by the contractor business.

Step 8: Prepare Business and Financial Documentation

Gather the organizational information, financial verification, and other materials required for the selected license class.

Step 9: Submit the Contractor License Application

Complete the application process through Virginia DPOR and satisfy the Board's remaining requirements before performing regulated construction work.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors establishes licensing requirements for construction businesses operating in the Commonwealth.

Virginia contractor licenses include two important components: the license class, which addresses the monetary scope of authorized projects, and the classification or specialty, which determines the work the business may perform.

Important Virginia Contractor Licensing Requirements:

  • License Class: Obtain the appropriate Class A, Class B, or Class C license.
  • Classification or Specialty: Identify the construction activities the business intends to perform.
  • Pre-License Education: Complete eight hours of Board-approved education for a new contractor license.
  • Designated Employee: Identify a qualifying individual for Class A or Class B business examinations.
  • Business Examinations: Satisfy the examination requirements applicable to the selected license class.
  • Qualified Individuals: Meet the applicable qualifications for each requested classification or specialty.
  • Financial Verification: Complete applicable financial documentation requirements for Class A and Class B licenses.
  • Business Documentation: Submit the required contractor firm information and application materials.
  • Professional Compliance: Follow Virginia contractor licensing laws and Board regulations.

Class A applicants complete the Virginia, General, and Advanced business examination portions. Class B applicants complete the Virginia and General portions. Class C applicants do not take the Designated Employee business examinations.

All three license classes require the applicable pre-license education for new contractor licenses.

Virginia also distinguishes between the Designated Employee, who satisfies the business examination requirement for Class A or B licensing, and the Qualified Individual, who establishes the qualifications associated with particular contractor classifications and specialties.

Understanding these distinctions is important when preparing a contractor licensing application.
