Exam Details

The Virginia Board for Contractors, through the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation, oversees licensing for construction businesses operating within the Commonwealth.

Virginia recognizes Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses. The business examination requirements differ according to the license class.

For Class A and Class B licensing, a qualifying Designated Employee completes the applicable contractor business examination portions administered by PSI Services.

Virginia Contractor Business Examination Information:

  • Licensing Authority: Virginia Board for Contractors
  • Testing Provider: PSI Services
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Class A Business Examination: Virginia, General, and Advanced portions
  • Class B Business Examination: Virginia and General portions
  • Class C: No Designated Employee business examination requirement
  • NASCLA Reference: Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Virginia, 11th Edition

The Virginia portion evaluates knowledge of state-specific contractor laws and regulations. The General portion addresses important construction business responsibilities, while the Advanced portion covers additional business and project administration subjects for Class A licensing.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Class A and Class B contractor business examinations are open-book tests. Candidates may consult authorized examination references during testing, subject to PSI requirements.

Open-book testing still requires preparation. Candidates must recognize relevant terminology, understand the subject of each question, and locate supporting information efficiently.

The highlighted and tabbed NASCLA manual supports these skills through emphasized passages and convenient chapter indexing.

Important Virginia Open-Book Examination Guidelines:

  • Bring the authorized reference publications for the examination portions being taken.
  • Reference books are not supplied at the testing center.
  • Approved references may be highlighted or underlined before testing.
  • Permanent indexing tabs may be used when they comply with examination requirements.
  • Temporary removable tabs and loose reference papers are prohibited.
  • Unauthorized handwritten notes are not permitted in examination references.
  • Candidates may not write in their reference books during testing.
  • Testing personnel inspect authorized materials before admission.

Licensing Steps

The Virginia Board for Contractors administers licensing through requirements involving business qualifications, pre-license education, examinations, financial documentation, and contractor classifications.

Step 1: Determine Your Contractor License Class

Identify whether your construction business requires a Virginia Class A, Class B, or Class C contractor license.

Step 2: Select Your Classification or Specialty

Determine which construction classifications and specialties correspond to the work your business intends to perform.

Step 3: Complete Pre-License Education

Complete the required eight-hour Board-approved pre-license education program through an eligible individual associated with the business.

Step 4: Identify the Designated Employee

Step 8: Prepare Business Documentation

Organize required business registration, financial verification, and supporting application information.

Step 9: Submit the Contractor License Application

Complete the application through Virginia DPOR and satisfy the Board's remaining requirements before performing regulated construction work.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates contractor licensing and establishes requirements for construction businesses performing regulated activities throughout the Commonwealth.

Virginia contractor licenses are classified as Class A, Class B, or Class C. Each license also identifies the classifications or specialties in which the business is authorized to perform work.

Important Virginia Contractor Licensing Requirements:

  • Appropriate License Class: Obtain the Class A, Class B, or Class C license corresponding to the business's activities.
  • Contractor Classification: Identify the construction specialties associated with the work performed.
  • Pre-License Education: Satisfy the applicable Board-approved education requirement.
  • Designated Employee: Meet the business examination requirements associated with Class A or Class B licensing.
  • Qualified Individual: Establish the qualifications required for the requested classifications or specialties.
  • Financial Responsibility: Meet the financial verification requirements associated with the selected license class.
  • Business Documentation: Provide required organizational and application information.
  • Professional Compliance: Follow Virginia contractor licensing laws and Board regulations.

Class A applicants prepare for the Virginia, General, and Advanced business examination portions. Class B applicants prepare for the Virginia and General portions.

Class C applicants do not take the Designated Employee business examinations, although the applicable pre-license education and other licensing responsibilities remain.

Virginia distinguishes between a Designated Employee, who satisfies the business examination requirements, and a Qualified Individual, who establishes technical qualifications for contractor classifications or specialties.

Understanding these roles helps construction businesses organize their licensing responsibilities and prepare appropriate applications.
