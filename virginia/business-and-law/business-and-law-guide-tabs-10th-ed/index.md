## About this product

The NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management – Virginia 10th Edition is the official reference for the National Association of State Contractors Licensing Agencies (NASCLA) and meets the requirements for the Virginia Board for Contractors (administered by PSI Services) licensing exam. According to NASCLA’s bookstore listing, the book is structured in three parts: Part 1 covers business planning, choosing a business structure, licensing and insurance; Part 2 focuses on estimating, contract management, scheduling, project management, safety and environmental responsibilities; and Part 3 addresses administrative functions such as financial management, tax basics, and lien laws.
