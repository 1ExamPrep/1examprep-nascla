## About this package

The Virginia NASCLA Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors Complete Book Set provides the full collection of approved trade references required for the NASCLA-accredited commercial general building contractor licensing exam in Virginia. The set covers structural design concepts, concrete and masonry standards, carpentry and framing methods, roofing systems, sitework and excavation practices, OSHA safety requirements, project management, estimating and bidding procedures, and contractor business and law fundamentals. Aligned with One Exam Prep’s structured exam-prep format, the collection supplies the technical criteria and regulatory guidance needed for performing compliant commercial building construction throughout the state.

### Is the NASCLA exam open book?

Yes. You bring the approved reference books, so knowing where things are in the books matters as much as knowing the material.

### Are these the actual exam questions?
