## Virginia Commercial GC questions

### Does Virginia Accept the NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor Exam?

Yes. Virginia participates in the NASCLA Commercial Exam Program, and the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors is accepted for the comparable Virginia Commercial Building classification.

### Does Passing NASCLA Automatically Give Me a Virginia Contractor License?

No. Passing NASCLA can satisfy the applicable trade examination component, but the contractor business must still complete Virginia's licensing requirements, including the applicable Qualified Individual, Designated Employee, pre-license education, business examination, classification, and application requirements.

### What are Virginia's contractor license classes?

Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses. The class determines the monetary level of projects the contractor may undertake.

### What business exams are required for a Virginia Class A contractor?

The Designated Employee for a Class A contractor must complete the Advanced, General, and Virginia examination portions.

### What business exams are required for Class B?

The Designated Employee for a Class B contractor must complete the General and Virginia examination portions.

### Does Virginia require pre-license education?

Yes. New contractor applicants must complete the Board-approved pre-license education requirement through an eligible member of the applicant organization.
