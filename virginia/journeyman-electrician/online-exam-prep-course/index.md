## State Requirements

Virginia Journeyman Electrician licensing is administered through the **Virginia Board for Contractors** within the Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation. The Board regulates individual tradesmen engaged in electrical, plumbing, HVAC, gas fitting, and other regulated trades.

Virginia provides several qualification combinations for applicants seeking a Journeyman tradesman license by examination. One pathway requires **four years of practical experience in the trade plus 240 hours of formal vocational training**.

Another pathway allows **five years of practical experience plus 160 hours of formal vocational training**. Applicants may also qualify with **six years of practical experience plus 80 hours of formal vocational training**, or **seven or more years of practical experience plus 40 hours of formal vocational training**.

Virginia also recognizes qualifying education-based pathways. An applicant may qualify with an associate degree or certificate from at least a two-year tradesman-related program from an accredited community college or technical school together with **two years of practical trade experience**.

A qualifying bachelor's degree or certificate of completion from an accredited college or university in an engineering curriculum related to the trade can also be used with the applicable practical-experience requirement.

Journeyman applicants who successfully complete an approved apprenticeship program may qualify through that pathway. Virginia's application also provides procedures for applicants who completed an apprenticeship program in another state, provided the required documentation from the regulating entity and apprenticeship sponsor is submitted.

Applicants must be at least **18 years old**. Virginia requires supporting documentation that verifies the experience, training, education, or apprenticeship pathway being used to qualify for licensure.

Virginia also requires pre-license education for tradesmen. Candidates should complete the Board-approved education requirements that apply to their licensing method before the license can be issued.

After licensure, Virginia Journeyman Electricians are subject to continuing-education requirements. Electricians must complete an approved **three-hour continuing-education course** in the applicable electrical code as a condition of renewal.

Virginia Journeyman Electrician licensure applies to an individual tradesman. Contractor business licensing is a separate matter. An individual who performs work that requires a Virginia contractor license must also comply with the Board for Contractors' business licensing rules.

Special occupancies, special equipment, communication systems, and fire alarm systems should also receive attention. These subjects may involve code sections that candidates encounter less frequently, making advance navigation practice especially valuable.
