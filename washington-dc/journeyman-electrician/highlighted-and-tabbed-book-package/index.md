The NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program offers standardized examinations for participating electrical licensing agencies. The program includes examinations for Electrical Contractors or Master Electricians, Journeyman Electricians, and Residential Electrical Contractors.

The District of Columbia Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection is listed by NASCLA as an accepting agency for the **Journeyman Electrician examination**. An accepting jurisdiction can recognize the applicable NASCLA examination in place of its state- or jurisdiction-specific trade examination while continuing to enforce its own licensing requirements.

## State Requirements

Electricians in Washington DC are regulated through the **District of Columbia Board of Industrial Trades**. The Board oversees electrician apprentice, journeyman, master, and contractor license categories along with several other regulated industrial trades.

District law provides several qualification pathways for a journeyman electrician. One pathway requires an applicant to have worked as an apprentice electrician for at least **8,000 hours over at least four years**. The District also recognizes an education-based pathway for applicants who graduate from an accredited college or university with a degree in electrical engineering and obtain at least two years of practical electrical experience certified by a licensed master electrician.

District law also requires a journeyman electrician applicant, except where another statutory provision applies, to establish completion of an approved class addressing the applicable District electrical code or an equivalent code within the required period before submitting the application.

The Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection currently identifies several materials associated with electrician applications, including **employment verification letters, letters of good standing, NASCLA accreditation, and valid state-issued electrician licenses**. All applicants are also instructed to provide a 2-inch by 2-inch passport-style photograph, government-issued identification, and payment information.

The current District electrician application fee is **$65**. The current journeyman electrician license fee is **$110** for a license term of up to two years. The District also lists a temporary journeyman electrician license at $110 for a term of up to 60 days.

Washington DC electrician licenses currently expire on **November 30 in odd-numbered years**. The current journeyman renewal fee is $110.

The District also maintains an electrician reciprocity pathway for qualifying Journeyman and Master Electricians licensed in **Maryland or Virginia**. For reciprocity, the Maryland or Virginia license must have been obtained by examination and must remain current, active, and in good standing. Reciprocity is a separate pathway from NASCLA examination acceptance.

Electrical work must be performed within the scope allowed by the District license or registration held and in accordance with applicable permitting, inspection, supervision, and construction-code requirements.
