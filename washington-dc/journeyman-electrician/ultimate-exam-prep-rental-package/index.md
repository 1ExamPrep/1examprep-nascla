Open-book testing makes reference familiarity an important part of preparation. ## State Requirements

One qualification pathway for a Washington DC journeyman electrician requires at least **8,000 hours of apprentice electrician experience completed over at least four years**. Washington DC also maintains a reciprocity pathway for qualifying Journeyman and Master Electricians licensed in **Maryland or Virginia**. Electrical work performed in Washington DC must remain within the scope authorized by the credential held and comply with applicable permitting, inspection, supervision, and District construction-code requirements.
