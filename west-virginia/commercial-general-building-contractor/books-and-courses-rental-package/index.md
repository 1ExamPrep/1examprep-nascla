## State Requirements

Contractor licensing in West Virginia is administered by the **West Virginia Contractor Licensing Board** with support from the West Virginia Division of Labor. Contractor licenses are issued according to classifications that correspond with the type of construction work being performed.

West Virginia requires all contractors to pass the state's **Business and Law examination** in addition to applicable trade examinations. The Business and Law examination addresses business management and the legal responsibilities involved in establishing and operating a construction business, including contractor laws and regulations.

West Virginia uses ProV as its contractor licensing examination testing agent. Candidates preparing for a West Virginia-specific examination should follow the current ProV candidate bulletin for the examination they intend to take and use the approved references identified for that examination.

West Virginia is also a participating jurisdiction in the NASCLA Accredited Examination Program. Candidates who pass the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors can use their NASCLA examination result through the participating-state process instead of retaking the equivalent state-specific trade examination.

A NASCLA passing result is an examination credential rather than a contractor license. Candidates must still apply for the West Virginia contractor license and satisfy the additional state requirements applicable to the requested classification.

The West Virginia General Building Contractor classification applies to contractors whose principal business involves structures built for the support, shelter, or enclosure of people, animals, movable property, or other qualifying uses and requires work involving more than two contractor classifications or supervision of such construction. Specialized electrical, plumbing, HVAC, piping, or general engineering work may require additional applicable classifications.
