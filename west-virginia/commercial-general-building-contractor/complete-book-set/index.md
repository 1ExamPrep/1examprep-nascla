## Open Book Test

The NASCLA commercial general building examination is open book. Candidates may use approved references in accordance with current exam rules. Open book does not mean the examination is easy or that every answer can be located quickly without preparation.

This reference set is extensive. Candidates who are unfamiliar with the books may spend too much time searching, choose the wrong title, or locate related information without finding the complete answer.

Begin by grouping the books into major subject areas. Create separate categories for business law, project management, building code, safety, concrete, steel, masonry, carpentry, roofing, accessibility, excavation, environmental compliance, and mechanical and electrical systems.

Practice identifying the subject of a question before opening a book. A concrete design question may belong in ACI 318-19. A reinforcing placement question may belong in Placing Reinforcing Bars. A jobsite safety question may require the OSHA reference. A commercial code question may belong in the 2021 International Building Code.

Do not rely on one familiar phrase or isolated sentence. A correct answer may depend on a definition, table note, exception, illustration, related section, or surrounding paragraph.

Use the table of contents, index, chapter headings, section numbers, figures, and appendices in each reference. Repeated practice with these navigation tools can improve speed and accuracy.

Large open-book exams reward organized preparation. Candidates should develop a system for quickly selecting the correct reference and confirming the complete answer before making a final choice.

Use only tabs, highlighting, notes, markings, or book-preparation methods permitted by current Prov and NASCLA rules. Review current candidate instructions before modifying or bringing any reference into the testing room.
