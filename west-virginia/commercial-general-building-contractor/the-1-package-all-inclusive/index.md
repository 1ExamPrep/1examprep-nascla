## Exam Details

West Virginia requires contractor applicants to pass the **Business and Law examination** in addition to an applicable trade examination. Contractors pursuing the General Building classification may satisfy the trade component through an accepted examination pathway such as the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors.

The NASCLA commercial examination evaluates a broad range of commercial construction knowledge. Candidates should be prepared for subjects involving planning and estimating, construction management, safety, sitework, concrete, masonry, metals, wood construction, building envelopes, accessibility, finishes, specialty systems, and other general building topics.

The NASCLA examination contains **115 questions**. Candidates must answer at least **81 questions correctly** for the required 70% passing score. Candidates receive **330 minutes, or 5 hours and 30 minutes**, to complete the examination.

West Virginia's separate **Business and Law examination** contains **50 questions** and provides **3 hours** for completion. Current subject areas include:

- Business Organization
- Contracts
- Estimating and Bidding
- Financial Management
- Labor Law
- Licensing
- Lien Law
- Project Management
- Risk Management
- Safety and OSHA
- Tax Law

West Virginia's current contractor examination program is administered by Prov on behalf of the Contractor Licensing Board. Candidates should approach the Business and Law test separately from the NASCLA technical examination because each exam measures a different part of contractor competency.

NASCLA preparation focuses primarily on commercial construction knowledge and reference navigation. West Virginia Business and Law preparation focuses on operating a construction business within the legal and regulatory framework that applies to contractors in the state.

## Licensing Steps

West Virginia contractor licensing is administered by the Contractor Licensing Board with support from the West Virginia Division of Labor. Passing an examination is only one part of the licensing process.

1. **Choose the appropriate contractor classification.** Contractors planning to perform broad commercial building work should determine whether the General Building Contractor classification matches their intended scope.
2. **Establish the contracting business.** Applicants may operate through an appropriate legal business structure and must complete applicable state business registration requirements.
3. **Identify the examination qualifier.** The individual qualifying the business must satisfy the applicable examination requirements associated with the requested classification.
4. **Pass West Virginia Business and Law.** West Virginia requires contractors seeking licensure to pass the Business and Law examination in addition to applicable trade testing.
5. **Satisfy the trade examination requirement.** The NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors is accepted by the West Virginia Contractor Licensing Board for participating commercial general building qualification.
6. **Complete applicable business registrations.** The contractor business must obtain required state business and tax registrations and provide the information required for licensure.
7. **Address workers' compensation and unemployment requirements.** Businesses with employees must satisfy applicable West Virginia employment-related registration and coverage requirements.
8. **Submit the contractor license application.** The business provides the required application, examination, classification, tax, employment, and business information to the Contractor Licensing Board.
9. **Receive license approval before contracting.** The applicable West Virginia contractor license must be issued before performing work for which state law requires licensure.

## State Requirements

The West Virginia Contractor Licensing Board regulates contractor licensing, while the Division of Labor provides administrative support and enforcement. Contractors must obtain the appropriate classification before engaging in work that requires a state contractor license.

The West Virginia **General Building Contractor** classification covers work involving structures used for support, shelter, and enclosure where construction involves more than two contractor classifications, as well as supervision of the whole or a portion of qualifying building construction. West Virginia describes a General Building Contractor as a commercial, business, or industrial contractor, and the classification applies to both commercial and private properties.

The General Building classification does not automatically authorize every regulated specialty. A General Building Contractor who intends to perform electrical, plumbing, heating, ventilating and cooling, piping, or general engineering contracting work must obtain the appropriate additional classification where required.

Every contractor applicant must satisfy the Business and Law examination requirement in addition to applicable trade testing. The NASCLA commercial examination can provide the accepted trade qualification for participating commercial general building licensing, but the contractor must still complete West Virginia's state-specific requirements.

Businesses operating with employees may have obligations involving workers' compensation, unemployment registration, tax accounts, employment eligibility verification, and other employer responsibilities. These requirements should be coordinated with the business formation and contractor licensing process.
