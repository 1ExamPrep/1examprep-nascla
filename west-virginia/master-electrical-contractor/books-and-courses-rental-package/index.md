## State Requirements

West Virginia contractor licensing is administered by the Contractors Licensing Board within the West Virginia Division of Labor. The Board regulates contractor licensing and establishes the examination and application requirements associated with contractor classifications.

West Virginia participates in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and accepts the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination. NASCLA examination results are not themselves a contractor license. After satisfying the examination requirement, applicants must still complete the West Virginia licensing process.

The West Virginia Division of Labor requires contractors to pass the Business and Law examination in addition to applicable trade examinations. The Business and Law test covers business-management and contractor-law subjects such as business organization, contracts, estimating and bidding, financial management, labor law, licensing, lien law, project management, risk management, safety, and tax laws.

West Virginia's contractor Business and Law examination is separate from the NASCLA electrical trade examination. Candidates preparing for both components should divide their study plan between technical electrical preparation and West Virginia-specific contractor law and business requirements.

Individual electrician licensing is also separate from contractor licensing. West Virginia individual Master Electrician and other electrician credentials are administered through the West Virginia Office of the State Fire Marshal rather than the Contractors Licensing Board. Candidates should distinguish between the individual electrician credential and the contractor business license required for the work they intend to perform.

The NASCLA examination can satisfy an accepted trade-examination component, but applicants remain responsible for completing the West Virginia application, Business and Law examination, and all other licensing requirements associated with their contractor classification.
