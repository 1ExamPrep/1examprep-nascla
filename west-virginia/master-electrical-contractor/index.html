West Virginia Master Electrical questions

Are all rental books highlighted and tabbed?

Yes. All rental books included in the package are highlighted and tabbed for organized study and reference navigation.

Does West Virginia accept the NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician exam?

Yes. The West Virginia Contractors Licensing Board, Division of Labor, is a participating agency in the NASCLA Accredited Electrical Examination Program and accepts the Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination.

Does West Virginia require Business and Law?

Yes. West Virginia requires contractors to satisfy the applicable Business and Law examination requirement in addition to the trade examination when required.

Is passing the NASCLA exam the same as receiving a West Virginia contractor license?

No. A passing NASCLA result can satisfy an accepted trade-examination requirement, but applicants must still complete the West Virginia contractor licensing process and receive approval from the appropriate licensing authority.

What should I study for the NASCLA electrical examination?

Preparation should include the National Electrical Code, electrical theory and calculations, wiring methods, grounding and bonding, circuit protection, electrical equipment, safety, project management, special systems, energy standards, and effective reference navigation.
